## Why these are the same finite predicates

The frozen `Verifier.run` first verifies the embedding/cardinals, node records, root witnesses, complete leaf partition and every leaf/mask. Its remaining mathematical operation is `self.boundaries(edges,corners)`, followed by summary checks. The old full-interior executions discharge the first block. Exact typed equality transfers those already checked finite inputs to the fresh proposal: the routine's only excluded fields are not read anywhere by the original run. This is equality of all actual proof objects, not trust in a count or a successful producer flag.

Inspection of the original boundary-method AST shows its only `self` dependencies are `cardinals`, `exact_pattern` and `edge_flat_values`. The constructor independently reconstructs the same cardinals/physical field. `exact_pattern` is the deterministic signed sum of those cardinals and their exact derivatives. `edge_flat_values` starts empty and is only appended by the boundary method for later optional global-value work; interior leaf replay does not populate it. Thus a newly constructed Verifier runs exactly the same boundary predicates as the existing staged Verifier, without needing any interior counters or root state. No acceptance condition is added, weakened, rounded differently or skipped.

The summary block is pure accounting of the already verified facts. Checking it against the actual independently verified interior/boundary results is equivalent to its checks after a monolithic run. It has no authority to replace any missing mathematical replay. The resulting record explicitly describes a composition of actual original-method executions; its machine flags do not pretend that a complete monolithic run was executed.

Finally, the open square, four open edges and four corners are disjoint and exhaust the closed square. PD-T170's finite reduction therefore sums the actually verified strict-maxima counts. PD-T184's interior theorem and the complete boundary predicates suffice for PD-T180 by that same reduction. Reflection is only a source of ordinary proposed records, all identical to verified records or explicitly replayed. Global-value work is outside T1 and is not smuggled into this composition.
