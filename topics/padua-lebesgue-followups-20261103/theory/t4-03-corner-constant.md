## Statement

Let n≥32, N=n+1/2, a=2/π, c₁=a(γ+log(8/π)), b=2ac₁ and c=c₁²+κ, where κ is given by t4-01. Then c is independent of parity, and

\[
 \Lambda_c(n)={4\over\pi^2}\log^2N+b\log N+c+R_n,
\]

with the explicit bound

\[
 |R_n|\le604n^{-1/5}+{(\log n+2)^2+1\over n+1}
       +{A_n\over2n^2}+{1\over16n^4}
       +{\log N+2\over n}.                                  \tag{1}
\]

Here A_n=a log n+c₁. Every displayed error tends to zero.

The exact decimal enclosures, with strictly open endpoints, are

\[
 0.919710118388492535099518225790<\kappa
 <0.919710118388492535099518225791,
\]
\[
 1.846160310423926079759838737947<c
 <1.846160310423926079759838737948.                              \tag{2}
\]

## Proof or evidence

The quadratic error of t4-02 gives

\[
 |L_n^2-A_n^2|\le{A_n\over2n^2}+{1\over16n^4}.
\]

Add t4-01's error to obtain Λ_c=A_n²+κ+o(1) with the first three terms of (1). Since log(N/n)≤1/(2n), a<1 and A_n≤A_N≤log N+2,

\[
 |A_N^2-A_n^2|\le {\log N+2\over n}.
\]

Expand A_N²+κ to obtain the stated coefficients. All estimates hold on the full sequence, so both parity subsequences have the same c.

For (2), evaluate the coefficient series of t4-01 through K=64 using Arb at 320-bit precision, then add the symmetric error ball with exact radius 8/[3·66·4⁶⁵]. All coefficient operations, ζ evaluations, π, γ and logarithms use Arb balls.
