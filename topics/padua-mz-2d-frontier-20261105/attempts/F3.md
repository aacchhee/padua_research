## Exactness compression observation

Let `k=m-n` and decompose `p=p_lo+p_hi` in the continuous total-degree
Legendre basis, where `p_lo` has degree at most k. Since `k+n=m`,
degree-m exactness gives

`(Q_m-integral)(p_lo^2)=0` and
`(Q_m-integral)(p_lo*p_hi)=0`.

Hence `(Q_m-integral)(p^2)=(Q_m-integral)(p_hi^2)`.
Equivalently, in the continuous orthonormal basis the defect has zero
rows/columns for total degrees at most `m-n`. Near half-exactness its
active shell is narrow, but its dimension grows with m, and no norm
smallness follows merely from that count. This routine consequence of
exactness is not a separate substantive theorem or a fixed-ratio case.
