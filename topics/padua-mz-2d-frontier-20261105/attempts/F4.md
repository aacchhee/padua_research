## First analytic direction

Integrating PD-T101's cardinal formula expresses the signed weights as
positive Chebyshev checkerboard weights times the triangular Chebyshev
partial sum of the density `pi^2 sqrt(1-x^2)sqrt(1-y^2)`, with the
exceptional highest-x correction. Absolute summability of its O(k^-2)
one-dimensional coefficients gives a uniform O(m^-1) density remainder.

The potential separation from PD-N052 is to estimate that remainder in
the exact weighted L1 formula. A coarse Chebyshev-to-Lebesgue polynomial
L2 comparison loses only O(m) in two dimensions, exactly compensated by
the density tail. The positive main density is controlled by tensor CC
upper estimates and the elementary CC sine weight floor. This route does
not require F1/F2 or bounded absolute-Gram eigenvalues.
