## IDEA-391 — quartic diamond localization

Original provisional ID: `IDEA?mz2d-frontier-codex-f3-01`.

Aliases: squared-Fejér plateau; smooth diamond reconstruction; lifted
checkerboard packing; positive product-sine sampled norm.

- Question / scope: close the diagonal weighted-localization obligation
  left in IDEA-226/PD-T115 and obtain an explicit positive reference form
  uniformly equivalent to continuous Lebesgue L2 at each fixed ratio.
- Mechanism and hypotheses: with `L=ceil((m-n)/4)`, multiply a Dirichlet
  kernel by `(F_L/L)^2`, then use the same parity projection in diagonal
  angles as PD-T115. The spectral plateau survives, its support remains
  in the `(m-1)` diamond, and physical decay is quartic in each diagonal
  coordinate. Regularized product-sine weight ratios cost at most one
  power in each coordinate. A two-sheet lift of the checkerboard gives
  a uniform packing estimate for the resulting cubic envelopes.
- Closest entries (read in full): IDEA-074/PD-T023 and
  IDEA-226/PD-T115; IDEA-224/PD-N053 is the rejected rectangular geometry.
- Separation criterion: exact diamond reconstruction must coexist with
  summable *weighted* diagonal kernel bounds for the product-sine
  measure, with constants depending only on `1-n/m`. PD-T115 proved
  reconstruction only. A wrong plateau or an unsummable weighted
  envelope kills this proposed completion.
- Literature baseline and limits: Fejér/Jackson-type smoothing and Schur
  estimates are classical. This is a local explicit variant, not a
  historical new-mechanism claim; no new primary-source theorem is used.
- Applicable obstructions and hypotheses broken: the construction escapes
  PD-N053 by diamond support. It does not escape PD-N045/048/052 by
  itself: its positive reference weights are not the signed Padua weights.
  No nodal positive-floor conversion or negative-corner subtraction is
  proposed. The ratio estimate is checked in the actual diagonal geometry.
- Novelty gate verdict: **variant with difference**, within the documented
  local search: the difference is summable fourth-order diagonal decay and
  an explicit weighted packing proof, rather than the same exactness
  identity in renamed notation.
- Cheapest decisive test: derive the scalar Fourier plateau/support and
  bound the weight ratio near a corner along a diagonal, before spectra.
- Kill criterion written before testing: stop if `n+4(L-1)>m-1`, the
  plateau is not exactly one, or the weighted diagonal envelope has a
  nonsummable exponent after the actual product-sine ratio is inserted.
- Test discriminability: these finite Fourier and geometric identities
  decide the proposed analytic norm proof; finite spectra would not.
- Actual test: `4(L-1)<=m-n-1`; convolution of the Dirichlet indicator
  with the nonnegative coefficients of `F_L^2` gives plateau one through
  n and support through `n+4(L-1)`. Quartic scalar decay loses at most
  one power in each diagonal variable, leaving cubic summable envelopes.
  Full constants and the two-sided positive reference inequality are in
  [the theory record](theory/f3-smooth-diamond-reference.md).
- Disposition: **retained**, analytic supporting result PROVED in
  development, independent/Claude audit pending. The signed fixed-ratio
  target remains OPEN; no theta above one half is claimed solved.
