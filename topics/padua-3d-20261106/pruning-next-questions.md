## Background calculation and conditional delivery

For context, ordinary A-optimal deletion has a loose familiar trace bound.
With m>N current rows, S=(A^T A)^-1 and h_i=a_i^T S a_i, let d_i=1-h_i.
The positive d_i sum to m-N. Averaging trace increments `||S a_i||²/d_i`
with probabilities `d_i/(m-N)` gives at most `tr(S)/(m-N)`, because
summing `||S a_i||²` over all rows gives tr(S). Rows h_i=1 have zero
probability and are omitted. Exact greedy A deletion therefore increases
trace by a factor at most `(m-N+1)/(m-N)`; iteration to k>=N gives
`(M-N+1)/(k-N+1)`. This is routine background algebra from the recorded
identities, no new theorem or stability claim. Growth near N supplies no
small-noise guarantee. It is an **upper** bound, not an infeasibility lower
bound, and concerns unit-variance OLS, not integer repeats, Haar maps or
amplification. It cannot prove the 10%/10% goal impossible.

Defer usable-artifact delivery until a quality survivor exists. A subset
can be represented by integer orders, canonical retained node IDs, ordering/
basis/fit recipe and generator/checker. Around 1100 IDs at n16 should use
only a few KiB; verify actual serialization size. Canonical angles specify
exact distinct cosine geometry without 969 binary64 coordinate triples.
Anisotropic n16 has denominator `L=lcm(32,34,34)=544`. The existing
[root-of-unity reduction](theory/t8-r06-certification.md) applies: use
unscaled product-Chebyshev modes or monomials, verify an odd prime p=1 mod L
and exact-order L root, and certify a nonzero N-minor. Avoid assuming
square-root normalizations lie in the cyclotomic ring. At k>N the minor
certifies determining rank, not a unique interpolant.

This known route underlies U3 witnesses; it is not a fourth mechanism or
an all-degree proof. Exact-cosine rank and rounded-node measured quality
are distinct. No certificate, replay, delivered subset or size test here.
