## LIT?padua-3d-20261106-codex-T10-08 — Kolomoitsev–Lomako, polyhedra

[*On the growth of Lebesgue constants for convex polyhedra*, arXiv1801.00608v1,2018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.00608). Coverage: abstract/introduction pp1–3, Theorem4.1 and its proof pp18–19, Corollary4.1 p20, reference list; supporting §3 lemmas not fully read. Theorem4.1 bounds the normalized L1 Dirichlet kernel of a bounded convex polyhedron contained in[0,n1]×...×[0,nd] by C(s,d) product_j log(nj+1), with s its triangulation size. An integer translate of the radius-n octahedron satisfies these hypotheses with nj=2n and fixed s. Thus the exact sharp premise missing from the elementary T10 proof is a published theorem statement. Its full supporting proof is not locally verified; source dependence is explicit in the conditional corollary. No theorem about minimal interpolation is attributed to this source.
