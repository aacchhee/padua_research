## 1. n2 all-real-phase classification (computer-assisted PROVED) — CONFIRMED

**My reading of the claim.** Let H ≤ T³ be finite, let a ∈ R³ and X = c(a+H). If |X| = 10 and Π₂ evaluation is invertible, then (H,a) is equivalent, under a→a+h (h∈H) and a_j→a_j+½, to (H,0) for one of 36 labelled H: rows (1,r,s),(0,4,0),(0,0,4) with r,s∈{1,3}, or one of 32 order-19 groups. No free-coordinate stratum is unisolvent.

**Reduction, reconstructed by hand. Every step is valid.**
- Fold bound: c is at most 8-to-1, so |H| ≤ 80. Also |H| ≥ |X| = 10.
- Projection caps 2 < m_j ≤ 20:
  - m_j ≤ 2 gives at most 2 cosine values, so a univariate quadratic vanishes on X.
  - m_j > 20 gives at least ⌈m_j/2⌉ > 10 values.
- HNF completeness: standard. I also checked it numerically (below).
- Strata (T7-03): the collision relation is fixed on S_j = (1/2m_j)Z and on its complement. So node count, axis counts and fixed-node counts are constant on each stratum.
- Quotient: a_j+½ is a sign flip, and a+h gives the same coset. On free coordinates a+h stays inside the free stratum. The HNF box is a valid set of coset representatives.
- Fixed-trace kill: h ∈ H[2] induces σ_e on X. The trace on R^X counts fixed nodes. The trace on Π_n is Σ(−1)^{e·α}. An invertible equivariant evaluation preserves the trace. On a free coordinate a zero cosine cannot occur.
- Short relations: support ≤ 2, |u_j| ≤ 2, u ∈ H^⊥, 2u·a ∈ Z on special coordinates only. This gives U_j − sU_k = 0, a nonzero polynomial of degree ≤ 2, and the kill holds on the whole stratum. Support-3 relations have degree ≥ 3, so omitting them is correct.
- Prime-order reduction: any collision forces 2a ∈ H and the involution s ↦ t+t'−s, which gives (p+1)/2 nodes. So only p = 19 is possible, and a−h ∈ {0,½}³ makes X a sign image of c(H).
- Order 10 with no fold: the half-period permutation is fixed-point-free (trace 0), but the Π₂ traces are 4, 2, 4.
- Actual-node lifting: Z[½,ζ_D] → F_p with p ≡ 1 mod D. A nonzero minor mod p is nonzero in characteristic 0.
- Symbolic vs specialization: no target stratum needed a symbolic decision. Every deficient decision is a stratum-uniform trace or relation kill. All 4 nonprime positives are all-special (zero-dimensional). Prime ranks are exact rational ranks.
