## Findings

### MAJOR

**M1. Li–Sun–Xu's question allows nodes anywhere in R^d, but the text implies Theorem A settles its whole positive-weight case.** Locations: paper-note-draft.md:49 and final-d1/gate.md:42.

- Evidence: LSX p1 defines nodes as "xk ∈ Rd" (execution 5).
- The paper says Theorem A answers the question "for positive weights" and "Their definition allows real nonzero weights [p1]; the signed-weight question … remain[s] open". A reader would take the positive-weight half as settled.
- It is not settled. Positive rules with nodes outside C are outside Theorem A (spectral-obstruction:54; D1a F1 example).
- gate.md:42 has the same omission. sources.md:46 already states it correctly ("only for positive weights with nodes in the closed cube").
- D1b-2 and audit-05's suggested sentence also checked only the weights, so this point is new.
- D1's truth and status are unaffected.
- Why MAJOR: the change alters the stated scope of an attribution claim and adds a newly read source fact. That is material (WORKFLOW:45), and a doubtful classification is returned as MAJOR (WORKFLOW:180). This follows the H1 precedent.
- **Suggested text, paper:49.** Replace the two sentences "It answers negatively, for positive weights and with non-effective constants, … in the cube. Their definition allows real nonzero weights [p1]; the signed-weight question and their n³/4+O(n²) conjecture remain open." with:

  > It answers negatively, for positive weights with all nodes in C and with non-effective constants, the question of Li–Sun–Xu [arXiv:0808.0180v2, pp3,16] whether degree-(2n−1) product-Chebyshev rules with n³/6+O(n²) nodes exist on the cube. Their definition allows nodes anywhere in R³ and real nonzero weights [p1]; the signed-weight and exterior-node cases and their n³/4+O(n²) conjecture remain open.

- **Suggested text, gate.md:42.** Replace "only for positive weights and non-effectively; p1 allows real nonzero weights, so the signed-weight question remains OPEN" with "only for positive weights with all nodes in the closed cube and non-effectively; p1 allows nodes in R^d and real nonzero weights, so the signed-weight and exterior-node cases remain OPEN".
