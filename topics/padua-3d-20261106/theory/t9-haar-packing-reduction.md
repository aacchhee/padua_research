# PD-T?padua-3d-codex-T9-03 — exact Haar cubature to lattice packing

ID kind: PD-T. Aliases: T9-HAAR; dual-lattice packing sketch.
Split from: none. Split into: none. Supports: T9-PACK conditional corollary.

## Statement / definitions

Let H be a finite subgroup of T^d, L={k∈Z^d:e^{ik·h}=1 for all h∈H},
and let Q_a be normalized Haar measure on a+H pushed forward by cos in
each coordinate. It has positive masses at the distinct folded nodes X_a.
Write ρ(x)=∏[π sqrt(1−x_j²)]^{-1} for the product arcsine probability
measure. For unshifted a=0, Q_0 is exact for Π_D^d iff

L∩{0<|k|₁≤D}=∅.

In d3, exactness through2n−1 implies a lattice packing of open l1 balls
of radius n, and |X_0|≥|H|/8=det(L)/8. If every lattice packing of the
unit3D l1 ball has density≤δ, then |X_0|≥n³/(6δ).
The equivalence fails in general for shifted cosets.

## Proof

The character average |H|^{-1}Σ_h e^{ik·h} is1 on L and0 otherwise:
if nontrivial choose h₀ with e^{ik·h₀}≠1 and translate the sum.
For α∈N₀^d the Chebyshev-product moment is therefore

Q_a(∏T_{α_j})=2^{-d}Σ_{ε∈{±1}^d}
                  e^{i(εα)·a}1_{εα∈L}.

The formula includes repeated terms when α_j=0 and so remains valid
without any support-size adjustment. The continuous arcsine moment is0
unless α=0. At a=0 all summands are nonnegative: exactness for α≠0
is equivalent to the absence of every signed frequency εα in L.
Chebyshev products with |α|≤D form a basis of Π_D, proving equivalence.

For completeness det(L)=[Z^d:L]=|H|. Choose q with H contained in
the q-grid (Z/qZ)^d. In that finite group, orthogonality of characters
gives |H||H^⊥|=q^d: sum over all q^d characters of their sums on H
equals q^d, since only h=0 contributes after reversing summation.
Every character trivial on H has sum|H|; thus the formula follows.
Reduction modulo q gives L/qZ^d=H^⊥, whence the index and covolume.

For D=2n−1, the integral lattice contains no nonzero vector shorter
than2n: strict exclusion through2n−1 suffices because its l1 norms
are integers. Translates by L of the open l1 ball of radius n have
disjoint interiors by the triangle inequality. Its volume in d3 is
2³n³/3!=4n³/3. Density is (4n³/3)/det(L), yielding the asserted
determinant bound if δ is available. Each cosine fibre contains at most
2^d torus points, so |X_0|≥|H|/8, including boundary stabilizers.

Phase necessity fails already for H={0}, a=(π/2,…,π/2): Q_a is
evaluation at the origin, exact throughdegree1, whereas L=Z^d contains
unit vectors. The terms e^{±ia_j} cancel. Thus the packing equivalence
does not exclude arbitrary shifted cosets or arbitrary positive weights.

## Two-dimensional consistency check

For the standard Padua checkerboard take cyclic H generated by
(π/n,π/(n+1)), of order2n(n+1). Its dual satisfies
(n+1)u+nv≡0 mod2n(n+1). It contains(2n,0). If |u|+|v|≤2n−1,
the linear expression has absolute value<2n(n+1), so is0. Coprimality
then forces(u,v)=(nt,−(n+1)t), whose nonzero l1 norm≥2n+1.
Thus its shortest dual norm is2n and Haar cubature is exact2n−1.
The diamond-packing density is2n²/[2n(n+1)]=n/(n+1)→1.
Cosine folding is exactly the even checkerboard of orders n,n+1:
angle residues i mod2n and j mod2(n+1) arise from a common cyclic
parameter iff i≡j mod2, by generalized CRT. Reflection preserves parity;
counting 0≤i≤n,0≤j≤n+1 with i+j even gives(n+1)(n+2)/2.
Hence the reduction is consistent with 2D Padua, where limiting density1
does not produce the 3D strict excess factor.
