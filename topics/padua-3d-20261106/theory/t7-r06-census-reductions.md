## Proof of1–2, including free coordinates

Adding1/2 to a_j changes x_j's sign and hence preserves rank and count;
it changes k_j by m_j. Adding any h∈H to the entire phase leaves the
torus coset a+H unchanged. On special coordinates this gives the index
translation in2. On a free coordinate, adding h_j∈(1/m_j)Z preserves
the complement of(1/(2m_j))Z. It is a bijection of this free-coordinate
domain and, in symbolic coordinates z_j, multiplies z_j by a nonzero
root of unity. Thus it preserves symbolic rank even when different
literal samples would have different ranks. The quotient acts on
strata, not on a finite arbitrary list of phases. These operations do
not alter H or discard any mixed special/free mask.

The quotient can be enumerated by integer lattice arithmetic: take

the quotient size; the box0<=k_j<B_jj is a complete set of coset
representatives. Reduction from the last coordinate upward proves
existence/uniqueness, just as in the triangular subgroup reduction.

## Proof of3

The physical sign change x_j->(-1)^(e_j)x_j is induced by translation
by h on the coset, so it permutes distinct cosine nodes even with
folding. Its trace on all functions on X is its number of fixed
nodes. On Pi_n its trace is the displayed signed monomial count.
Invertible evaluation intertwines the two actions and preserves trace.

To check constancy, an active coordinate e_j=1 has even m_j. A zero
cosine requires a_j=1/4-h'_j mod1 or3/4-h'_j mod1 for some h'∈H,
which lies in(1/(2m_j))Z because m_j is even. Therefore it is impossible
on a free coordinate and has fixed truth value on a special one.
The full node-equivalence relation is constant by T7-03, so counting
the fixed nodes also is constant. This establishes the exact pruning
logic without assuming no folding or constant rank within a stratum.
