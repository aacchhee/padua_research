## Known baseline and limits

The existing anisotropic parity family has orders `(n,n+1,n+1)`, positive
folded Haar weights and orthonormal Chebyshev Gram `I+ee^T`, where `e`
indexes `(n,0,0)`. It already yields an explicit rank-one moment solver,
a local log-four bound and a source-dependent log-three bound. Equal
orders `(n+1,n+1,n+1)` have Gram `I`. These are known mechanisms. Existing
finite operator norms do not establish actual function approximation or
a sampling-budget-matched noise advantage.

selection. The QR/Leja nodes follow the exact source chain stated above;
the QR selection basis is unscaled, in inherited total-degree order.
All coefficient maps and diagnostics use the same continuous orthonormal
product-Chebyshev basis in that order.

Parity nodes are indexed lexicographically by triples
`0<=i_j<=m_j` with all three indices of the same parity. Coordinates are
`cos(pi i_j/m_j)`. Integer multiplicity is the product of factors1 at an
axis endpoint and2 elsewhere; weight is multiplicity divided by
`2 m_1 m_2 m_3`. Save every integer index, multiplicity and operator/node
identity, not only a digest. The anisotropic coefficient is
`c_α=sum_i w_i φ_α(x_i)y_i`, then halve only `c_(n,0,0)`; equal orders
need no correction. This is the known diagonal Gram solver.

Four functions, frozen in this order:

1. `exp((x+y+z)/3)`;
2. `1/(1+16(x²+y²+z²))`;
3. `abs(x+0.3y−0.2z)`;
4. `cos(4x−3y+2z)`.

4096 scrambled Sobol points with seed20261107, transformed coordinatewise

tune parity/QR/Leja nodes. Report maximum error over the union and RMS
over Sobol only. The maximum is a finite lower sample of supremum error;
RMS is empirical, not an exact integral. Retain every function/case.
