## Answers to the key questions
- **Exact |X|=dim for octahedral frequencies.** Trig: any lattice rule of any rank with exactly |I| points reconstructing
  the ℓ1-ball of radius n in Z³ is a lattice tiling by Lee spheres (Thm 10), impossible for n≥2 (GMP 1998); n=1 exists
  (z=(1,2,3) mod 7). Cosine: no exact-N lattice result in the literature; Plan B/C bounds 2N−1/2N−2 are the repo's classes.
- **Uniform-norm oversampling.** General spaces: any factor b>1 gives O_b(√N) (source 1); m=N gives only O(N)
  (Fekete/Auerbach) or O(N^{3/2}) constructively (source 2). No source gives polylog at O(N); polylog needs exact
  positive cubature (hyperinterpolation). So the repo's 1.5N parity grid is, to my search, the best proved polylog
  oversampling in the 3-cube. 1+ε is possible only for √N-type bounds.
- **Exactly N with polylog by subsampling:** no provable scheme found. Weights cancel in square interpolation.

## Candidate ideas
**I1. Riesz/complete-interpolating-sequence (CIS) route to proved O(√N), with mesh-free certificates.**
Mechanism (elementary, my derivation, unaudited): ℓ(x)=V^{-T}p(x) gives ℓᵀDℓ = pᵀ(VᵀD^{-1}V)^{-1}p ≤ K(x,x)/λ_min(Ĝ).
Cauchy–Schwarz then gives Λ ≤ √(K∞·Σ_j 1/K(x_j,x_j)/λ_min(Ĝ)), with K∞=Σ_k 2^{|k|₀} exact. With free weights w,
Λ ≤ √(K∞/λ*), λ* = max_{w∈Δ} λ_min(VᵀWV), which is an E-optimal SDP on the node set. A family with uniform
λ_min≥c (a CIS, which is what Padua is in 2-D) has Λ=O(n^{3/2}). That is the brief's "second-best" target and beats
Fekete's N and the O(N³) R-Leja bound.
Why it might escape: it swaps the nonsmooth sup over the cube for a smooth finite eigenvalue objective, and gives the
repo its first rigorous whole-cube Λ upper bounds; current values are mesh lower samples.
Closest: IDEA-027 (dominant-submatrix N bound), LIT-387/T10 (Gram I+rank-1, I+eeᵀ), LIT-489 (MZ⇒Riesz map), U1/U2
direct norm optimization; grep found no Riesz/CIS/E-optimal entry.
Separation: certificate and objective are new to the topic; a falsifier is an existing entry that bounds Λ via λ_min.
Cheapest decisive test: λ_min, λ* and the certificate for the repo's U2/QR sets at n=4..16 (seconds); Riesz optimizer
at n=6..10. Kill if λ_min of the best sets decays like n^{-a} under two optimizers.
Promise 3, difficulty 2 (numerics) / 4 (uniform proof).
Critique: only √N, not polylog. The λ_min trend 0.96→0.43 (n=2..7) looks like slow decay. Riesz-optimal ≠ Λ-optimal. A uniform proof
probably needs the Padua-type structure the repo has excluded in lattice classes. CIS existence in 3-D is open
(BOC/AMO give only density conditions).

**I2. Lee-code dictionary for the lattice programme (hygiene + prior art).**
Mechanism: (a) record GMP 1998 + Thm 10 as the trig exact-|I| nonexistence; (b) record T9 as the cosine analogue of
Golomb–Welch's 1968 packing-density proof; (c) replace the Betke–Henk start ⌈38n³/27⌉ by the elementary |DS(3,n−1)|
in the R08 enumerator. At n=4..7 that adds only ~3/6/12/21 determinants and makes those minima self-contained.
Closest: T9 packing, R08-minima, T10 census. Separation: none mathematically for (a)(b); (c) removes a LITERATURE
dependency. Test: rerun the enumerator from q=88/170/292/462. Promise 2, difficulty 1.
Critique: the real obstructions live in sign folding (73/18), which coding theory does not address.

**I3. Dominant (exact D-optimal) designs: (N+m)-point LS with Λ ≤ √((N+m)·max(1, N/(m+1))).**
Mechanism: Fedorov's swap identity det ratio = (1+d(x))(1−d_j)+d(x,x_j)² ≤ 1 at a max-volume K-set. Summing over j
gives sup d(x) ≤ N/(K−N+1); this is Thm 4.2 of source 4. On compact D it holds for every n-dim V ⊂ C(D): 2n−1
unweighted samples give ‖P‖ ≤ √(2n−1), against 60√n with 2n samples in source 1, and n+n^α give ≲ n^{1−α/2}.
For the cube, (1+ε)N nodes give Λ ≤ √((1+ε)/ε)·√N. Constructive by exchange on an admissible mesh, times the mesh constant.
Closest: IDEA-027, LIT-385, source 1's open problem. Separation: novel only if absent from Krieg–Ullrich (Acta Numerica)
and the optimal-design literature; likely classical (G-efficiency of exact D-optimal designs).
Test: literature check, then exchange runs at K=N+m. Promise 2 (target) / 3 (side note), difficulty 1.
Critique: √N only; useless at m=0.

**I4 (deferred). BSS/MSS or Carathéodory–Tchakaloff thinning of the 1.5N parity grid to (1+ε)N while keeping polylog.**
My bound: a spectral δ-sparsifier perturbs Λ by up to δ√(8N), so polylog needs δ ≲ polylog/√N, i.e. ~N² samples.
CT only guarantees support ≤ #moments ≈ 8N > 1.5N. If the grid's degree-(2n−1) evaluation matrix has full column rank
(cheap check), the exact positive weights are unique, and no thinning preserves exactness. Resume only with an L1-MZ
(Lewis-weight) sparsifier that keeps degree-(2n−1) exactness and top-block Gram control.

**I5 (rejected by inspection). Weighted interpolation / weighted Fekete.** Weights cancel in square systems. The weighted
bound Λ(x) ≤ Σ w(x_i)/w(x) is ≳ N. Christoffel-weighted AFP measured worse than optimized sets (Λ 10.5/17.5/30.3 at
n=4..6) [M]. Kept only as the I1 initializer.

**I6 (rejected as duplicate). Rank-1 Chebyshev Plan B/C at n=2N−1, 2N−2.** These are the repo's killed or explored
cyclic classes; Plan A would need ≈4–6N nodes.
