## B. Side measurements (MEASURED; scripts in scratchpad `code/`; not repository evidence)

- **M1.** Take k cosets, with non-folding phases, of one sign-invariant H (R_jH=H; annihilator types P, C, I, F) with k|H|=N. T7-01's B matrix is then block-diagonal over sign-orbits of classes, and injectivity needs #(orbit∩I_n) = k·#classes in every orbit. **No lattice passes for n=2..16, k∈{1,2,3,4,8}** (`balance.py`). For n≤12 and k∈{1,2,4}, every lattice already fails at an orbit of size 1, i.e. a class fixed by all signs (`balance_fixed.py`). The 2-D analogue also fails for n≤14 (`balance2d.py`), so this is a discrete-boundary effect, not tiling.
- **M2.** An unstructured Levenberg–Marquardt search for n=3 Padua-type sets was run (20 nodes, w=s²>0, exact on Π₅; `pt2.py`). 7 of 12 starts reached residual ~1e−30 with min w·N 0.11–0.45. All are Π₃-unisolvent (cond V 4e1–6e2), but the sampled Λ is **38–609**, against 4.05 optimal (S5). n=4 and 5: the runs that finished did not converge (residual 1e−4 to 3e−2), so they are inconclusive. Those runs were stopped.

## C. Candidate ideas (adversarial)

**I1. Sign-fixed-class kill theorem.** Prove M1 for all n.
- Mechanism: an orbit of size 1 (a class c with 2c∈L) needs exactly k representatives in I_n. Combining the up to 8 such classes with the index D=N/k should give a contradiction. S10 serves as an asymptotic fallback for bounded-block limits.
- Not an escape: it extends T1 (reflection-stable cosets) to sign-invariant H with arbitrary non-folding phases, and to same-H unions. Any viable lattice design must then use non-sign-invariant H (giant aliasing components) or folding.
- Closest entries: PD-N T1-01, PD-T T1-02, T7-01, t9-r06-phase-weight-boundaries.
- Kill criterion: some (a, glue, k, n) with all sign-fixed classes balanced. Cheapest test: already measured up to n=12–16; next, write the inequality proof.
- Promise 3, difficulty 2. It will likely succeed but only narrows the field.

**I2. Unstructured Padua-type sets plus Λ optimization on their manifold.**
- Mechanism: exactness through degree 2n−1 gives Gram = diag(I, G_top), so ℓ_j = w_j[K_{n−1}(·,x_j) + T_n(·)ᵀG_top⁻¹T_n(x_j)]. This holds for any set, lattice or not. The free dimension 4N − dim Π_{2n−1} is 24, 20, 4 at n=3, 4, 5.
- Escape: every Padua-type exclusion so far is lattice or Haar based (r08-complement, T7-07, T5-03).
- Closest entries: IDEA-029, IDEA-120.
- Criterion: a certified (Krawczyk) Padua-type set at n=3, settling the brief's open "some n≥3" item, which M2 suggests is yes. Then compare min Λ on the manifold, minimizing ‖G_top−I‖ or Λ, with S5 at ≤1.25× and ≥2×.
- Test: under an hour.
- Promise 2, difficulty 1. **It likely dies for quality.** M2 shows the structure alone does not localize, and 4N < dim Π_{2n−1} ≈ 8N rules out generic sets for n≥6. That is heuristic, since 2-D Padua beats its own count of 3N vs 4N.

**I3. Near-cubature defect of the best known sets.**
- Mechanism: for the U2 and QR sets at n=8, 12, 16, find the largest m such that some w>0 is exact on Π_{2m−1} (an LP), and record the conditioning of the shell Gram for degrees m..n. S3's count allows m ≈ 0.87n with N nodes.
- Escape: it identifies the structure good sets actually have before more constructions are designed.
- Closest entries: none found.
- Criterion: a stable c=m/n ≥ 0.6 supports a "degree-2cn cubature on N nodes" target. c→0 kills cubature-based heuristics.
- Test: minutes. Promise 2, difficulty 1.

**I4. Aliasing-component size versus Λ for existing cyclic witnesses** (n≢2 mod 4, n≤12).
- Mechanism: compute the components of the graph I_n ↔ Z³/L (α→[εα]) and the magnitudes of B⁻¹. Conjecture: unimodular chain inverses do not decay, so Λ grows with component diameter. Together with I1 and T1 this would give a dichotomy against single-lattice designs.
- Closest entries: t2/t3 dictionaries, T8 phase records.
- Criterion: rank correlation of log Λ with log component size ≥ 0.8. A large-component witness with Λ ≤ 3log³n kills it.
- Test: about an hour. Promise 2, difficulty 2.

**I5. Structure factor of optimized sets.**
- Mechanism: compute |Σ_j e^{ik·θ_j}|² for θ=arccos x (reflection-unfolded) on the n=8, 12, 16 optimized sets, k≤3n. Bragg peaks would expose a hidden lattice or phase family that no killed class matched.
- Closest entries: none found.
- Criterion: peaks above 10× the Poisson level, stable after rescaling by n.
- Test: minutes. Promise 2, difficulty 1. Most likely amorphous.

**I6. Certified Λ intervals (S4 tool).**
- Mechanism: replace the brief's uncertified mesh lower samples with S4 intervals on (3n)³ Chebyshev meshes (≈7.7%, 110k points at n=16).
- No escape, but it gives an evidence upgrade for every Λ in the brief.
- Closest entries: t4-mesh-observations, u2-sampled-norm.
- Test: minutes. Promise 1, difficulty 1.

**Order.** First read S2 and S3 against the T10 log³ claims, since there may be prior art or a discharge. Then I1, then I2 at n=3, then I3/I5 as diagnostics.
