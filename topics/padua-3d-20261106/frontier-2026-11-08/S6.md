## 2. Key literature findings (missing or secondary-only in the repo)
- **F1 (LIT Li–Xu 2010).** On the A_d domain Δ*_d (Steiner/hypocycloid type) there is a node set Y_n with |Y_n| = binom(n+d, d) = dim Π_n^d, boundary included. It has all of the following:
  - it is the variety of the two-term quasi-orthogonal ideal ⟨T_α − T_{α*} : |α| = n+1⟩, orthogonal to Π_{n−2} (Thm 5.11);
  - it carries a degree-(2n−1) cubature of Gauss–Lobatto type for w^{−1/2} (Thm 5.10);
  - Π_n interpolation is unique, and that interpolant *equals* the trigonometric L*_n of Thm 4.7 (closing remark), so ‖L*_n‖ ≤ c(log n)^d (Thm 4.8; transfer by a homeomorphic change of variables);
  - the interior points give Gaussian cubature for w^{1/2} (Thm 5.8).

  This is a complete d-dimensional Padua analog: AC's "deeper structure" is the root-system DFA. It lands on Δ*_d, not on the cube. The repo has no record of this paper (grep found nothing).
- **F2 (LIT LSX 2009, Cor 4.12, Thm 4.9).** Interpolation on Γ_n, with n³/4 + O(n²) nodes, into Π*_n = span{s^k : k_i + k_j ≤ n} has ‖L_n‖ ≤ c(log n)³. Π*_n ⊃ Π_n reaches degree 3n/2. The proof reduces to products of 1-D Dirichlet sums. The brief says "log³ … by least squares, NOT interpolation"; that should read "not interpolation *onto Π_n*". The repo's LSX record is secondary only. Dencker–Erb Lissajous–Chebyshev nodes in 3-D (∏(n_i+1)/4 nodes; spectral set γ_i/n_i + γ_j/n_j < 1) are the anisotropic version of the same object.
- **F3 (LIT BMT).** X_{a,a+1} is hyperbolic with respect to the origin (Thm 3.5), so it has a definite determinantal representation (Helton–Vinnikov, not re-read). Padua nodes are then plausibly corank-2 points of a special symmetric pencil (DERIV, unverified).
- **F4 (LIT ORSV Thm 1.1, Degtyarev–Itenberg).** Transversal quartic spectrahedra exist with all 10 nodes real *and on the spectrahedron*. Toeplitz (cosine-moment) symmetroids are non-transversal: they are singular along the curve (cos θ, cos 2θ, cos 3θ) (p. 22).

## 3. Structural observations (DERIV, to be proved or checked by a developer)
- **O1.** Reflection-invariant subgroups of T³ (equivalently their duals) are exactly the orthorhombic parity lattices P, C, I, F with arbitrary axis scalings. Reason: g − σ_i g ∈ G gives the sandwich ∏2a_iZ ⊂ L ⊂ ∏a_iZ.
  - Grid fraction: such a set covers a fraction s/2^d of the grid, while unisolvence needs ≈1/d!. For d=2 take s=2. For d=3 the requirement is s=4/3, impossible, and with every axis order ≥ n you need s ≤ 2^d/d! = 4/3. This is the arithmetic core of T1.
  - Packing: the best density for ℓ1 balls is P 1/6, C 1/3, I 1/3, F 2/3 (each a two-line check of the minimal ℓ1 vectors).
  - **Theorem candidate:** every unshifted lattice rule invariant under all reflections and exact to degree 2n−1 needs ≥ (n³/4)(1−o(1)) folded nodes. LSX's fcc construction is optimal in that class, which proves the LSX conjecture for this class. This sharpens T9's 19n³/108 (whose 18/19 lattice is not reflection-invariant) for invariant rules. Non-invariant cosets fold less; a stability argument is needed and is open.
- **O2.** Gorenstein is impossible (h-vector (1,3,6,…,binom(n+2,2))). Two codim-3 determinantal structures do fit exactly:
  - *Eagon–Northcott:* an (n+3)×(n+1) matrix of affine-linear forms in (x,y,z). Its maximal minors give binom(n+3,2) generators of degree n+1; Porteous gives degree binom(n+3,3) = N.
  - *Józefiak symmetric:* A(x) = A0 + ΣxᵢAᵢ with Aᵢ ∈ Sym_{n+2}. The corank-2 locus has binom(n+3,3) points; the generators are the binom(n+3,2) submaximal minors.

  Both have a linear resolution with Betti numbers (1, binom(n+3,2), (n+1)(n+3), binom(n+2,2)), the same as N general points. By generic perfection, *any* such locus that is finite, reduced and affine is Π_n-unisolvent. Józefiak's resolution and generic perfection are classical (Józefiak 1978, Kutz; not re-read). The n=1 case is the Cayley cubic / 3×3 elliptope 1−x²−y²−z²+2xyz, whose 4 nodes form the 3-D "n=1 Padua" tetrahedron. In 2-D the corresponding statement is Hilbert–Burch for (n+2)×(n+1) matrices. NEW? (no symmetroid–interpolation link found.)
- **O3.** Padua-type (N nodes, positive degree-(2n−1) cubature) is equivalent to commuting extensions with fixed Jacobi sections on Π_{n−1}. Write I+E for the discrete Gram matrix on P_n. Commutation of the top-left block forces A_{n−1,i}·E·A_{n−1,j}ᵀ to be symmetric in (i, j). Expected dimension at n=3: 80 − 56 = 24 for the cubature family. Intersected with Eagon–Northcott (codim 15) that leaves ≈9; the Józefiak family (codim 25) is over-determined.

## 4. Ranked routes (promise × testability)

**R1. Determinantal / symmetroid node sets (top).**
- Conjecture, n=3: some real (a) 5×5 symmetric pencil, corank-2 locus = 20 points in [−1,1]³, or (b) 6×4 Eagon–Northcott matrix, rank ≤3 locus = 20 points, has Λ within 1.2× of the repo's best n=3 value. n=4: 6×6 symmetric (35 points) or 7×5 Eagon–Northcott, against the repo's 5.54.
- Separation from killed routes: these are not cosets, grids, Lissajous curves, stacks or Leja sets, and use no Haar measure. Unisolvence is algebraic, and the family has ≈n² parameters against 3N ≈ n³/2.
- Test (<1 h):
  1. *Exact algebra.* Random integer Aᵢ with k=4,5,6; Gröbner basis of the (k−1)-minors over GF(32003) (Singular, Macaulay2 or msolve; sympy is fine for k=4,5). Check: quotient dimension = binom(k+1,3); the radical has the same dimension; every monomial of degree ≤ k−2 is standard.
  2. *Quality.* Nodes by homotopy continuation, or by Newton with deflation on det A = ∇det A = 0, checking the count. Start from ORSV's 10-real-node matrices (their §2) and from a perturbed elliptope or Toeplitz matrix. Normalize affinely into the cube and compute Λ on the 41³ mesh. Then minimize Λ over the matrices, keeping all nodes real.
  3. *Variant.* Compute 2-D Padua's Hilbert–Burch matrix symbolically at n=3,4 (syzygies of BDMVX's Q_k). If it is built from Chebyshev–Jacobi blocks, copy that block structure into a 3-D Eagon–Northcott matrix.
- Kill: real all-in-cube configurations unreachable, or best Λ > 2× the repo optimum at both n=3 and n=4.

**R2. Joint spectrum with low-rank Gram defect (IDEA-120; never run).**
- Conjecture, n=3: there is a 20-node positive degree-5 rule that is Π_3-unisolvent with rank E ≤ 3. n=4: 35 nodes, degree 7, rank E small.
- Separation from killed routes: arbitrary nodes and weights; the brief excludes this only for unshifted cosets.
- Test: box-constrained Levenberg–Marquardt on the 56 (n=3) or 120 (n=4) Chebyshev moment equations, 200 random starts. For each solution record Vandermonde rank, eig(E) and Λ. Then minimize ‖E‖_* or Λ subject to the moment equations.
- Kill: rank E ≥ ~r_n/2 everywhere and Λ never below the repo optimum. A pass would be a small positive result: Padua-type points exist at n=3.

**R3. Reflection-invariant lattice bound (negative and provable; cheap).**
- Statement: O1's n³/4 theorem.
- Test: hand-prove the P/C/I/F densities. Enumerate orthorhombic lattices with axis parameters ≤ 4n for n=3..12; compute the minimal folded |X| with dual ℓ1-min ≥ 2n; compare with the repo minima 16/35/54 (n=3–5) and with N.
- Outcome: if the repo minima beat the orthorhombic minima, non-invariant cosets matter at small n. Otherwise the coset world is settled at LSX's n³/4.

**R4. 3-D Morrow–Patterson (interior Gaussian, even degree), speculative.**
- Statement: a positive degree-4 rule with 10 nodes for ∏(1−xᵢ²)^{1/2}. Degree 2 already works with 4 nodes, (±½,±½,±½) with even sign parity (DERIV).
- Test: LM on 35 equations in 40 unknowns. If a rule exists, add 25 face points and test Π_4 unisolvence and Λ.
- Kill: no positive solution. Padua-plane stacks are already poor, so the prior is low.

**Mere restatements, which cannot produce new 3-D sets:** a2, b, c with two-term generators, e, g (Gorenstein/Cayley–Bacharach), i (finite field), and anything Lie-theoretic that keeps total degree on the cube (F1/f: only affine maps preserve total degree; the octahedron is not a tile). The only items that change the 3-D search space are R1 and R2. F1 and F2 should be registered as literature, and the brief's "not interpolation" should be corrected to "not interpolation onto Π_n".
