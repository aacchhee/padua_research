### MAJOR

**M1 — missed lattice-rule predecessor (Lyness 2003) and an incomplete gap list.**
- *What the source says.* At search-excerpt coverage, Lyness, *Notes on lattice rules* (J. Complexity 19 (2003) 321–331; ANL P1013), does three things:
  - it defines ρ=δ^s/(s!N), which is the packing factor of the dual lattice relative to the octahedron;
  - it states that optimal 3D lattice rules are known for δ≤4 and for every δ≡0 mod 6;
  - its appendix gives δ=6, N=38, z=(1,7,27) and its m-copies.
- *Overlap with the topic.* This is exactly det L≥38n³/27 attained at n=3m. It means the unrestricted minima at n=1,2,3,6,9,12,15 (2, 12, 38, 304, 1026, 2432, 4750) were reportedly already known to be optimal. Relative to the read sources, local certification adds only n4/5/7/8/10/11/13/14/16.
- *Where it is missing.* The source was flagged as an unread "octahedral lattice-packing comparator" in literature-3d.md:18 and literature-T1.md:57–63, together with Cools, *A Belgian View on Lattice Rules*. D5 never follows it up. novelty-gate:17/42–43/52 and paper:72 omit it, and the gap lists (paper:104, outcome:30, report:19 "four original fulltext gaps") do not include either source.
- *Fix.* Add both sources as named predecessors or gaps with coverage labels. Reword paper:72 to say that unrestricted optimality is reportedly known for δ≤4 and δ≡0 mod 6. This is new attribution content, so it goes back to the developer.
