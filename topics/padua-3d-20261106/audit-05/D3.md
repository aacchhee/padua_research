## (1) Independent reconstruction of the finite proof

My formulation differs from the producer's in basis, preconditioner, arithmetic and rank method:

- **Basis.** The 56 equations are written in the tensor Chebyshev basis: G_α = Σ_j w_j T_α1(x_j1)T_α2(x_j2)T_α3(x_j3) − δ_α0, for |α| ≤ 5. The T_α with |α| ≤ 5 are a basis of Π_5 related to the monomials by an invertible triangular change. So G = 0 if and only if F = 0, i.e. exactness on Π_5, including mass one.
- **Preconditioner.** R is my own: an mpmath inverse of the *exact* Fraction Jacobian J(c), at 250 digits, rounded to 2^-240. The producer uses Decimal at 85 digits, rounded to 2^-60.
- **Arithmetic.** Interval endpoints are exact Fractions, with no rounding at any step. T_k and T'_k = kU_{k−1} are enclosed by interval recurrences, which is valid by inclusion isotonicity. I verified that the box enclosures contain the center values. Matrix products are exact integers at scale 2^1318 (every quantity is dyadic). Failures raise `SystemExit`, not `assert`.

## (3) Nonlattice argument (theory.md:99–125)

Reconstructed and correct. a = c₀ = −8375088658315393/2^53 and b = c₃ = −1233507562271173/2^52 are *fixed* x-coordinates of nodes 0 and 1, so they are exact at the root.
- D = (1 − a²)(1 − b²) > 0. Its reduced denominator is 2^210, but its numerator is not a perfect square, so D is not a rational square.
- 4ab has denominator 2^103, so it is not an integer.
- I checked exactly that the norm (2ab)² − 4D equals 4(a² + b² − 1), so the minimal polynomial t² − 4ab·t + 4(a² + b² − 1) is right.
- A degree-2 algebraic integer has integral trace. So 2ab ± 2√D = 2cos(θ ∓ φ) is not an algebraic integer, where sin θ sin φ = √D > 0 on (0, π).
- If ψ ∈ Qπ, then 2cos ψ = ζ + ζ⁻¹ is an algebraic integer. The theory's monic C_q(t) − 2 relation needs q ≥ 1; taking 2q handles this.

Coset step:
- If X ⊂ cos 2π(s + H) with H finite, the two x-lifts differ by a torsion element, which is rational.
- 2π(s₁ + h₁) ≡ ε₁θ and 2π(s₁ + h₁′) ≡ ε₂φ, so θ − ε₁ε₂φ ∈ Qπ, a contradiction.
- This covers any phase, order and non-cyclic H. Unions of cosets are correctly left out of scope (theory.md:125, protocol.md:84).
