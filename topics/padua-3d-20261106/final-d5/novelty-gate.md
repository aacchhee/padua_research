## Closest newly read primary comparisons

**Cools–Lyness2001**, *Three- and four-dimensional K-optimal lattice rules of moderate trigonometric degree*, Math. Comp.70,1549–1567, DOI[10.1090/S0025-5718-01-01326-6](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-01-01326-6). Publisher/CiteSeer fetches failed; [Ronald Cools's uploaded fulltext](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220576946_Three-_and_four-dimensional_K-optimal_lattice_rules_of_moderate_trigonometric_degree) was actually read: §1 bounds; §2 eq2.2, Thms2.4–2.5, Def2.7; §4 Tables1/3 and distinctions among count functions; §5 limitations. Enhanced degree δ equals the shortest dual l1 norm. Setting δ=2n makes Table1's K-optimal counts agree exactly with the topic's determinant candidates for n1..15. Table3 supplies associated HNFs. The paper explicitly distinguishes K-optimality from globally optimal lattices. Thus the reduction, candidate values and constructions are predecessors; local unrestricted certification may strengthen their status. n16 is outside its δ≤30 table. No invention/first-discovery wording is warranted.

**Poppe–Cools**, [*In search for good Chebyshev lattices*](https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~josefdick/MCQMC_Proceedings/MCQMC_Proceedings_2010_Preprints/PoppeCools-final.pdf), author proceedings preprint,15pp: §1.2–1.3, §2.2, §3–§6 and tables/figure captions. It covers cosine images of shifted finite lattice rules, HNF enumeration, exact integer folding/deduplication and bounded searches. §5 says the main search is unshifted and shifts are added to known rules; §6 labels optimality computational evidence. These mechanisms precede the topic. The read text does not state a continuous all-phase ten-node classification or the universal reflection-invariant coset obstruction. The source's bounded degree/denominator/rank searches cannot establish their negations.

**Cools–Poppe2011**, *Chebyshev lattices, a unifying framework for cubature with Chebyshev weight function*, BIT51,275–288: metadata/author abstract only. The [publication page](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/225645725_Chebyshev_lattices_a_unifying_framework_for_cubature_with_Chebyshev_weight_function) explicitly has no fulltext. The KU Leuven report route redirected to SSO. This remains a material fulltext gap for priority; its abstract establishes the older framework, not absence of a particular theorem. No no-match verdict is assigned to unread contents. Repository [LIT-381](../../../literature/INDEX.md) records an earlier partial fulltext reading of §3.2, Table2 and §6 only; it does not reach the claims compared here.

**De Marchi–Vianello–Xu**, [arXiv0805.3529v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.3529v1),20pp: introduction and §2, especially Thm2.2 and proof pp5–6. Complementary even/odd tensor grids already give degree2n−1 positive product-Chebyshev cubature with about n^d/2^(d−1) nodes. Hence the 3D parity rule, its ~n³/4 scale, and hyperinterpolation route are inherited. It is not a minimal-cardinality Π_n interpolation construction. The read theorem does not cover arbitrary reflection-invariant cosets or give the ten-node phase census.

**Dencker–Erb**, [arXiv1511.04564v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.04564v2),30pp: intro; §2.1–2.3 node/orthogonality/interpolation definitions; eq2.19 and Thms2.7–2.8; Remark2.13; §3 lead. Their Lissajous–Chebyshev sets admit interpolation in a frequency-selected space with pairwise weighted degree restrictions, generally different from the ordinary total-degree simplex. Rank-one curve generation and discrete orthogonality are established predecessors. The read statements neither classify all finite torus cosets nor quantify all real phases for ten-node Π_2 interpolation.

**Carnicer–Sauer**, [arXiv1610.01850v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.01850),18pp: intro; §2; §3 Thm3.1 and its proof/cardinal formula pp4–6. Radon–Berzolari's line-extension induction is old; its plane-by-plane counterpart explains the stack's elementary unisolvence and block determinant mechanism. Those alone are not a contribution. No monotone 3D Padua-stack exponential-rate theorem was located in the read sections. The specific coefficient asymptotic needs scoped wording and has no established priority here.

**Kolountzakis**, [arXiv1606.02452](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.02452),14pp: spectral packing/tiling equivalences §1.1; weak convergence/density definitions; all of §2 Thm1 and Lemmas1–5/proofs pp5–9. For a nonnegative integrable normalized function, with f>1/2 on a positive-measure set, exact translate packings at densities tending to1 imply a tiling. This is a direct compactness/saturation precedent. D1's finite near-critical frames are not exact packings at each finite stage; its new obligation is the proved passage to a stationary orthogonal law at critical mean intensity. The broad compactness or near-tiling principle cannot be claimed new.

**Antezana–Marzo–Ortega-Cerdà**, [arXiv2101.08064v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.08064v1),17pp: exact Thm14 and proof pp15–16 reread. Finite exact orthogonal kernel bases on weighted balls lead, by scaled kernel asymptotics and weak limits, to forbidden orthogonal exponentials. This is the closest kernel blow-up proof; sphere/tight-design analogy is explicit. It does not by itself supply a cube near-critical positive-frame theorem. D1 must credit this mechanism, rather than claim spectral blow-up as new. Marzo's sphere p≠2 complete-interpolation obstruction does not fill a p=2 or cube hypothesis.

**Kuperberg**, [arXivmath/0405366v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0405366),15pp: post-R2 follow-through (committed after the R3 preregistration, on the supplemental reviewer's lead) read §3 Prop3.1/Thm3.2 and explanation p5; §6 Thm6.1/Cor6.2 pp11–13 and Scholium6.5 statement/sketch p14. The finite subgroup/dual packing equivalence, lattice-design asymptotics and octahedral18/19 example are explicit prior art. The local density bounds concern uniform simplex measure and uniform simple polytopes, using an epsilon-net metric; their read statements give neither the product-Chebyshev fixed relative excess nor D1's near-critical frame-to-spectrum step. No transfer of measure or constants is asserted. This further removes novelty from the packing mechanism and supplies positive-cubature local-density context.
