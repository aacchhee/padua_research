## Findings on the stated results

**NOTE U3-1 — the general coherent reduction is valid, including folds.**
For beta-rho in H-perp and 2a in H, `(beta-rho) dot 2a` is an integer.
Dividing each Fourier character row by `exp(2pi i rho dot a)` leaves
exact integer signs, including multiplicities when an exponent is zero.
Fourier invertibility preserves rank; deleting coincident evaluation rows
preserves the kernel. Complex, real and rational ranks then agree by minors.
Changing representatives multiplies a row by a sign and cannot change rank.
No no-fold, cyclicity, cubature or positivity assumption is hidden here.

The new checker tests 108 combinations of degree, coherent phase and finite
group, with cyclic/noncyclic groups, redundant generators, trivial groups
and folding. All exact dictionary ranks equal the actual-node modular ranks;
all 108 changed-representative tests preserve rank. A boundary example
`H=C3, a=1/12` fails 2a in H: frequencies 0 and 3 share a character but their
phase ratio is i. Thus the proof's integer-sign mechanism cannot simply be
extended to arbitrary free phases. This is a hypothesis check, not a new
universal exclusion of other exact methods.

## Why nine corrections can repair rank without controlling quality

Let A be the evaluation matrix on the q=N-9 large component and B the nine
remaining rows, using orthonormal Chebyshev columns. Each checked large
component has exact rank q and each small component exact rank 9. Thus the
large component's kernel has dimension exactly nine. Choose orthonormal
column bases Y,Z for its row space and kernel. Then

```text
V [Y Z] = [ A Y     0  ]
          [ B Y   B Z  ].
```

Full rank is equivalent to invertibility of the 9-by-9 repair matrix BZ.
The inverse also contains `(BZ)^-1 BY (AY)^-1`; determinant maximization
alone gives no bound on that term, evaluation norms or the Lebesgue function.
This elementary factorization explains exactly what the finite certificates
settle and what remains missing from a stability argument.

There is also a useful audit boundary: `sigma_min(V)<=sigma_min(A)`. The
span of ker(A) plus a least right singular vector of A has dimension ten;
it meets ker(B) nontrivially, giving a unit vector with this bound. Since
the basis is orthonormal for the probability Chebyshev measure, a unit
coefficient polynomial has sup norm at least one, yielding
`Lambda(V)>=1/sigma_min(V)>=1/sigma_min(A)`. Floating singular values are
diagnostic estimates of these bounds, not rigorous numerical enclosures.

At n8 the main-component floors are only **2.36 (M), 2.81 (R)**, well below
QR45.54. Therefore this test does **not** prohibit a useful nine-node repair.
At n7 the observed condition numbers of BZ are about 39.5/36.7; at n8 they
are 28.85/7.22. These diagnose the selected witnesses only. Neither nine
correction nodes nor exact full rank implies good quality, and the present
poor examples do not imply that nine corrections can never provide it.
