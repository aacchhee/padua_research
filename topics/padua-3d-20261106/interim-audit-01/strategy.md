withdrew the attempted distinction between explicit weighted design and MZ

into N-node interpolation. The source-dependent log3 projection theorem is
also not a bound for a selected minimal interpolant: the T9 correction factor
`||C^-1||_infinity` is still required.

addresses the actual failure of the first rule. Neither that distinction nor
the elementary separation/Neumann arguments establish historical novelty:
QR/Fekete extraction, determinant ascent and Lebesgue-oriented optimization
already occur in the retained literature/registry. The exact payload theorem
has independent value as a reproducible finite artifact, without being a
solution of the Padua-type asymptotic problem.

U3 has the most direct connection to the unresolved structural target, but
