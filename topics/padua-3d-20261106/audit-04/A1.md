3. The dual group is h = Σ j_i·g_i, with g_i the columns of adj = qB⁻¹ and ranges j1<a, j2<d, j3<f. This gives q distinct elements with Bh≡0. Folding by min(h, q−h) is exact, and q≤8|X|.
4. The producer's character-incidence rank, with classes `k·adj mod q` = Z³/L and sign variants weighted 2^{-s}, equals the real evaluation rank because characters are linearly independent.
5. **Weight transfer** (`t9-r06-phase-weight-boundaries.md:32-42`), re-derived:
   - F = 1−v²−w²+2uvw = u² ≥ 0 on X, with deg F ≤ r (since b≤a), integral 0 and F(1,1,1)=1.

   - So any rule on the whole of X with nonnegative weights and positive mass at the identity forces dual minimum ≥2n. Haar weights work for every admissible lattice, so admissible enumeration decides existence for every strictly positive full-support unshifted rule.
6. **Packing window.**
