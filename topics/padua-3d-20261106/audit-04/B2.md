## 1. Exact claim: 455 stored points are Π_12-unisolvent

**Determinant test.**
- Exact integer matrices M' = diag(2^{12k_i})·M (k_i = largest denominator exponent of point i), in the monomial basis and in the product-Chebyshev basis (integer recurrence).
- Primes checked by trial division. det(M') mod p, monomial / Chebyshev:

|---|---|---|
| 2147483647 | 1868890202 | 1458982917 |
| 998244353 | 119012695 | 368878056 |
| 1000000007 | 293828173 | 299984590 |

**Proof text** (theory/u2-degree12-unisolvence.md:100-126) is correct:

- 0 violations;

- separation ratio ≥ 0.9085, accepted ρ ≤ 0.49983, smallest Armijo margin 9.8e-8 (n2);

**Guard argument** (u2-admissible-norm.md:80-85) is exact:

- the floating guard is only a safeguard, as disclosed.
