## 0. Outcome

The campaign produced no programme. Twenty generator drafts went to the gate; eleven drafts passed the gate and, after merging gen4-draft-3 into gen1-draft-1 (M1) and gen2-draft-4 into gen2-draft-2 (M2) and absorbing gen4-draft-5's negative note into gen4-draft-2, formed nine distinct candidates (two merged, seven single) that went to nine adversarial checks [response-01, A9.1: the audited text said "nine pass items (seven distinct programmes …)", subtracting the merges twice]; seven were killed (M1, M2, gen1-2, gen2-3, gen3-1, gen3-3, gen4-1) and two were wounded (gen1-3, gen4-2); the two decisive tests then killed gen1-3's residue (its windowed recurrence does not exist as stated and the corrected one is unstable, `checks/decisive-gen1-3.md`) and confirmed gen4-2's residue as a provisional two-to-three-paper project in classical approximation theory with a criterion-5 fit flag (provisional: its counting certificate is not established and its fit-precision target was not met, audit-01 A1, A6) (`checks/decisive-gen4-2.md`). Zero candidates meet all five programme criteria of `CAMPAIGN_CONTEXT.md`. The campaign rules ask for at least three survivors and for honesty when there are fewer; there are none, and this report does not pad the list. What AC gets instead is one project proposal (`proposal-1.md`), one note-sized residue, a set of measured and proved by-products for the running topics (b), (c), (d) and the working track, a merged acquisition list, and a process record with three concrete changes for a future campaign.

## 2. Why the campaign found no programme

**(a) Literature the baseline had not read in fulltext decided most kills.** Step 0 ran no web search by design (`lit/baseline.md` §5) and lists Degani–Schiff–Tannor 2005, Kolomoitsev–Lomako 2021 and De Marchi 2022 among the PDFs it did not have; the gate ran no web search either (`gate.md` §0, coverage limits). The owner checks therefore happened only at step 4, after generation and gating, and five of the nine checks were decided by one source each, read at the coverage recorded in the adversarial file — a full text, selected sections or a tool digest; the Codex audit primary-checked excerpts of DST 2005, Kreuzer–Robbiano 2025, Kolomoitsev–Lomako 2021 and arXiv 2605.23299 [response-01, A9.2: the audited text said "one fulltext each"]: M1 by DST 2005 §§2–6 (symmetric-positive extension IEP, gauge and dimension counts, compatibility condition, Möller as Thm 12, completion by gradient flow/Newton/rotations; `adversarial-M1.md` (i), (iv)); M2 by Kreuzer–Robbiano 2025 Prop. 5.4–5.6 and Kreuzer–Long–Robbiano 2022 Cor. 7.5 (the planar simplicial border basis scheme is an affine cell on the rim coefficients, i.e. M2's "minimal chart"; `adversarial-M2.md` (i), (ii)(3)); gen3-3 by Álvarez de Morales–Fernández–Pérez–Piñar 2008 Thm 3.1 (the (b−1, b−1) band is a specialisation) and by Olver's 2019 Leverhulme project "Constructive approximation theory on and inside algebraic curves and surfaces" (`adversarial-gen3-3.md` (i), (ii)(1)); gen1-2 by the Granada coherent-pair papers (JAT 245 (2019), Mediterr. J. Math. 18 (2021)) and Van Buggenhout 2023 §4.2/§5.1.2 (`adversarial-gen1-2.md` (i), (ii)); gen4-2's sub-problem 2 by Kolomoitsev–Lomako Thm 2.2 (projection constant capped at (16/π⁴) log² n) and its sub-problem 4 by Briani–Sommariva–Vianello 2012 Table 5 and De Marchi–Usevich 2014 Prop. 1 (`adversarial-gen4-2.md` (iii), (ii)). In every case the generator had the paper as an abstract, a listing or a memory, and the gate accepted the generator's "new within documented search" because the documented search was thin.

**(b) The Huhtanen collapse and tensor-product structure turn most bivariate commuting-pair questions into univariate ones.** The brief's obstruction row "Huhtanen 2002 collapse" (B generically a polynomial in A) was read by every generator as a remark about f(A,B); the adversaries showed it bites algorithmically. gen2-3's joint spectrum is the spectrum of H_x plus a discrete pairing: eigh(H_x) with Rayleigh quotients gives all 1035 nodes to 3.2·10⁻¹⁵ in 0.25 s, Eidelman–Haimovici 2023 is the published route, and the one two-index statement (the δ-tolerant symmetrised-product count) cannot do better than √δ on symmetric pairs (PD-N?programme-ideas-claude-01; no claim for computed sections) (`adversarial-gen2-3.md` (ii), (iii)). gen4-1's saturating case is a product grid, where the total-degree discrete OPs are products of 1-D ones, so s_k(x,y) = Σ_{i+j≤k} u_i(x)u_j(y) exactly and the saturated set, the Christoffel law and C₂ = C₁ are corollaries of Kuijlaars–Rakhmanov (`adversarial-gen4-1.md` (ii)(a)–(b)). gen3-1's "exact commutation in the Fourier basis" is false because the truncated sections' commutator is the unitary invariant −(B_xᵀB_y − B_yᵀB_x), and the circulant structure is a dimension reduction, not a rank structure (`adversarial-gen3-1.md` (iii)). M1's functional route is well defined beyond Π_{2k+1} only for lifts f = φ∘g that are functions of one Hermitian operator, where univariate Lanczos reaches parity at k = 4 (measured for k ≤ 4 only) (`adversarial-M1.md` (iii)(a)). gen3-2 was deferred by the gate for the same reason (angular decoupling to univariate Laguerre blocks), and in gen1-3's decisive test the harmonic targets were won by rational Krylov on the single normal matrix X+iY (`adversarial-gen1-3.md` (iii)). A one-line "does it reduce to univariate or to a tensor product" test before generation would have removed four of the nine pass items.

**(c) The structured-matrix angle on Padua-type sets is already occupied, so new mechanisms landed in adjacent fields.** Exclusions (a)–(d) and the already-judged list cover recurrences, downdating, conditioning, HSS compressibility, Uvarov and measure modification, border bases, cubature extensions and f(A,B) Krylov. The one draft that attacked a recorded escape clause head-on (gen2-draft-1, divide-and-conquer tears of the pair) fired its own kill at N = 1035 (`gate-rejected.md`, gen2-draft-1). The generators' genuinely new mechanisms therefore sat in approximation theory (gen4-1 constrained equilibrium, gen4-2 Lebesgue functions, gen3-1's κ-laws), commutative algebra (M2's chart is the border basis scheme; gen1-2's breakdown is Alexander–Hirschowitz), analytic spectral methods (gen3-3) and rational approximation (gen1-3) — fields with active owners (Xu; Kreuzer–Robbiano; Granada; Olver; Leuven NALAG and NUMA; Padova and Kraków; Kolomoitsev–Lomako) and where criterion 5 fails or is flagged. The pattern is visible in the verdicts: every kill is (ii) "really a note" after removal of owned content, never (i) "a named programme exists on exactly this", and the generators' own pre-registered kill tests mostly did not fire (gen3-1's κ is mild, gen1-2's similarity test failed, gen2-3's banded LDLᵀ passed at N = 231, gen4-2's corner argmax had no counterexample at 75 tested degrees to n = 200). The tests measured whether a mechanism exists; they did not measure whether it was new or large enough, and that is where the candidates died.
