## 2. Why the campaign found no programme

**(a) Literature the baseline had not read in fulltext decided most kills.** Step 0 ran no web search by design (`lit/baseline.md` §5) and lists Degani–Schiff–Tannor 2005, Kolomoitsev–Lomako 2021 and De Marchi 2022 among the PDFs it did not have; the gate ran no web search either (`gate.md` §0, coverage limits). The owner checks therefore happened only at step 4, after generation and gating, and five of the nine checks were decided by one fulltext each: M1 by DST 2005 §§2–6 (symmetric-positive extension IEP, gauge and dimension counts, compatibility condition, Möller as Thm 12, completion by gradient flow/Newton/rotations; `adversarial-M1.md` (i), (iv)); M2 by Kreuzer–Robbiano 2025 Prop. 5.4–5.6 and Kreuzer–Long–Robbiano 2022 Cor. 7.5 (the planar simplicial border basis scheme is an affine cell on the rim coefficients, i.e. M2's "minimal chart"; `adversarial-M2.md` (i), (ii)(3)); gen3-3 by Álvarez de Morales–Fernández–Pérez–Piñar 2008 Thm 3.1 (the (b−1, b−1) band is a specialisation) and by Olver's 2019 Leverhulme project "Constructive approximation theory on and inside algebraic curves and surfaces" (`adversarial-gen3-3.md` (i), (ii)(1)); gen1-2 by the Granada coherent-pair papers (JAT 245 (2019), Mediterr. J. Math. 18 (2021)) and Van Buggenhout 2023 §4.2/§5.1.2 (`adversarial-gen1-2.md` (i), (ii)); gen4-2's sub-problem 2 by Kolomoitsev–Lomako Thm 2.2 (projection constant capped at (16/π⁴) log² n) and its sub-problem 4 by Briani–Sommariva–Vianello 2012 Table 5 and De Marchi–Usevich 2014 Prop. 1 (`adversarial-gen4-2.md` (iii), (ii)). In every case the generator had the paper as an abstract, a listing or a memory, and the gate accepted the generator's "new within documented search" because the documented search was thin.

**(b) The Huhtanen collapse and tensor-product structure turn most bivariate commuting-pair questions into univariate ones.** The brief's obstruction row "Huhtanen 2002 collapse" (B generically a polynomial in A) was read by every generator as a remark about f(A,B); the adversaries showed it bites algorithmically. gen2-3's joint spectrum is the spectrum of H_x plus a discrete pairing: eigh(H_x) with Rayleigh quotients gives all 1035 nodes to 3.2·10⁻¹⁵ in 0.25 s, Eidelman–Haimovici 2023 is the published route, and the one two-index statement (the δ-tolerant count) has the generic exponent ½ (`adversarial-gen2-3.md` (ii), (iii)). gen4-1's saturating case is a product grid, where the total-degree discrete OPs are products of 1-D ones, so s_k(x,y) = Σ_{i+j≤k} u_i(x)u_j(y) exactly and the saturated set, the Christoffel law and C₂ = C₁ are corollaries of Kuijlaars–Rakhmanov (`adversarial-gen4-1.md` (ii)(a)–(b)). gen3-1's "exact commutation in the Fourier basis" is false because the truncated sections' commutator is the unitary invariant −(B_xᵀB_y − B_yᵀB_x), and the circulant structure is a dimension reduction, not a rank structure (`adversarial-gen3-1.md` (iii)). M1's functional route is well defined beyond Π_{2k+1} only for lifts f = φ∘g that are functions of one Hermitian operator, where univariate Lanczos reaches parity at k = 4 (`adversarial-M1.md` (iii)(a)). gen3-2 was deferred by the gate for the same reason (angular decoupling to univariate Laguerre blocks), and in gen1-3's decisive test the harmonic targets were won by rational Krylov on the single normal matrix X+iY (`adversarial-gen1-3.md` (iii)). A one-line "does it reduce to univariate or to a tensor product" test before generation would have removed four of the nine pass items.

**(c) The structured-matrix angle on Padua-type sets is already occupied, so new mechanisms landed in adjacent fields.** Exclusions (a)–(d) and the already-judged list cover recurrences, downdating, conditioning, HSS compressibility, Uvarov and measure modification, border bases, cubature extensions and f(A,B) Krylov. The one draft that attacked a recorded escape clause head-on (gen2-draft-1, divide-and-conquer tears of the pair) fired its own kill at N = 1035 (`gate-rejected.md`, gen2-draft-1). The generators' genuinely new mechanisms therefore sat in approximation theory (gen4-1 constrained equilibrium, gen4-2 Lebesgue functions, gen3-1's κ-laws), commutative algebra (M2's chart is the border basis scheme; gen1-2's breakdown is Alexander–Hirschowitz), analytic spectral methods (gen3-3) and rational approximation (gen1-3) — fields with active owners (Xu; Kreuzer–Robbiano; Granada; Olver; Leuven NALAG and NUMA; Padova and Kraków; Kolomoitsev–Lomako) and where criterion 5 fails or is flagged. The pattern is visible in the verdicts: every kill is (ii) "really a note" after removal of owned content, never (i) "a named programme exists on exactly this", and the generators' own pre-registered kill tests mostly did not fire (gen3-1's κ is mild, gen1-2's similarity test failed, gen2-3's banded LDLᵀ passed at N = 231, gen4-2's corner argmax holds to n = 200). The tests measured whether a mechanism exists; they did not measure whether it was new or large enough, and that is where the candidates died.
