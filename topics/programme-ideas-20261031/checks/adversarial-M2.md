## Attack (ii): really a note

**(1) The dimension theorem is a one-page corollary; written proof.** Let (H_x, H_y) be real symmetric N×N, N = dim Π_n, supported on the generalized-Hessenberg pattern of a graded order (every monomial m_k with k ≥ 2 has an x-parent or a y-parent m_j with π(j) = k), commuting, with all pivots H_x[π_x(j), j], H_y[π_y(j), j] nonzero. (a) Commuting symmetric ⇒ simultaneously orthogonally diagonalisable: H_x = QΛ_xQ^T, H_y = QΛ_yQ^T, joint eigenvectors q_k with joint eigenvalues (x_k, y_k). (b) Define polynomials by the recurrence read column-wise: p_1 = 1 and, for m_k = x·m_j (or y·m_j), p_k := (x p_j − Σ_{i<k} H_x[i,j] p_i)/H_x[k,j]. (c) For any joint eigenvector v with eigenvalue λ = (x,y), the column-j equation (H_x v)_j = x v_j, which by symmetry and the pattern reads Σ_{i≤k} H_x[i,j] v_i = x v_j, gives by induction v_i = v_1 p_i(λ) for all i; hence v_1 ≠ 0 (else v = 0) and the joint eigenspaces are one-dimensional, so the N joint eigenvalues are distinct and q_k = ±P(λ_k)/‖P(λ_k)‖, P = (p_1,…,p_N)^T. (d) Q^TQ = I is Σ_k w_k p_i(λ_k)p_j(λ_k) = δ_{ij} with w_k = ‖P(λ_k)‖^{−2} > 0: the p_i are orthonormal for μ = Σ w_k δ_{λ_k}; being N independent elements of Π_n they are a basis, so the λ_k are unisolvent, and p_k has leading monomial m_k by construction, so {p_i} is the graded orthonormal basis of μ. (e) H_x = QΛ_xQ^T = [⟨x p_j, p_i⟩_μ]: the pair is the section of the N-node measure μ. (f) Conversely nodes and weights determine the pattern pair with positive pivots uniquely (QR of the weighted graded Vandermonde with positive diagonal is unique — this is the ETNA 2014 p. 133 remark and the nonzero-pivot "implicit-Q theorem"), the map (λ, w)/scaling → pair is smooth with smooth inverse (eigendecomposition with simple joint spectrum), so the positive-pivot commuting pattern pairs form an embedded manifold of dimension 2N + N − 1 = 3N − 1. (g) Block-tridiagonal class: the gauge Π_{k≤n} O(k+1) acts; LQ-factoring each A_{k,x} = [L 0] fixes the gauge up to signs and forces the grlex pattern (induction on the leading forms), so the dimension is 3N−1 + Σ_k k(k+1)/2 = 3N−1 + C(n+2,3). This reproduces both of the generator's measured numbers (re-measured here: 29/44/62/83 and 39/64/97/139 for n = 3..6, `m2_favard_commutant.py`). Steps (a)–(e) are Xu TAMS 342 Thm 3.2/3.4 + Mysovskikh in finite form, (d)–(e) are the positive flat-extension theorem, and (b)'s PD-T?…-304(b) ("exactly commuting square data ⇒ N_n-point positive rule whose nodes are the joint eigenvalues", PROVED in L3/R01) is the same statement. The draft's "≈ 4N for general measures" is a count of non-commuting sections (moments up to degree 2n+1 ≈ 2n²); by Xu Thm 3.4 the degree-n sections of a non-discrete measure commute only when a Gaussian cubature of degree 2n+1 exists, in which case they are the sections of that cubature's N-node measure. There is no second component.

## Attack (iii): ill-posed or blocked

- **"≈ 4N parameters for general measures"** (host draft, central question and T1) is ill-posed inside the commuting variety: commuting degree-n sections are exactly the N-node sections (Xu Thm 3.4 + (ii)(1)); the ≈ 4N count (moments of degree ≤ 2n+1) belongs to non-commuting sections, which are outside the question. The gate's merged wording (3N−1) is the only consistent one.
- **"Minimal recurrence-compatible chart"** is undefined in the drafts ("triangular", "chasable", "in the order Algorithm 1 computes them"); once defined structurally (Dulmage–Mendelsohn triangularity of the linearised commutator system) it is answered in the measured scope: top-down yes, bottom-up no. Neither answer yields an algorithm, because the top layer is not computable without the lower layers (orthonormal basis of degree n needs degrees < n), and no stability statement exists for the top-down completion.
- **Core/canonical form (sub-problem 3)**: the obstruction "Q not HSS-compressible" is not avoided by moving to the condensed pair; the condensed pair has Θ(N^{3/2}) entries and any product-of-cores representation of it has at least as many parameters (bandwidth count in (ii)(5)). For the normal case the windowed HL-SIMAX recurrence is measured unstable in (b) (PD-N?…-102); for the torus, F10 applies.
- ~~**Pencil normal form with 2N unitary parameters** is blocked by the orbit count in (ii)(6): the comrade pencils of a fixed X form an (n+1)(n+3)-dimensional set modulo O×O.~~ [response-01, 2026-11-01, A4: the orbit count blocks it only for the unrestricted GL-gauged class; for the normalised family the question is open.]
- **Pencil conditioning**: the brief's "Graf–Townsend instability" escape clause (orthonormal bases) is irrelevant here because the comparison object, the symmetric pair, has condition number 1; the measured pencil factors (10–30 on Padua, 10²–3.5·10³ on random sets) leave nothing to escape to.
- **Huhtanen 2002 collapse** does not bite (it is about f(A,B); here only the commutant inside a pattern was involved, and it is the affine group).
