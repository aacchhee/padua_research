## Attack (ii): really a note

Removing what is known or false, sub-problem by sub-problem:

1. **Hilbert function and block sizes (sub-problem 1).** For M > 2mn equispaced samples the discrete measure has the same Gram on Π_n as the continuous curve measure dt (trapezoidal exactness), so the "sampled" Hilbert function is the Hilbert function of the real curve γ(S¹), which is Zariski dense in an irreducible complex curve of degree D. Textbook: h(d) = C(d+2,2) for d < D, h(d) = C(d+2,2) − C(d−D+2,2) for d ≥ D, increments 1, 2, …, D−1, D, D, D, … (first kernel element = the implicit equation at degree D). D itself is the Sendra–Winkler formula D = 2m/τ with τ the tracing index (τ = 2 for cosine-only Lissajous and odd rhodoneas, 1 generically; MEASURED agreement for every family in `kappa_out.txt`). The block sizes of (H_x, H_y) stabilising at D is HL 2002 Cor. 2.2 + Thm 2.3 (p. 398), and HL's Example 2.1 is the circle case. The "classification of degenerate cases" is the classification of improper trigonometric parametrisations (Hong–Schicho simplification; Chebyshev varieties for the cosine-only case). Nothing is left for a paper; the draft itself says the proof is "textbook once the implicit degree is known".
2. **Structured computation (sub-problem 3).** Dead by its own kill criterion and by an identity — see (iii). Residue: data along γ are Fourier coefficients already; the recurrence/IEP is a QR of the (2mn+1)×N_n coefficient matrix. At the degrees where κ is finite (n ≤ D−1 ≈ 2m−1) this matrix is at most ≈ 4m² × 2m²; there is no size, no HSS, no bulge-chasing question.
3. **Exact arithmetic (sub-problem 5).** Implicitization of trigonometric curves is Hong–Schicho 1998 (RISC 96-13/97-09); the degree is Sendra–Winkler 2001; hybrid trigonometric varieties 2017. A footnote.
4. **Conditioning κ_n(γ) (sub-problem 2).** Survives the kill test (see Scripts): rhodonea k against the disk has κ_{D−2} = 3.7, 6.1, 8.5, 10.7, 13.1, 15.3 for k = 3, 5, …, 13 (≈ 1.2k, linear) and κ_{D−1} growing ≈ ×3.9 per step; phase-shifted Lissajous (cos 5t, cos(6t+φ)) has κ_n = 1 for n ≤ 5, a φ-dependent constant (1.3–8) for 6 ≤ n ≤ 10 and ≤ 14 at n = D−1 = 11. But (a) the sup-norm positive regime for every curve is the Dubiner-covering principle (‖p‖_K ≤ sec(nρ)‖p‖_γ for nρ < π/2; Bos 2017 Prop. 0.2 is exactly this for Lissajous curves), the L² version following with Nikolskii factors; (b) the families that are mild are mild because their frequency support is sparse, so the Gram in the reference-orthonormal basis is I plus aliasing terms indexed by integer relations Σ n_i b_i = 0 — the Lissajous aliasing arithmetic of BDMV 2017 Prop. 0.3 / Thm 1 and of Dencker–Erb; for rhodoneas the aliasing lives in Erb's parity-modified Chebyshev–Fourier frame on the disk (r = cos kθ is a Lissajous-type curve in polar coordinates — inference, not proved here); (c) generic full-spectrum trigonometric curves are hopeless relative to any fixed reference domain they do not fill (κ ≈ e^{2.3n}–e^{3.3n} against the square, MEASURED, m = 3–8). So the open content is one theorem family: "κ-laws for sparse-frequency trajectories up to degree D−1, including the geometric breakdown at D−1". That is one approximation-theory paper, provable with owned tools.
5. **Reconstruction (sub-problem 4)** is an application of 4 plus a least-squares solve with an MPI noise model; it needs the Bruker/OpenMPI data and an imaging collaborator; the competitors are the trajectory-independent model-based line and Lübeck's DCR.
6. **Escalation (sub-problem 6)** follows BDMV 2017 (cube) and Erb 2020 (sphere) templates once 4 exists.

## Attack (iii): ill-posed or blocked

- **The "Fourier-basis commutation structure" is a renaming of an identity, and the identity goes the other way.** For any commuting pair X, Y (any node set) and Q_n an orthonormal basis of the graded Krylov space K_n, [Q_nᵀXQ_n, Q_nᵀYQ_n] = Q_nᵀ(XP_nY − YP_nX)Q_n = −Q_nᵀ(XP_n^⊥Y − YP_n^⊥X)Q_n = −(B_xᵀB_y − B_yᵀB_x) placed in the degree-n diagonal block, where B_x, B_y are the degree-n → degree-(n+1) coupling blocks. This is exact, independent of the basis of ℂ^M (the Fourier basis is a unitary change of coordinates; the commutator norm is invariant), and nonzero whenever K_n ≠ ℂ^M. MEASURED (`commutator_sections.py`): rhodonea k = 7, generic degree-4 curve and the Lissajous (5,6) control, M = 64, 128, 512, n ≤ 8: ‖[H_x, H_y]‖₂ = 0.08–0.50 (relative 0.09–0.71), equal to ‖B_xᵀB_y − B_yᵀB_x‖₂ to ≤ 8e-15 (≤ 2e-12 where κ is large), identical after mapping everything to the Fourier basis. The draft's entry-point-3 kill criterion ("if the truncated sections … do not commute to O(u‖H‖) for M = 64, 128, … sub-problem 3 is dropped") fires. Curve nodes are not special here: the Lissajous control gives 0.36–0.50 at every degree tested.
- **The template (Szegő/CMV for the pulled-back measure) is vacuous under the stated hypotheses.** Equispaced samples t_k = 2πk/M pull back to the uniform measure on the M-th roots of unity, whose CMV operator is the cyclic shift and whose Verblunsky coefficients vanish below degree M; Laurent monomials are already orthonormal. The "map between (H_x, H_y) and the CMV matrix" is H_x = Qᵀx(S)Q, the compression of a Laurent polynomial of the shift — HL's recurrence with a banded-circulant input. The only exploitable structure is MEASURED fact (3) in `commutator_out.txt`: the degree-n Krylov space is supported on Fourier modes |k| ≤ mn (coefficients beyond mn ≤ 3e-15 at n ≤ 6 for all three curves), so the IEP is the graded QR of a (2mn+1)×N_n matrix, computed by one FFT of the data. A non-uniform or weighted time sampling would make the Szegő side non-trivial, but that is a different candidate with different hypotheses.
- **The "Q not HSS-compressible" escape is misstated.** Q is F^* times a matrix with 2mn+1 nonzero rows; that is dimension reduction, not rank structure, and it says nothing about the HSS ranks of Q for node sets with M ≈ N (there K_n = ℂ^M and the Fourier picture is the full DFT).
- **Central question.** "Which polynomial information is recoverable" is answered in full by the Hilbert function (all of Π_n for n < D, Π_n/(F) for n ≥ D); "through which structured computation" by the FFT+QR above; only "with what conditioning as a function of (n, m, γ)" is open, and its positive regime is the Dubiner-net principle. The gate's obstruction "computed H_x, H_y never commute" (brief §3) is confirmed, not escaped, by the identity above.

## Attack (iv): renaming of an exclusion

- **vs (d) M4/M5.** Sub-problem 2's quantity is exactly an MZ constant: κ_n(γ; w)² = λ_max/λ_min of the Gram of the curve measure in the w-orthonormal basis of Π_n, the same measurement as M4/M5's λ_max/λ_min of a rule's Gram; the kernel at degree D is M5's recorded obstruction in the plan ("polynomial vanishing at all nodes kills n ≥ node-count"), with D in place of the node count; the proof tools are the same (Gram spectra, Schur/aliasing). What differs is only the rule family (curve-supported positive rules that are exact for nothing on the square) and the regime (n < D instead of n ≤ N−1). The gate's separation ("a continuous curve measure with a kernel is not a quadrature rule on Π_{N−1}") holds for the object, not for the measurement or the method; the rename and the handover clause the gate demanded are therefore necessary, and with them sub-problem 2 is not ownable by this candidate without (d)'s consent.
- **vs the judged "explicit Padua recurrences" (R2 §1).** Excluded by construction for cosine-only Lissajous curves; but the mild families found here (phase-shifted Lissajous, rhodoneas against the disk) are mild *through the same Lissajous–Chebyshev aliasing structure*, so the surviving positive content is a variant of the Dencker–Erb/BDMV exactness arithmetic rather than something separated from it.
- **vs (b).** The "exact commutation in the Fourier basis" claim, had it been true, would have been a finite-precision statement about a special node family (OBST-01/-13 territory). It is false, so there is nothing to hand over.
- **vs gen1-draft-6.** The "X, Y polynomials of one unitary" remark that the gate re-housed here is the circulant structure above; it is a remark, as the gate said of gen1-draft-6.
