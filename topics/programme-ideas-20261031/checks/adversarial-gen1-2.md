## Attack (ii): really a note

The draft's five sub-problems collapse as follows.

1. **Structure theorem (sub-problem 1) is a lemma, and the univariate lemma verbatim.** X̃ = D + N with D = diag(node coordinates) symmetric and N the s unit couplings (rank s). Hence H_x = QᵀDQ + QᵀNQ = S + R, S symmetric, rank R ≤ s; the lower off-band part of H_x vanishes (graded Krylov nesting, exactly as in the seed), so on every strictly-upper off-band position H_x = (R − Rᵀ), whence every off-diagonal block H_x[deg ≤ i, deg ≥ i+2] has rank ≤ 2s and rank(H_x − H_xᵀ) ≤ 2s. That is the whole proof; the draft calls it two-line. The d-variate version and higher Jordan blocks change nothing (rank R = number of derivative functionals). The classical univariate form of the same fact — multiplication by a polynomial ρ vanishing to the right order at the mass points is self-adjoint, giving a banded symmetric (2m+3)-term relation (Marcellán–Xu survey §7, eq. (7.4)-type; Hermoso–Huertas–Lastra–Marcellán arXiv:2203.03318, "(2N+1)-banded symmetric") — transfers word for word: for ρ = Π_j((x−x_j)²+(y−y_j)²), Qᵀρ(X̃,Ỹ)Q is symmetric (MEASURED, 2e-16, script T2). Van Buggenhout 2023 §5.1.2 already names the "Hermitian diagonal plus rank-one" splitting as the explanation to be worked out. Nothing here is a paper on its own; it is Section 2 of one.
   Factual flag: the generator's "measured upper off-band quasiseparable rank is exactly 2s" was computed (per its committed code) as the rank of the *masked* strictly-upper-off-band matrix, which is not a quasiseparable rank and cannot equal 2s in the reported scopes (at deg 3 the masked region has 3 rows; MEASURED: masked rank 3/3/3 for s = 1/2/3 at (12, 3) and 9/14/15/15 for s = 1..4 at (40, 6)). The correct law — max off-diagonal block rank = min(2s, block size), skew rank = 2s — holds (MEASURED, `qs_rank.py`, 5 seeds per case). The committed script `checks/code/gen1/sobolev.py` does not run (its `run()` subtracts `None`), so the reported numbers did not come from it.

2. **The unitary IEP (sub-problem 2) is the seed's Algorithm 1 unchanged.** The seed (Numer. Algorithms 55, §4.1, Algorithm 1 and (14)–(15)) starts from D = [w | X | Y] and applies Givens similarities to "make element d_{i,π(j)} zero" below the pivots; nothing in the algorithm or in the derivation (§3, X Φ = Φ H_x) uses diagonality or normality — the seed already treats complex nodes, for which X, Y are non-Hermitian and H_x, H_y have full upper parts (§4.2 restricts to real points only to show symmetry). Replacing X, Y by X̃, Ỹ requires only data(xp) = X̃ data(p) (MEASURED 1e-16, script T1 `cons_err`) and a weight vector with zeros at the derivative rows — exactly Van Buggenhout's 2023 §3.2 eqs (11)–(12) and §4.2 updating in one variable. So "Givens updating with 3×3 Jordan node blocks" is a transcription. The implicit-Q/uniqueness for the pair (draft item (c)) is the same Krylov argument with nonzero pivots. What is *not* a transcription: (a) exploiting band + rank-2s to cut the O(N²)-per-node chase cost (a generator representation that survives Givens similarities) — a real but single algorithmic task, which even the univariate template has not done; (b) a windowed variant with 2s carried generator vectors — this is the known short-recurrence theory for Hermitian-plus-low-rank operators (Faber–Manteuffel / Barth–Manteuffel multiple recursions; named in Van Buggenhout §5.1.2) transcribed to the pair, and its finite precision is exclusion (b)'s L1/L2 question with a non-normal node operator. One paper, co-owned in method.

3. **Breakdown theory (sub-problem 3) is a restatement plus a citation.** "Zero pivot at degree k ⇔ Hilbert-function defect of the fat-point ideal in degree k" is the definition of a dependent column of the Hermite–Vandermonde in a graded order; any exact orthogonalisation (LDLᵀ of the Sobolev Gram, Gram–Schmidt, the IEP) breaks exactly there (MEASURED over ℚ: `ah_breakdown_exact.py`, the first zero LDLᵀ pivot sits at the dependent monomial in every case). For generic double points the defect list is Alexander–Hirschowitz (verified, Brambilla–Ottaviani Thm 1.1); for mixed double and simple generic points the defect is governed by the double-point part (a general simple point cuts the dimension of a nonempty linear system by one — elementary, conditional reasoning here, not from a source; MEASURED consistent: case B); for special configurations it is the Hilbert function of fat points — classical commutative algebra, not NLA. Entry point 2, run exactly (5 seeds each): 5 double points, degree 4 → rank 14/15 with kernel = (conic)² in 5/5 seeds; control 4 double + 3 simple, degree 4 → 15/15 in 5/5; 2 double points, degree 2 → 5/6 (double line) in 5/5; Padua degree-2 points doubled → no defect at degrees 4, 5. The thesis holds and is a worked example, not a sub-problem. "Exact look-ahead that skips the defective degree" is skipping the dependent column — Buchberger–Möller behaviour, and the zero-pivot/certificate machinery of (c) U3 and L5 §3.4 (CH IDEA-014), as the gate already noted.

4. **Bivariate coherence (sub-problem 4) is owned** (attack (i)); the GRS 2024 §5 question concerns continuous Sobolev products on the square/ball, where Granada's connection formulas are the answer in the classical cases; for the *discrete* Hermite-data product the connection to the standard OPs of the value part is the kernel formula of the Sobolev-type papers (Mello et al. 2011; Dueñas–Garza–Piñar 2015: OPs of ⟨·,·⟩ + gradient-at-a-point expressed through the standard family and kernels), i.e. a Sherman–Morrison on the Gram — a remark.

5. **Application track (sub-problem 5)** has customers only in d ≫ 2 UQ (gradient-enhanced PCE, compressive Hermite interpolation), where tensor/sparse bases are used and total-degree bivariate recurrence matrices are not the tool; the draft itself lists the value risk.

## Attack (iii): ill-posed or blocked

The gate's target — "the Sobolev pair is similar to a standard pair on an enlarged node set, so (b)'s theory and Van Buggenhout's updating transfer verbatim" — FAILS, and the draft's object is well defined:

- **Square case** (N ≥ M+2s, Q square): H_x = QᵀX̃Q is unitarily similar to X̃, which has a 2×2 Jordan block at every gradient node (MEASURED: M = 6, s = 2, deg 3: rank(H_x − x_jI) = 8 of 10 at both gradient nodes, two singular values ~1e-16; eigenvector matrix condition 9e7, i.e. defective). No similarity to any diagonalisable, hence to any standard, pair.
- **Least-squares case** (N < M+2s): H_x has complex eigenvalues in 10/20 random instances at (M, s, deg) = (12, 2, 3) and 19/20 at (40, 4, 6) (MEASURED, script T4). A real standard pair has symmetric sections with real spectra, so no similarity exists either; zeros of discrete Sobolev OPs being complex is the univariate phenomenon reappearing.
- **The enlarged-node-set picture that is true:** the Sobolev product is the ε → 0 limit of a *block-weighted* standard product on M+2s distinct nodes (triples z_j, z_j+εe₁, z_j+εe₂ with 3×3 positive-definite divided-difference weights): ‖H_x(ε) − H_x^{Sob}‖ = 2.2e-1, 2.2e-2, 2.2e-3, 2.2e-4 at ε = 1e-1 … 1e-4 (MEASURED, script T5), with the ε-node operator's eigenvector condition number growing like 2/ε (21, 200, 2000, 20000). A *scalar*-weighted standard pair on the same enlarged nodes is symmetric for every weight choice (MEASURED 1e-16) and cannot approach the Sobolev pair (skew rank 4). This is the matrix form of de Boor–Shekhtman (LAA 2008: bivariate ideal projectors are limits of Lagrange projectors; for first-order jets the limit is the simplex coalescence z_j, z_j+εe₁, z_j+εe₂ in any d — standard, not traced to a source here). So (b)'s theory does not transfer by similarity; what transfers is the *method* (Krylov nesting, Givens updating), which is attack (ii), not (iii).
- Obstruction table: "computed H_x, H_y never commute" applies to the algorithm as in the standard case; the exact Sobolev pair commutes exactly (the draft's "rank-s defect" of commutation has no referent: X̃Ỹ = ỸX̃ to 0.0, as the draft's own entry point 3 also states). The sections' commutator has rank n+1 for s ≥ 1 (and n for s = 0, skew-symmetric) at deg 6 and is supported on the top-degree *columns* only, but, unlike the standard case, on all rows (MEASURED, script T3; the generator's "4 and 7, independent of s" is this rank n+1). Huhtanen's collapse is inapplicable (non-Hermitian pair), as the draft says — this removes a shortcut, it does not add a problem. Popov curse: exact Hermite-data OP generation is an instance of (a)'s balanced exact problem with more functionals; nothing new.

Verdict on (iii): not ill-posed, not blocked; the only "blocked" element is the window variant's finite precision, which is (b)'s open question with a non-normal operator.

## Required changes if it survives

It does not survive as a programme. For the record, what would have to change to re-propose it, and what the surviving note needs:

- Drop sub-problem 4 (owned: Granada coherent pairs, JAT 245 (2019); Koornwinder–Sobolev, MJM 18 (2021)); drop sub-problem 3 as a sub-problem (keep the AH example, now verified, as a remark citing Brambilla–Ottaviani Thm 1.1 and CH IDEA-014 / L5 §3.4 for zero-pivot handling); drop sub-problem 5 unless a bivariate gradient-data customer is named.
- Retract "the computed pair's rank-s commutation defect" (the exact node operators commute exactly; sections' commutator rank is n+1 as in the standard case) and the "measured exactly 2s quasiseparable rank" wording; state the law as "off-diagonal block ranks ≤ 2s and skew rank 2s", proved, with the measurements of `qs_rank.py`.
- Reframe sub-problem 1 + 2 as one paper: "Bivariate discrete Sobolev OPs by a structured Givens IEP" — structure lemma, Jordan-block updating (seed Algorithm 1 + Van Buggenhout §4.2 acknowledged as the template), a band+rank-2s generator representation that lowers the per-node chase cost, comparison with Zhang et al.'s G-Arnoldi. Co-authorship with Van Buggenhout / Van Barel is the realistic route (campaign rules allow it); Van Buggenhout now sits in Marcellán's department, which also owns the analytic side.
- A resurrecting mechanism would have to be new: e.g. a finite-precision phenomenon of the windowed recurrence that is specific to non-normal node operators and not absorbed by (b) L1/L2 (decisive test: compare loss of orthogonality of the windowed Sobolev recurrence with 2s carried generators against the s = 0 case at the same node set, n ≤ 8; if the loss law is the same function of the Ritz residuals of Z = H_x + iH_y, nothing is new), or a bivariate customer whose gradient data cannot be handled by G-Arnoldi.
- Papers that MUST be read before any first paper: Van Buggenhout, Numer.
