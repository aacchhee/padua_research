## Unsuccessful and adjusted runs

The initial same-suite run used `ncv=40` and completed through Padua m80,
then failed at the tensor-CC m80 upper eigenpair with ARPACK nonconvergence
after 10001 iterations. An `ncv=120` retry also stalled at that upper
eigenpair and was interrupted. A small m80 diagnostic confirmed that its
minimum converged immediately while its maximum did not converge within
21 iterations. The final runner retains matrix-free eigsh for every Padua
case and tensor controls through m60; at tensor m80/100 it solves the four
coordinate-parity blocks separately and checks the resulting full vector
against the nodal operator. The maximum dense block dimension is 1275 at
m100; no 5050-by-5050 dense Gram was formed. The corrected full suite was
executed successfully, then rerun after adding explicit negative-weight
entries to the evidence. Earlier transient JSONs were replaced by the final
complete run; this history preserves the failures without treating them as
evidence of a mathematical obstruction.
