The unresolved signed-metric obligation is explicit. In continuous
orthonormal coefficients write

`G=P-U U^T`,

where P is the positive-node Gram matrix and U has columns
`sqrt(-w_B) phi(B)` for the actual negative nodes. If P is positive
definite, elementary congruence gives

`G>0 iff I-U^T P^(-1)U>0`.

`ZZ^T` and `Z^T Z` agree. More quantitatively
`G >= (1-||U^T P^(-1)U||)P` when that norm is below one.
