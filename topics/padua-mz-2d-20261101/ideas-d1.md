## Retained convention correction

The hypothesis that the signed `dx dy` rule has no inexact modes below
`2m` is false: its algebraic degree of exactness is exactly `m`, as
`T_(m+1)(y)` aliases to `T_m(x)`. This is a routine consequence of the
exact formula, not a separately proposed negative research direction.
The classical positive Chebyshev cubature has a different measure and
a different exactness degree. Conflating these measures would
invalidate the moment pencil.
