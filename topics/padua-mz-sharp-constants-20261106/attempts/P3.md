## Round 2 — sharp primitive and all-node assembly

P3-02, ../theory/P3-discrete-primitive.md, embeds the discrete input
in harmonic-length cells and uses the exact Volterra norm. The
ordinary physical cells yield a uniform sin2 averaging error bounded
by 3E/2; the resulting leading squared coefficient is 2/pi2 before
the reciprocal-cardinal factor.

P3-03, ../theory/P3-reverse-bound.md, uses exact global cotangent
interpolation on each physical half. The bounded cot remainder costs
O(sqrt L). At good indices ceil(L2)<=k<=floor(N/L), actual CC
weights obey w_k >=pi2(1-2/L2) k/N2. The signed residual costs O(1).
Opposite-edge terms cost O(sqrt L). Microscopic inputs and endpoint
columns cost O(sqrt(L log L)); the remaining bulk, including k=N/2,
costs O(sqrt L) by exact angular DCT-I stability. Every node belongs
to exactly one group. Thus the full interpolation norm is at most

    (2sqrt(2)/pi2)L+O(sqrt(L log L)),

with the displayed explicit C_N,D_N in P3-03. This proves the reverse
liminf, and S1-02 supplies the matching limsup. P3-03 claims the full
limit pi4/8 on Pi_(N-1) and also Pi_N, through all integer N.
