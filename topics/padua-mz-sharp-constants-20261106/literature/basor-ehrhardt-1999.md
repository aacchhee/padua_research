Let M(φ)=T(φ)+H(φ) on ℓ²(Z₊), with entries T(φ)ⱼₖ=φⱼ₋ₖ and H(φ)ⱼₖ=φⱼ₊ₖ₊₁.

Proposition 2.1 (p.3), for φ∈L∞(T), gives ||φ||∞≤||M(φ)||≤√2||φ||∞; both constants are sharp. Theorem 2.2 and following remark (p.4) give ||M(φ)||=||φ||∞ when φ(t)=φ(t⁻¹) almost everywhere.

Proposition 4.1 (p.11) characterizes finite-section stability for piecewise continuous φ: M(φ) and T(φ̃) invertible, plus |σ(τ)|,|σ(τ̄)|,|σ(τ)+σ(τ̄)|<1/2 for Imτ>0, and −1/2<σ(1)<1/4, −1/4<σ(−1)<1/2. Here σ(τ)=arg(φ(τ−0)/φ(τ+0))/(2π), principal argument in (−π,π]. Stability means eventual invertibility with uniformly bounded inverses. These results provide no explicit rate ε(N).

Application remains conditional on identifying the actual Gram endpoint operator with this same-symbol construction. Different Toeplitz/Hankel symbols, a generalized pencil, and norm-convergence errors require separate work. Merely naming a symbol does not verify that identification.
