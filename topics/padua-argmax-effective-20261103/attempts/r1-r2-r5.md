## R1: what G5 actually requires

For a specified partition of the square into a physical-corner zone C,
near-noncorner-coset zone H and the remaining far zone F, an effective
completion needs three simultaneous, degree-uniform inequalities:

* on C, a strict reflection-first comparison, with equality only at the
  designated physical corners;
* on H, `S-Lambda_c <= E(n)` and `Delta >= d(n) > E(n)`;
* on F, `S <= Lambda_c-f(n)` with `f(n)>0`.

All hypotheses must hold for every n>=n0 and all points in the stated
zones, including transitions, both parities and removable collisions.
The reduction is the inherited identity `lambda=S-Delta`; it adds no
new mechanism or theorem. This is what a proposed n0 would have to solve.

The registered G2–G4 measurements do not specify E(n), d(n), f(n), the
zone boundaries or a uniform remainder. Doubling a sampled local K or
global C does not determine those missing quantities. In particular the
global estimate `S <= a Lambda_c + C log(n+1)` has a nonvanishing error
at the coset origin, whereas the localized estimate and physical defect
are needed there. The observed constant-size edge gap cannot be compared
with an unspecified O(1) or o(1) remainder.

**G5 verdict: not evaluable as a numerical threshold under its current
inputs.** Its original criterion is preserved. Neither the factor-two
kill rule nor the n0-versus-n_cert selection test is triggered by a
manufactured n0. This is a missing planning input, not a negative
mathematical result about R1 or PD-T099. The independently measured G1
capacity remains useful on its own. R6 is run as required regardless.

## R5: coefficientwise dual certification is the existing Bernstein test

The cheap pre-test is whether enumerating all nodal sign polynomials
improves the existing sufficient bound on a fixed Bernstein box. Let
`L_l=sum_i c_li B_i`, where i is the tensor Bernstein index and the
`B_i` are nonnegative with sum one. For any sign vector epsilon,
`p_epsilon=sum_l epsilon_l L_l`. The coefficient of
`Lambda_c-p_epsilon` at i is `Lambda_c-sum_l epsilon_l c_li`.
Therefore

`all epsilon: Lambda_c-sum_l epsilon_l c_li >= 0`

is exactly equivalent to

`sum_l |c_li| <= Lambda_c`.

The maximizing signs at each fixed index are the signs of its coefficients;
the universal quantifiers over epsilon and i commute. Strict inequalities
give the same equivalence for bound leaves. The inherited corner equality
condition remains necessary for corner leaves. This is an elementary
reformulation of PD-T210, not a new positive result or a PD-N.

Thus the *specified full-sign-family coefficientwise Bernstein test* has
disposition **equivalent**, consolidated into IDEA320. It changes neither
the acceptance region nor the degree-uniform mathematics. It does not
rule out a genuinely different positivity representation, cancellations
after combining Bernstein indices, a smaller justified family of signs,
or sum-of-squares certificates. Those alternatives are untested and
deferred until a concrete structure and separating test are supplied.
The bounded R5 round stops here as requested; no arbitrary sign pattern
is assumed to maximize at every point.
