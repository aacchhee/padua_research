## Cotangent decomposition and signed rows

`a(1+cos v)-(sin alpha/2)sin u`, and the denominator is
`cos u-cos v`. The two elementary half-angle identities in theory12
give the stated coefficients A_v and B_v, including their signs and
their factorizations. The parity grid has exactly n first angles and
spacing `2pi/n`, so each half-angle cotangent has spacing `pi/n`.

The rational identity
`sum_k 1/(w*omega^k-1)=n/(w^n-1)` is correct. Substitution into
`cot(z+k*pi/n)=i*(w*omega^k+1)/(w*omega^k-1)` proves the complete
cotangent sum directly, wherever regular. This applies to a full row;
it does not sum an arbitrary sign-selected subset.

For a folded row use `r*=min(r,2m-r)`. Then
`n*v=r* pi-v` and `r*` has the same parity as r. The two complete
cotangents reduce to the two negative tangents in theory12. Inserting
the factored coefficients gives `n*cos^2(alpha/2)=na`. The finite
kernel extends this equality through the removable parameter values.
