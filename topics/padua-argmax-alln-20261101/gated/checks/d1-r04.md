## Positivity, pole separation and finite counts

actual numerator at least `223/512>3/8`, uniformly in the signs of both
phases and the other physical coordinate. The vertical target angles
and the horizontal selected v angles in every case satisfy this baseline.

In the small-angle case, the folded targets are `b'-gamma,b'+gamma`,
or `a'-gamma,a'+gamma`; they lie in `[pi/6,2pi/3]`. The selected periodic
angle lies within half that band and is separated from each target by
at least gamma/2. Cosine monotonicity on `[0,pi]` makes the two
denominators nonzero and opposite in sign. Their span is at most
2gamma, so the point triangle defect is at least `3/(8gamma)`.

The physical row interval has length pi/6. Its count is at least m/12
vertically or n/12 horizontally. The periodic interval has length gamma.
The spacing is `2pi/n` vertically and `2pi/m` horizontally, and
`gamma>=4pi/n>=4pi/m` permits the respective lower counts
`n gamma/(4pi)` and `m gamma/(4pi)`. Multiplication by the exact
normalization gives `1/(128pi)>1/1536` in either orientation.

In the middle-angle case, the targets `gamma+-b'` or `gamma+-a'` lie
in `[0,2pi/3]`. The selected periodic interval has half-width b'/2
or a'/2 and remains strictly inside the target band, even at all closed
interval endpoints. Crucially, the cosine span is at most **one**,
because the row angle is at most pi/6. Thus each half magnitude is
at least 3/8 and the point defect is at least 3/4.

`n/24-1>=n/48` vertically and `m/24-1>=m/48` horizontally.

`(m/24)(n/48)(3/4)/(nm)=1/1536`
