## Reconstruction of the reduction

For each physical angle point, choose a physical mesh rectangle containing
it. At a boundary an adjacent rectangle still exists because n>=2. The
two diagonally opposite odd vertices have normalized L1 distances summing
to 2. One is therefore at distance at most 1. This proves the full closed
phase diamond cover with physical J,K and odd J+K, including edges,
corners and nonunique nearest vertices. No continuity of the choice of
J,K is needed.

Endpoint-weighted reflection converts the four-copy majorant at the point
to the uniform periodic even-board sum translated by its angle. Removing
the selected odd mesh vertex changes this board to the odd board in the
primitive theorems. Consequently its S is exactly the same S in records
02 and 03, with the same corrected mode and normalization. The individual
factor `(-1)^j` in F disappears under absolute values. Thus lambda<=S and
the exact lambda=S-Delta decomposition apply at those same phases.

The contradiction argument tests only points whose value exceeds
`Lambda_c-c_delta+epsilon`, which is enough to prove the uniform upper
asymptotic bound. At such points the global primitive inequality implies
`(1-a) Lambda_c <= C log(n+1)+c_delta-epsilon`. Since each phase lies in
[-pi,pi], the sine chord estimate gives

`1-a >= (alpha^2+beta^2)/pi^2`.

The inherited explicit corner lower bound grows quadratically in log n;
therefore both phases tend to zero. Eventually they belong to the fixed
local phase neighborhood of record 03. Its uniform local supremum estimate
then gives S<=Lambda_c+o(1) along exactly this same moving sequence.

Every point of K_delta has at least one coordinate in
[delta,pi-delta]. If it is phi, the vertical projection yields
Delta>=g_v(phi)+o(1)>=c_delta+o(1). If it is theta, use the horizontal
projection. The two projection errors are uniform in the unused coordinate
and in the moving mesh/parity choice. Their maximum still tends to zero,
so a sequence alternating between the two orientations is covered without
extracting a fixed-orientation subsequence. Near-node coincidences and
physical edge points are already included in the projection lemmas.

Subtracting the defect gives lambda<=Lambda_c-c_delta+o(1), contradicting
the fixed epsilon. This proves the asserted uniform upper error bound.
Its definition supplies some integer N_delta after which the error is
less than c_delta/2. The proof supplies no numerical value for N_delta.
