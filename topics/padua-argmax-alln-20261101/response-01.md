alpha=n*acos(1-h), beta=0, theorem14(9) gives
`(lambda(c+(h,0))-Lambda_c)/h <= -q_n*alpha²/h`.
As h decreases to0, alpha²/h→2n², so A_n<=−2n²q_n. The other
axis gives B_n<=−2(n+1)²q_n. These are one-sided limits already
defined by theorem13; no cardinal sign-stability assumption is added.
