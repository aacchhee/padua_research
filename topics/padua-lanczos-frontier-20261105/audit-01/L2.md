## 2. Reconstruction

### L2-01

1. **Forced baseline coefficients.** c_kl = α_kl√(d_l/d_p) and β_k = √(d_k/d_p).
2. **Monic derivative recursion.** w_k = A_k w_p − Σα_kl w_l + F_k + h_k, with h_k ∈ S_deg(k). Induction by column index, using A_k E_p ∈ S_deg(k), gives R_s q̇_k = R_s V_k/√d_k.
3. **Projector derivative.** Ṗ = ZQᵀ + QZᵀ.
4. **Frame derivative:** −Ṗ_{s+1}XQ_s + R_{s+1}XQ̇_s = R_{s+1}XZ_s − Z_{s+1}H_X,s.
   - Both moving-projector terms are present. The QZᵀ term vanishes because R_{s+1}XQ_s = 0.
   - The operator derivative is DQ_sᵀ, which preserves the norm.
   - Re-orthonormalizing the frame with T(0) = I leaves the derivative unchanged.
5. **Removing the infimum over rules.** For each rule and source the limit is the same, the source sphere is common, and the class is nonempty. No interchange of inf and limit is needed.
6. **Certificate endpoints.** All re-derived:
   - the impulse is rank one, with norm squared ℓ;
   - the Hilbert–Schmidt bound is H_s²;
   - the row bound U_s² follows from Cauchy–Schwarz on each node row;
   - the variational form (16) and the PSD form (17) hold.
7. **Exact trace.** H_s²/((N_n−1)M), from the covariance I/((N_n−1)M).
