## Exact claim and scope

The method application proposed here is only to the named D36x9 and
D65x9 exact finite inputs. The sign algebra below is stated for the
shared invariant-measure hypotheses to make its proof explicit; no
additional scan family is proposed or admitted by this record.

Consider the positive finite weighted monic model of
[P4-02](theory/p4-weighted-normal-quotient.md), with distinct nodes,
U(n), the fixed graded order x^(t-j)y^j, and any admissible parent choices
frozen from a full exact run, including its recorded ties. Suppose the
weighted node measure is invariant under the two independent coordinate
sign flips. Every original node, basis/Gram calculation, source scale,
source column and degree prefix is retained.

For each reflection e=(e_x,e_y) in {+1,-1}^2 and ordered column
k=(r_k,j_k), define

    e z=(e_x x,e_y y),   chi_k(e)=e_x^(r_k) e_y^(j_k).

Then the exact monic GS basis, selected creator coefficients and frozen
responses obey

    b_k(e z)=chi_k(e)b_k(z),                               (1)
    alpha_kl=0 if (r_k,j_k)!=(r_l,j_l) modulo2,             (2)
    g_kh(e z)=chi_k(e)chi_h(e)g_kh(z).                    (3)

Consequently every squared response and every original endpoint energy

    v_hi(s)=sigma_h^2 sum_(h<=k<N_s)g_kh(z_i)^2/d_k         (4)

is constant on the node's independent-sign orbit. One may compute (4)
at one representative of each exact pair (abs(x),abs(y)), and copy it
to **each original node in that orbit** before the unchanged endpoint
updates. This produces exactly the unreduced original rational values.
There is no node-weight factor in (4), as in P4-02.

This is a PROVED routine corollary about exact finite computation,
conditional for any implementation on its guards passing. It neither
identifies the finite capacity with an endpoint nor changes any finite
screen threshold, family, rule, source budget, or all-degree claim.

## Proof before implementation or experiment

Reflections preserve the exact weighted inner product. The monic GS
polynomial b_k is the unique polynomial with its prescribed leading
monomial, only earlier monomials otherwise, and orthogonality to every
earlier monomial. The polynomial chi_k(e)b_k(e z) has the same leading
coefficient1, the same earlier support and the same orthogonality, because
the earlier ordered monomial span is reflection invariant. Uniqueness
proves (1). Equivalently, every projection between different sign
characters is zero. This proves basis parity without changing the order.

For an admissible selected creator, a_k is x or y and its parent p(k)
has the corresponding exponent reduced by1. Therefore

    chi_k(e)=e_axis(k) chi_p(k)(e).

The raw product a_k b_p(k) has character chi_k. Its inner product with
b_l equals chi_k(e)chi_l(e) times itself after reflection. If the two
parity pairs differ, choose a sign flip giving product -1; this inner
product is zero. Dividing by positive d_l proves (2), for every chosen
parent. Source scales sigma_h and d_k are unchanged base-run constants.
Pivot rules need no transported re-selection: the original full run and
its actual selected coefficients are used, and its ties are retained.

Define the full unit-lower frozen recurrence matrix A(z) through degree n:

    A(z)_kk=1,
    A(z)_kl=alpha_kl-a_k(z)*1_(l=p(k)) for l<k.

This definition also covers the case that both terms occupy one entry.
Let S_e=diag(chi_k(e)). For an alpha term, (2) implies that multiplying
by chi_k chi_l leaves the term unchanged. For the parent term,
chi_k chi_p=e_axis(k), precisely the sign acquired by a_k(e z).
The diagonal stays1. Hence, entry by entry,

    A(e z)=S_e A(z) S_e,
    A(e z)^(-1)=S_e A(z)^(-1) S_e.                        (5)

The inverse entries in source columns h>=1 are the existing g_kh:
solving A(z)g_.h=e_h gives the frozen response recursion, including
g_hh=1 and zero earlier entries. The unperturbed start is still exact;
no source at column0 is introduced by defining the full matrix.
Equation(5) proves (3). Squaring removes both signs, proving (4)'s
orbit invariance for every h and completed prefix s containing h.
At a node fixed by one reflection, (3) simply forces a response with
odd character to vanish; axis nodes cause no exception.

For each prefix let the actual full-node normal diagonal be

    R_s,ii=1-w_i sum_(k<N_s)b_k(z_i)^2/d_k.

The unchanged original endpoint formulas are

    L_s^2=max_(h,i)R_s,ii v_hi(s),
    H_s^2=sum_i R_s,ii sum_h v_hi(s),
    Urow_s^2=max_i sum_h v_hi(s),
    upper_s^2=min(H_s^2,Urow_s^2).

Copying (4) to every original i produces the same summands and maxima
as an unreduced run, term for term. Use the actual full-node R_s,ii in
these formulas. Although (1) and equal reflected weights also imply
R_s,ii is orbit invariant, this method does not replace its full-node
construction or sums. It does not divide H by an orbit size, weight
a representative as one original node, or reduce the source domain.
In particular arbitrary sources at different reflected nodes remain
distinct input coordinates: the shortcut reuses only equal *squared
coefficient values* in the endpoint calculation.

5. Build exact orbit keys (abs(x),abs(y)), retaining original-node indices.
