  uniform positive weights, exactly as defined in P2-01.

  support; >2s directions on each of >s/2 circles prove polynomial rank.

  a vanishing polynomial. Full continuous rotation invariance is absent.

  the written general injectivity and support proofs.
- Kill criterion: fewer than 324 distinct points, rank failure through
  degree16, nonpositive weight or failure of the proposed support proof.
- Test discriminability: admission only; finite exact scans do not
  represent ν_μ without an independently proved exactness statement.
- Results: N1 finite-grid admission PROVED in P2-01, through degree17;
