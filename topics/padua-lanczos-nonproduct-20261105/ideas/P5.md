## IDEA-582 — unsaturated Padua capacity

Provisional alias: `IDEA?lanczos-nonproduct-codex-P5-01`.

Aliases: positive Chebyshev cubature capacity; finite moment-product
transfer; truncated Padua aggregate upper; explicit Chebyshev impulses.

- Question/scope: for positive weighted Padua order m and stage 1<=s<m,
  classify the aggregate spectral span capacity of BJ and mirror as bounded,
  polynomial or exponential. State s and m separately.
- Mechanism/hypotheses: derive the positive cubature moments locally; use
  inherited product-type recurrence structure, explicit Chebyshev impulse
  estimates and weighted normalized quotient/source scaling. Prove the
  finite measure is not affine-product independently of its low moments.
- Closest full entries: IDEA133/136/137,246–250,414/415; PD-T033/038/039,
  136/137/324. PD-T069/144 have different positivity-radius outputs.
- Separation criterion: an actual aggregate spectral-output upper on a
  genuinely non-affine-product family, with M>N_s and all sources included.
  A matching predecessor capacity theorem, missed source factor, or a
  parallelogram/product exception erases the distinction.
- Literature baseline/limits: repository product recurrence proofs only;
  classical Padua ingredients receive no novelty claim. No external theorem
  will be used without a fulltext read; a self-contained cubature proof is
  the preferred cheapest route.
- Applicable obstructions: full s=m saturation has zero normal space;
  signed Lebesgue weights are not these positive Chebyshev weights; raw
  growth supplies no normal lower; aggregate errors are not IEEE errors.
- Novelty verdict: variant with the stated output/domain distinction.
- Cheapest test and prospective kill: derive exactness, non-affine-product
  hull and normalized Chebyshev impulses; reject the application if any
  hypothesis fails. A polynomial upper disproves exponential BJ growth in
  this model. A lower growing with s is separately needed to reject bounded
  capacity. Check small exact algebraic degrees; no fitted rate is a proof.
- Discriminability: the upper controls every source, while a legal normal
  witness supplies a lower. Neither need compute the unknown exact norm.
- Actual result: not yet derived at this entry's creation.
- Disposition: cleared for the bounded analytic test.
- Canonical theory: to be written as p5-prefixed provisional PD-T records.
