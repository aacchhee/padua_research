## Independent reconstruction of the source

1. **Actual collisions are removed first.** The four special rows use
   the unchanged total finite primitive on all n samples. For each
   ordinary row, the two actual denominator collision bands, buffered by
   [-1,1], and both seam samples produce one fixed actual set D before any
   coefficient relaxation. `narrow12` certifies the full band width below
   one; integer extraction requires finite, signed-long-representable
   bounds. At most eight actual samples result. Their values use the
   actual ratio/sinc primitive, preserving cancellation at actual
   collisions. If D fills the row, the entire actual row is handled
   directly and no artificial vertex or epsilon is charged.

2. **The global vertex remains common across rows.** Full-phase Arb
   enclosures of `A=cos(alpha)+cos(beta)`, `p=sin(alpha)`, `q=sin(beta)`
   are clipped to their proved triangle ranges `[0,2]x[0,1]^2`. Extracted
   ARF endpoints become exact dyadic Arb points, with duplicates merged.
   Their Cartesian product has at most eight triples. The code keeps one
   accumulator for each triple through all successful ordinary rows.
   Only after the row loop does it take `max_z(acc_z/N+E_z)`. It never
   substitutes a rowwise vertex maximum. For any fixed phase, the sum of
   absolute affine functions of the same triple is convex, so this common
   box-vertex maximum is valid. Adding separately enclosed direct terms
   and summing different row interval enclosures may lose common phase
   dependence, but preserves an upper bound.

3. **The freezing allowance has the correct units and radius.** Let
   `db=bh-bl` in normalized pi units, `m=n+1`, and `v0=pi*(r+bmid)/m`.
   The code forms the full ball `A0*(1+cos(v0))+q0*sin(v0)`, chooses its
   exact dyadic midpoint projected to [-5,5], and sets rho to the upward
   bound of the absolute difference between the entire center ball and
   that point. It therefore retains the full transcendental/arithmetic
   error, including any projection shift. Since `L=A0+q0` bounds
   `sqrt(A0^2+q0^2)`, the implemented

       epsilon_r,z = L*pi*db/(2m) + rho_r,z
       E_z = (2/pi)*max_successful_rows(epsilon_r,z)*(1+log n)^2

   is an outward upper on C14's allowance. In particular `widthBeta`
   explicitly multiplies the normalized width by pi. The independent unit
   check separately reconstructs its phase-only part as
   `Lmax*db*(1+log n)^2/m` at 512 bits, where pi cancels. Center errors are
   not assumed zero on a point box. Rows handled entirely as actual terms
   or replaced by a common cap require no freezing charge.

4. **Fixed numerator roots and full phase balls survive reuse.** Every
   artificial row calls the accepted C12 `vertex12` with fixed exact
   `(ctilde,p0)`, not the correlated original signed row formula. Constant,
   zero, no-root and exact tangency cases are distinguished; the three
   complete angular root families supply closed direct samples and cuts
   even when a band contains no integer. Root/index widths are certified
   on the full balls. The artificial direct values use their own rational
   numerator and denominator. Signed cores use the full coefficients
   `(p0-ctilde/sin v)/4` and `(p0+ctilde/sin v)/4`.
   Although the wrap candidate is selected from a midpoint, both strict
   pole/wrap inequalities must hold on the entire supplied offset ball.
   Root, sign, wrap, finite-value or backend uncertainty throws to the
   shared row fallback. No unknown comparison is accepted as a valid core.

5. **C11 tails are propagated, not counted twice.** The explicitly pinned
   backend is P=160, CP=640, J=16, K=32, LH=16, WD=96. Its complete partial
   balls include the J16 smooth tail, harmonic and w-prime tails, moment
   arithmetic and input-radius charge. `vertex12` multiplies and sums those
   full balls using both coefficient balls and the signed core. C15 keeps
   the full staged row ball and complete accumulator. Its reported tail
   diagnostic is not added again to U. The coefficient-cube diagnostic is
   likewise separate; E_z is included once. A maximum over eight complete
   vertex sums does not require summing eight copies of the same uniform
   tail budget.

6. **Whole-row rollback preserves convexity.** Actual buffered values,
   all artificial vertex rows, epsilon and rho updates are staged. The
   commit occurs only after every shared vertex succeeds. Any exception
   discards the entire actual row from every vertex accumulator and from
   the error updates, then adds the actual row cap `n*N` once to common.
   Previously evaluated buffered terms are not committed separately. The
   result is the same convex construction on the subset of successful
   rows plus common actual-row caps. No failed vertex is dropped, and no
   artificial row is capped using the bound for the actual polynomial.
   Statistics may count work performed before a failed attempt, which is
   appropriate for measuring work and does not affect numerical sums.

7. **Caps, operation count and scope are consistent.** All row/global
   caps use Arb products; the n guard precedes row/index construction.
   The native interval permits `2m<=8194` and bounded signed-long index
   arithmetic. The conservative count is `4n` retained actual evaluations,
   at most eight actual direct evaluations and eight times three
   artificial direct evaluations per ordinary row, and at most
   `8*24=192` partial calls per ordinary row. Failed bounded attempts do
   not trigger a q-sized direct sum or an unbounded retry. This is O(n)
   scalar work at fixed precision/order, not O(n) bit complexity or a
   demonstrated practical large-n speed result. The final `min(U,2N)` is
   a valid global actual-polynomial cap. The two isolated triangle
   vertices use the exact S=1 identity; all other calls remain guarded.

8. **The comparison claims stay properly limited.** C14's exact-real
   `S<=G<=M<=G+2 max E` does not bound executable `U-sup S`: interval
   dependence, arithmetic, tails and fallback also matter. The common
   coefficient cube still has O(width times H_n squared) relaxation,
   separate from the thin beta-freezing term. Numerical dominance over
   C12 is not implied. Even an exact row-majorant comparison needs matching
   boxes, direct sets and common fallback treatment; a clipped actual-row
   executable cap need not order an artificial model. No total O(width/n)
   loss or Padua-specific first-order cancellation is claimed.
