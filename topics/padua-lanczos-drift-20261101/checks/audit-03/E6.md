For independent uniform points X,Y on a planar domain D of area A and
Z=(X+Y)/2, change of variables gives

    f_Z(z) = (4/A²) area(D ∩ (2z−D)).

For the native square, each coordinate has density `1−|z_i|` on [-1,1],
independently, so the joint density is `(1−|z_1|)(1−|z_2|)`; rotation only
rotates this density. For the stated triangle A=2, so the coefficient is
one. Its intersection vertices are piecewise affine in z and the shoelace
area is piecewise quadratic. At z=(-1+t,-1+t), 0<t≤1/2, the intersection
is the square [-1,-1+2t]²: its extra sum inequalities are inactive, giving
exactly 4t². Fubini gives integral A²/4 for the overlap area, hence density
normalization one. The midpoint retains the mean and halves the covariance,
supporting the qualified concentration statement. These are analytic
reconstructions; the separate Fraction polygon clipper corroborates the
4t² example at t=1/16,1/8,1/4,1/3,1/2. Deterministic T24/Q24 inputs have no
iid probability law. No histogram inference is used.

IDEA57's quantifier is universal over targets. One accurate `exp(x+y)` fit
is therefore sufficient for its stated falsifier even though the ridge
alignment makes that target a weak probe of general smooth-function loss.

about all smooth functions, unseen targets or other nodes follows.
