## IDEA-256 — Parent-repair and reference-basis rules against the BJ span drift

Aliases: parent reorthogonalization against an extended-precision basis; reference-basis injection; PD-T055's “cheapest discriminating experiment”; rules outside the projection-only class of PD-N062; computed-prefix parent reorthogonalization (R17a); reference-prefix parent projection (R17b).

- Question / scope: does a rule *outside* the class R of PD-N062 remove the multi-degree BJ span drift on non-product measures, with vectors stored in double? The rule repairs the parent before its product is formed. The fixed-degree question is settled by the exact remarks below, so only the multi-degree drift remains.
- Mechanism and hypotheses. The repair is defined here, in response-02 (audit-02 A02-L2); nothing is run.
  - *Notation.* The run is BJ with full CGS2 against all previous columns and double storage. Column k has graded index k and degree t+1; its parent has index p=p(k) and degree t, and B_k is its operator. The stored double columns are q̂_0, q̂_1, …. The computed space Ŝ_t=span{q̂_0,…,q̂_(N_t−1)} contains q̂_p. E_(≤p) and E_(<p) are the exact spans of the node vectors of the monomials with graded index ≤p and <p; thus E_(≤N_t−1)=S_t, and the exact column q_p is the unit vector of E_(≤p)⊖E_(<p) with positive leading coefficient.
  - *Excluded readings.* PD-T055's phrase “reorthogonalizing the parent column against an extended-precision orthonormal basis” has three degenerate readings. (D0) Subtracting from q̂_p its projection onto Ŝ_t, or onto any computed span that contains q̂_p, gives zero in exact arithmetic, whatever the precision of the basis; keeping that projection returns q̂_p. The previous text of this entry (“…of the computed or the exact previous space”) admitted this reading. (D1) Subtracting its projection onto the exact S_t leaves (I−P_t)q̂_p, the drift component alone, and discards the intended component. (D2) One can replace q̂_p by the reference column r_p of R17b. Recomputing it recursively from its ancestors gives the same result in exact arithmetic. The recursion runs down to the exact constant column, and each product is orthogonalized against a reference basis of the exact prefix that precedes it. By admissibility, a product of ±r_(p(p)) lies in E_(≤p) with a nonzero coefficient on monomial p, so each step returns ±r_p. (Recomputing from the stored, drifted grandparent instead keeps that grandparent's drift.) D2 is reference-column injection, i.e. computing from the reference basis itself. It may serve as a control but is not a parent repair.
  - **R17a: computed-prefix reorthogonalization; no outside information.** Its reference columns are the stored columns q̂_0,…,q̂_(p−1) that strictly precede the parent's graded index. They exclude q̂_p, the later degree-t columns and every degree-(t+1) column. The rule:
    1. Convert these columns and q̂_p to extended precision (e.g. mpmath, 50 digits).
    2. Form an orthonormal basis U_(<p) of their span by two-pass Gram–Schmidt; it is nested in p, so it is built incrementally.
    3. Set q̃_p=(I−U_(<p)U_(<p)^T)q̂_p/||·||, round it to double, and form B_kq̃_p in place of B_kq̂_p.

    The stored q̂_p, the coefficients and the windows are unchanged. The repair is nonzero exactly when q̂_0,…,q̂_p are independent; the run records the smallest residual norm over all parents. No information from outside the stored columns enters, since q̃_p∈span{q̂_0,…,q̂_p}⊆Ŝ_t. By PD-T055's heuristic, R17a changes Ŝ_(t+1) only through B_k-defects of the computed spaces and rounding. R17a is the *in-span control*: it tests PD-T055's first-order rule-independence for an in-span change of parent gauge, not H17.
  - **R17b: reference-prefix projection; the one place where outside information enters.** Before the run, compute an orthonormal basis r_0,…,r_(N_n−1) of S_n in extended precision (e.g. mpmath, 50 digits).
    - *Construction.* Use two-pass Householder QR of the weighted evaluation matrix Φ of a graded, well-conditioned polynomial basis φ_i. For example take φ = T_(t−j)(x)T_j(y), the product Chebyshev polynomials on the bounding box, in graded order. Then span{φ_0,…,φ_p}=E_(≤p), so the basis is nested: span{r_0,…,r_p}=E_(≤p) for every p. Choose signs so that each r_p has a positive coefficient on φ_p.
    - *Recorded quality.* Record ||R^TR−I|| and u_ext·κ(Φ).
    - *Rule.* The reference columns are r_0,…,r_p, i.e. R_(≤p)=[r_0,…,r_p], through and including the parent's index. Set q̃_p=R_(≤p)R_(≤p)^Tq̂_p/||·||, computed in extended precision and rounded to double. Use it only as the multiplicand of B_k; the stored vectors, the coefficients and the windows are unchanged.
    - *Nonzero, with the intended leading component.* γ_p=⟨q̂_p,r_p⟩ is the parent's component along the exact column of its own index (r_p=q_p up to the reference error). The rule requires |γ_p|≥1/2 at every parent; otherwise the run is inconclusive. Then q̃_p≠0, and B_kq̃_p∈B_kE_(≤p)⊆E_(≤k) has a nonzero coefficient on monomial k, by admissibility. So the repaired product keeps its intended leading monomial.
    - *Where information enters.* Only through R_(≤p), which is computed from the nodes, the weights and φ, independently of the recurrence. The projection removes from the multiplicand the parent's drift outside S_t and its same-degree components along r_l, l>p. It keeps the parent's components along r_0,…,r_(p−1). After rounding to double the multiplicand again carries an O(u) error outside E_(≤p), but no inherited drift.
    - *Effect on the span (heuristic for the run, from PD-T055's span identity).* Step k adds span{B_kq̃_p−f_k} to Ŝ_(<k), and B_kq̃_p lies in S_(t+1) up to the reference error and the O(u) rounding of q̃_p. So B_k no longer multiplies the parent's drift; that is the B_k-defect term of the heuristic. The drift already in Ŝ_(<k) is carried forward, because the spans stay nested. R17b thus isolates the product-step amplification channel. It is a diagnostic experiment, not a proposed algorithm: a rule that has the reference basis can use it directly.
  - *Fixed-degree remarks (exact, routine).* In PD-N062's exact-prefix setting, R17a and R17b return every exact parent unchanged, since q_p⊥E_(<p) and q_p∈E_(≤p). So the rule stays in R and that lemma applies; no parent repair touches a raw-product source. Projecting the sourced input onto S_(t+1) gives P_(V_m)(v_m+f)+(1+α)w, and hence the exact S_(t+1) when 1+α≠0. That is reference injection at the product (record 15, Open items).
