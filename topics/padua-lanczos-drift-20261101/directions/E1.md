## Drift and input/output norm

S_s is the exact polynomial evaluation space, P_s its Euclidean orthogonal projector; P_hat_s is the projector onto the computed/perturbed span of the same dimension. The drift is

    theta_s=||(I-P_s)P_hat_s||_2,

the sine of the largest principal angle. This is theta_s itself, not theta_LB(n), which bounds the maximum over stages.

Use the rational monic orthogonal gauge b_0=1, b_k=A_k b_p-sum_(l<k)alpha_kl b_l, d_k=||b_k||^2. Inject epsilon f_k immediately before exact full projection. Define sigma_k^2=||A_k b_p||^2 and

    ||f||_src^2=sum_k ||f_k||_2^2/sigma_k^2.

The span condition kappa_s is the operator norm from this Hilbert source norm to the **spectral** norm of the normal first-order graph. The precise map and proof are [PD-T?drift-codex-01](../../../tracks/bivariate-lanczos/theory/drift-codex-e1-01-span-derivative.md).     Knode^2=max_(h,i) sigma_h^2(1-P_s,ii)sum_k g_kh(z_i)^2/d_k,
    KHS^2=sum_(h,i) sigma_h^2(1-P_s,ii)sum_k g_kh(z_i)^2/d_k,
