The first theorem uses exact full projection in the zero-error branch of an upper-bound perturbation model. Exact vectors in that branch are a model relaxation; they are not asserted to satisfy literal working-precision storage in PD-T055. The literal old wording has an unfixed quantifier and cannot simply be relabeled false. Its proposed pathwise interpretation is refuted; a worst-case-over-errors interpretation survives that test.

The second theorem formalizes “information never formed” at the vector-operation level. Parents and BJ products are fixed, stored earlier vectors are not revised, and a step can subtract any finite linear combination of prior vectors and rescale. Thus it permits arbitrary coefficients and any number of reorthogonalization passes within the affected degree. It excludes extra products, restarting, reference-basis injection and parent repairs. A noiseless degree-one prefix is required; a theorem covering rules that intentionally change that gauge is not claimed.

For the ten-point non-product triangle T_3 and a single source εe in Yq_x, the completed degree-two span is exactly

  V⊕span{w+εe},       S_2=V⊕span{w},       ||w||²=28/115,

where dim V=5, e⊥S_2 and ||e||=1. These spans are independent of all the allowed projection coefficients. Hence θ_2=|ε|/sqrt(28/115+ε²), and the unit degree-one vector q_y gives a nonzero missing J-product residual. A relative source ε=u sqrt(3/2), 0<u≤1/4, yields θ_2≥2u and δ_J(1)≥u, even with exact full reorthogonalization. The proof supplies all ten rational evaluation rows and needs no uncertified numerical norm.

This is a robust obstruction to exact closure or o(u) error, not to ordinary O(u) accuracy. It does not prove the growth seen on T_24 or rotated Q_24. A closure residual can also have a much smaller projection into the computed span, so no large implicit-section error is inferred without a separate argument.

Candidate history, the failed stronger route and its resumption conditions are preserved in [ideas-e3-e4.md](../ideas-e3-e4.md). The cheapest next step toward a substantive universal lower bound is a multi-degree quotient invariant that survives every permitted change of earlier basis, with a controlled χ and a realizable error source. The clamped recursion alone still supplies no lower bound.
