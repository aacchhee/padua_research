**The zero-error branch.** It uses exact full projection in an upper-bound perturbation model. Exact vectors in that branch are a model relaxation; they are not asserted to satisfy literal working-precision storage in PD-T055. The literal old wording has an unfixed quantifier and cannot simply be relabeled false. The pathwise reading refuted here was introduced by this campaign. It is false for the one-line reason that the zero error is admissible, and the exactness of the implicit section in exact arithmetic is already PD-T032 part 2. Hence it is an IDEA disposition and a proposed quantifier note for PD-T055, not a PD-N. A worst-case-over-errors reading survives this test.

**The fixed-degree lemma.** It formalizes, at the vector-operation level, what fixed parents and fixed products imply. Earlier vectors are not revised, and a step can subtract any finite linear combination of prior vectors and rescale. Thus the lemma permits arbitrary coefficients and any number of reorthogonalization passes within the affected degree. It excludes extra products, restarting, reference-basis injection and parent repairs. An exact prefix through degree t is required; a theorem covering rules that change that gauge is not claimed.

Its span identity is the exact, fixed-prefix instance of **PD-T055's own recorded heuristic**, span Q̂_(≤k)=span(Q̂_(<k), B_kq̂_(p(k))−f_k). With the prefix fixed, the heuristic's parent-change term vanishes, so the identity holds for all coefficients without a first-order caveat. The new content is the closed form: θ=||f_⊥||/sqrt((1+α)²b²+||f_⊥||²) and δ_A=κ_Aθ with κ_(A_m)≥b, plus the explicit constants below.

**T_3/BJ instance.** On the ten-point triangle T_3, put a single source εe in the formed product v_21=Yq_x. The completed degree-two span is exactly

  V⊕span{w+εe},       S_2=V⊕span{w},       ||w||²=28/115,

where dim V=5, e⊥S_2 and ||e||=1. These spans are independent of all the allowed projection coefficients, and θ_2=|ε|/sqrt(28/115+ε²). The defects are

  δ_X(1)=(2/sqrt(3))bθ_2   (the earlier bound (8), now an equality),
  δ_Y(1)=bθ_2=(sqrt(3)/2)δ_X(1).

So the formed operator Y carries a defect of the same order. A relative source ε=u sqrt(3/2), 0<u≤1/4, yields θ_2≥2u, δ_X(1)≥u and δ_Y(1)≥u, even with exact full reorthogonalization. The proof supplies all ten rational evaluation rows and needs no uncertified numerical norm.

**Not a J-specific or non-product phenomenon.** The defect lands on the operator that formed the perturbed column, and on any other operator whose image of S_t meets that column modulo the other columns. Exact checks (audit-01 run 1; [response-01-e4-closure.json](../evidence/response-01-e4-closure.json)) instantiate this:

- product grid Q_3: δ_X=δ_Y≠0;
- sources in the formed X- or Y-columns of T_3: defects in X only or in Y only;
- the mirror selection gives the same values;
- degree 2→3 on T_4 with three selections and non-uniform weights also instantiate it.

Hence this lemma does not explain the measured J/F asymmetry of PD-N013(b), where F-defects stay ≤1.5e−15 while J-defects grow across degrees.

**Relative-source limit.** For a unit normal source of relative size u||v_m||, δ_A(t)/u=κ_A||v_m||/sqrt(b²+u²||v_m||²)→(κ_A/b)||v_m||. For the forming operator this limit is ≥||v_m||, with equality only when κ_(A_m)=b. In the T_3 instance above (source in v_21) κ_Y=b. With the source in v_20 instead, κ_X²/b²=4/3 and the squared limit is 88/15, not ||v_20||²=22/5. Response-01 had asserted the limit ||v_m|| in general (audit-02 A02-M1). The exact regression is [response-02-e4-creator-limit.json](../evidence/response-02-e4-creator-limit.json).

**Limits.** This is a robust obstruction to exact closure or o(u) error, not to ordinary O(u) accuracy. It does not prove the growth seen on T_24 or rotated Q_24. A closure residual can also have a much smaller projection into the computed span, so no large implicit-section error is inferred without a separate argument.

**Next step.** Candidate history, the failed stronger route and its resumption conditions are preserved in [ideas-e3-e4.md](../ideas-e3-e4.md). The cheapest next step toward a substantive universal lower bound is a multi-degree quotient invariant that survives every permitted change of earlier basis, with a controlled χ and a realizable error source (IDEA?drift-codex-16). The clamped recursion alone still supplies no lower bound. The cheapest discriminating experiment *outside* the fixed-degree class is PD-T055's extended-precision parent repair, retained as deferred IDEA?drift-claude-17. Its literal reading against the computed space that contains the parent gives zero. IDEA?drift-claude-17 therefore fixes two arms. R17a reorthogonalizes against the stored columns strictly before the parent's index; it supplies no outside information. R17b projects onto an extended-precision nested reference basis through the parent's index, and that reference is where outside information enters. It runs nothing.
