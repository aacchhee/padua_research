## Priority wording for the paper

**1-D CC MZ constants (PD-T013–028, PD-N004).** Keep the merged wording of mz-prior-art report.md §"What to tell ASV" and comparison.md §"Answer to the priority question", with two additions. (i) Cite De Marchi–Kroó 2018 next to Mastroianni–Totik: explicit univariate CGL constants exist only in a heavily oversampled window (n < m/36 at p = 2), which lies inside half-exactness; no explicit beyond-half-exactness constant was found. (ii) Cite Wu, Appl. Math. Lett. 182 (2026) as an abstract consumer of η: our explicit η(θ) can be inserted there, and our endpoint order shows that η → 1 at the CC endpoint degree. König–Nielsen stays an analogous same-weight borderline (critical exponent depends on max(α,β)); cite the arXiv locators and note that the journal body was not compared. Suggested sentence: "To our knowledge, and within the search documented in [repo], no published result gives the two-sided order (1+log N)^{−2} for the CC Gram minimum on Π_{N−1}, or explicit MZ constants for CC nodes beyond half-exactness; qualitative failure on Π_N at CC nodes follows from Mastroianni–Milovanović–Notarangelo (2012)."

**2-D signed Padua (PD-T106–117, PD-N044–053).** Attribute the signed rule itself to Sommariva–Vianello–Zanovello 2008 ((33), (36), journal pp. 419–420), including its degree-m exactness (the limit Σ|w| → 4 is asserted there for these weights without a separate proof), and note in a footnote that their eq. (25) misprints the weight denominator ((n+2)(n+3) for (n+1)(n+2)) in both preprint and journal. Attribute the existence of negative weights to SVZ (p. 421, Table 1); claim as ours the exact corner formula, negativity for every m ≥ 3 and its asymptotics. Attribute the positive-rule checkerboard resonance to Bos et al. 2006 and Dencker–Erb 2017. State that the only located published MZ inequality at Padua/Lissajous nodes (KLP 2018 Lemma 5.1, from Erb 2016) concerns the positive Chebyshev rule in Chebyshev measure with unspecified constants, and that the signed Lebesgue-measure targets (λ_+ < 2, fixed-ratio η(θ) < 1, endpoint order, uniform C→L² norm) remain OPEN here and unmatched in the documented search. Do not cite the An–Ran–Wu survey as saying that Padua hyperinterpolation is open in our sense.
