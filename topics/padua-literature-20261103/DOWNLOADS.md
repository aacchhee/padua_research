## Notes and corrections found while verifying

- Brutman's 1997 survey is Ann. Numer. Math. 4 (1997) 111–127. The "J. Inequal. Appl." survey is Ibrahimoglu 2016. McCabe–Phillips is in BIT, not SIAM J. Numer. Anal.
- arXiv 2009.10803 is Hokanson's stabilized Sanathanan–Koerner (rational approximation) paper, with V+A in its §2. "Vandermonde with Arnoldi" itself is Brubeck–Nakatsukasa–Trefethen (arXiv 1911.09988).
- The ASV journal data are Math. Comput. Simul. vol. 252 (issue dated 2027-02), pp. 733–745.
- "Powell 1965" (Chebyshev maximum at ±1, as cited in Białas-Cież et al.) is not identified. The secondary surveys credit Ehlich–Zeller 1966 (Part B).
