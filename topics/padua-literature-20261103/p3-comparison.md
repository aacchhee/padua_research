## Priority wording for the note (per result group)

**A. Paige-type local theory in 2D (PD-T029–T034, PD-T031).** "For a single symmetric operator, Paige's loss-of-orthogonality relation [Paige 1980, (3.13)] and its block form [Sinha 2026, Thm 7] are known. Our identity for one operator, with real symmetric data, normalised blocks and no injected starts, reduces at an exact Ritz pair to the quantity bounded there. What is new is the bilinear complex-symmetric form for N = X + iY with a start injected at every degree, and the commutator source term in the injecting-operator specialisation." Cite Chen 2026 (v2, Thm 4.1) for a short 1D proof. Do not say "first Paige identity for blocks".

**B. Backward interpretation (PD-T056–T060, PD-T069/N303, PD-T144, PD-T145).** "In one variable, finite-precision Lanczos admits a Greenbaum-type backward model. Greenbaum's Theorem 1′ gives interval widths σ(N + m)³·O(J²γ√ε‖A‖), or O(c_max^{3/2}Jε^{1/4}‖A‖) when unconverged Ritz values are not well separated [Greenbaum 1989, p. 51]. Chen 2026 (v2, Thm 5.7) gives a model I_{k+1} ⊗ A + Δ with ‖Δ‖ ≲_k ε‖A‖. Paige 2010 gives an augmented, non-standard model. For block Lanczos, Šimonová–Tichý give a continuation process and sufficient conditions, and leave the key bounding step open. We know of no backward analysis for Lanczos-type recurrences driven by two commuting operators from one start vector." Then present PD-T058/T059 as a reduction and rate theorem for exact representation of consistent perturbed sections (reading (E)), and PD-T069 as an obstruction in that reading. Say explicitly that reading (G) (actual runs) is open, and that the 1D O(ε) results concern actual runs, not reading (E). Do not attribute "√ε" or "ε^{1/4}" to Greenbaum without naming the branch.

**C. Span drift and V+A (PD-T053, PD-T136–T143, PD-T146, PD-N?…-201).** "Multivariate V+A goes back to Austin et al. (CPC 2021; arXiv 1912.02272), with the total-degree ordering and smallest-index parent rule of Hokanson (arXiv 2009.10803, Alg. 2.1) and the sample-complexity analysis of Zhu–Nakatsukasa (IMA JNA 2026). The published rounding statements concern the orthogonality of the computed basis: two-pass CGS gives ‖Q*Q − I‖ = O(MN^{3/2}u) [ZN, Rem. 5.1(5)], which feeds fit-stability results. To our knowledge no published result addresses whether the computed 2D span equals the graded polynomial space. The claim that V+A polynomials are unaffected by Vandermonde ill-conditioning [ZN §5.2] is supported there by one-variable comparisons." Keep PD-T146's own caveat: the finding concerns particular polynomial directions, not general failure on smooth data. Cite Paige 2010 as the 1D augmented-Krylov counterpart, which gives no span-distance bound.

**D. Structure (PD-T057, PD-T076).** For PD-T057: "The commuting conditions and a Favard-type converse for full orthonormal sequences are known (Xu 1993, 1994; Dunkl–Xu, Thm 3.5.1, as cited by Liu–Narayan). We state and prove the truncated version on Π_{2n+1}, with the last block free, the words formula and the manifold structure, for self-containedness." The novelty sentence must stay limited to the truncated form until Dunkl–Xu 3.5.1 and Xu TAMS 343 are read. For PD-T076: "It is remarked in [ZN §3; Hokanson §2] that the multivariate monomial basis is not a Krylov sequence; we prove a precise version for operators commuting with X, Y and band Krylov sequences with a fixed number of starts."
