- Statements, paraphrased:
  - Thm 3.3.7 (p. 74) and Thm 3.3.8 (pp. 75–76): Favard-type equivalences for the three-term relation with rank conditions, in the quasi-definite and the positive-definite (orthonormal) cases. Proved by induction on the degree.
  - Thm 3.4.1 (pp. 82–83): the commuting conditions (3.4.2) follow from orthonormality.
  - Thm 3.5.1 (p. 88; proof pp. 92–93): polynomials generated by the recursion formula (3.3.10) are orthonormal for a positive-definite functional iff the B_{k,i} are symmetric, the A_{k,i} satisfy the rank condition, and together they satisfy (3.4.2).
    - The text stresses that in several variables the recursion is weaker than the three-term relation without the commuting conditions.
    - The proof proceeds degree by degree: conditions up to level k give the three-term relation up to level k+1.
  - §3.7 (p. 104): truncated block Jacobi matrices J_{n,i}. §3.9 (p. 113): notes. Joint-eigenvalue results credited to Xu 1993a/1994b. Möller's bound (3.9.1), N ≥ dim Π²_{n−1} + ½ rank(A_{n−1,1}A_{n−1,2}ᵀ − A_{n−1,2}A_{n−1,1}ᵀ), for a cubature formula of degree 2n−1 in two variables (dimension term: bivariate polynomials of degree ≤ n−1; corrected 2026-11-05 per audit-01 F4 from the rendered page image of p. 113; the 2026-11-03 text-layer reading had flattened the superscript 2 into the subscript), is stated in matrix notation.
