- Statements, paraphrased, with journal pages (journal page = PDF page + 192):
  - Thm 2.1 (p. 195): Favard-type equivalence. A polynomial sequence is orthogonal for a quasi-definite functional on all of Π^d iff it satisfies a three-term relation with the rank conditions.
  - Thm 2.2 (pp. 195–196): the orthonormal version, with symmetric recurrence (C_{k,i} = A_{k−1,i}ᵀ). The proof is by induction on the degree.
  - Cor. 2.3 (p. 196): the commuting conditions (2.4)–(2.6) on the A_{k,i} and B_{k,i}, obtained as consequences.
  - Thm 3.1 (p. 197): a determinate compactly supported measure exists iff the coefficient matrices are uniformly bounded.
  - Thm 4.1 (p. 198): a commuting bounded self-adjoint family with a cyclic vector is unitarily equivalent to multiplication operators on L²(μ), with μ(B) = ⟨E(B)Φ₀, Φ₀⟩.
- Relation to PD-T057: these results concern infinite sequences, with a functional on all of Π^d. Thm 4.1's spectral representation is the infinite-dimensional analogue of our words formula L(x^a y^b) = e₀ᵀ w(H_x, H_y) e₀. No truncated (Π_{2n+1}) statement was found.
