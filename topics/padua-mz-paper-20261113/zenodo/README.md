Both packs were staged at commit `90860ecdc12d9887f7d31c3b0440d3b14e8d6169`. They carry the **licence GNU AGPL version 3 or later** (SPDX `AGPL-3.0-or-later`; AC, 2026-11-14), as does the repository. The MZ-CC pack omits SIAM's `siamart251216.cls` and `siamplain.bst`, because SIAM's terms forbid distributing them without the full macro package. Each pack contains:
- `repository/`: a `git archive` of the directories that the paper names, plus every file those programs read or hash;
- `data/`: the files that are not in Git;
- `verify.sh`, `tools/compare_receipts.py`, `requirements.txt`, `LICENSE`;
- `README.md` (from [README-mz-cc.md](README-mz-cc.md) and [README-mz-sp.md](README-mz-sp.md));
- `VERIFICATION-2026-11-14.log`;
- `MANIFEST-SHA256.txt`.

The zips are deterministic, with sorted members and fixed timestamps and modes.

## What is outside Git, and where it came from

**MZ-CC.**
- `data/table1/reproduction.json`: regenerated by `reproduce.py --suite all --max-N 1280`. It agrees in every number with the historical output (Git blob at `2b52b432`, also included).
- `data/protocol-history/attempts-P4-d03e74dc.md`: the preregistered protocol that the P4 receipt hashes. It comes from PR31's branch history; the repository copy was later extended.

**MZ-SP.**
- The PD-T376 witness arrays and exact norm files at m = 1101, 1240 and 1600.
  - They were regenerated from the frozen commit `39479ff` with the pinned stack.
  - The source vectors and the decompressed payloads reproduce the pinned SHA-256 exactly. Receipts: [../evidence/pd-t376-regeneration/](../evidence/pd-t376-regeneration/).
- The source vectors themselves.
- Measurement item (1): the `padua` and `marginal` suites, regenerated.
- The two protocol versions (`c1e6e59d`) that the P1 receipts hash.

Not included, because neither paper uses them: the positive controls at m = 60 and 400, the raw scan logs of the cleanup campaign, and the P2 raw outputs. The P2 raw outputs were never retained, and their commands are in the committed receipts.
