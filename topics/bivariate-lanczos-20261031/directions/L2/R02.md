## 4. Reading

The reorthogonalization question of L2 splits into two independent problems: (i) **orthogonality** of the computed vectors — solved cheaply and measurably by the two-pass window plus ω-triggered partial reorthogonalization with exact J-rows (PRO-2D-J), with the F-section accurate as Theorem A predicts; (ii) **the spaces** — not touched by any reorthogonalization (PD-T?…-202/-205), fixed only by the relation choice: the adaptive one-relation rule repairs it on five of six non-product sets, the two-relation completion on all six but breaks the clustered product-type checkerboard. No single scheme tested is safe on every benchmark; a hybrid (two relations on isotropic supports, one relation where the measure is product-type) is the evident next candidate, but no criterion that decides between them a priori is known.

## 5. Failed routes
