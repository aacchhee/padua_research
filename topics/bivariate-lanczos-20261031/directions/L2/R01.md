## 4. Main reading

Three channels separate cleanly on the benchmarks: (1) a **local** channel — the one-pass CGS window cascade (triangle: loss = local level at every degree) — removed by a two-pass window; (2) a **propagated** channel — tracked by the ω-estimate and removable by ω-triggered partial reorthogonalization, after which the F-section is O(u)-accurate; (3) a **commutator / space** channel — the J-closure of the computed spaces fails, driven by the clamped shadow recursion of PD-T?…-202, untouched by any reorthogonalization: with the BJ selection the orthonormal basis produced by any reorthogonalization scheme (including the O(MN²) CGS2 scheme) spans spaces that drift from S_n exponentially on non-product measures. Consequence for L2: a reorthogonalization strategy can be provably useful only together with a selection rule whose shadow recursion is benign. Reconstruction 01's finding (selection rule changes loss by 10⁴–10⁸) is the natural next test, now with the drift/J-defect criteria.
