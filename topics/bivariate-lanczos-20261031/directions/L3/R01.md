## 3. Precise formulation (what the data determine)

- **Data ↔ functional (PROVED, PD-T?…-301).** A structured pair (block tridiagonal, echelon off-diagonal blocks with positive pivots) satisfying the *interior commutativity conditions* — [H_x, H_y] = 0 outside the last diagonal block (n,n) — is the degree-n section pair of exactly one normalized functional L on Π_{2n+1} with M_n(L) ≻ 0; inverse by the words formula L(x^a y^b) = e_0ᵀ w(H_x,H_y) e_0. So degree-n sections determine moments up to degree 2n+1 (orchestrator route (i) confirmed) and nothing more; the last-block commutator κ is the free part (route (iii) made exact).
- **Partly computed H_y (PROVED count, PD-T?…-301(d)).** The recurrence forms H_x fully and only columns (k,0) of H_y. These entries determine L and hence all of H_y, but they are overdetermined by e_n = 1, 6, 17, 36, 65, 106, … (n = 1..6) exact consistency relations — so in floating point "the completed H_y" and L̂ are defined only up to the consistency defect.
- **Three readings (PD-T?…-302).** (L) literal perturbative: computed sections within O(ε) of exact ones ⇒ μ′ = μ, δ = 0 works with constant 1 — trivial. (E) exact representation of consistent ε-perturbed data (residual < perturbation): ⟺ L̂ ∈ C(K_δ) ⟺ positive cubature of degree 2n+1 for L̂ with nodes in K_δ; all ε-perturbations representable ⟺ inradius r(δ) := min{L_μ(p) : p ≥ 0 on K_δ, ‖p‖ = 1} ≥ ε. (G) Greenbaum regime: sections far from exact (loss of orthogonality) but satisfying the local relations — OPEN.
