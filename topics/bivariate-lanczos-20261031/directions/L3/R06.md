## 1. Question

Reading (G) of L3-Q1 needs, before any support question, that the computed coefficients be (close to) consistent section data, i.e. near 𝒞_n (PD-T?…-301). Decide in which regimes this holds after loss of orthogonality, using a computable consistency diagnostic.

## 2. Lemma to be used (statement written before the tests; proof in theory file 314)

Let Ĥ_F be the N_n × N_n matrix of computed F-coefficients (columns of degree ≤ n, rows truncated to degree ≤ n) and Ĥ_J L1's implicit J-section (computed columns (t,0); other columns by the recursion of PD-T?…-104 part 2). Then, as an exact identity on the computed numbers, [Ĥ_F, Ĥ_J] e_c = 0 for every column c of degree ≤ n−1. Consequently the computed data are exactly the degree-n sections of a (unique, normalised) functional L̂ on Π_{2n+1} iff Ĥ_F and Ĥ_J are symmetric (given the structural pattern with positive pivots, which PD-T?…-102 guarantees for the exact window); the asymmetry ‖Ĥ_F − Ĥ_Fᵀ‖ + ‖Ĥ_J − Ĥ_Jᵀ‖ is therefore the consistency defect.

## 3. Kill criteria (written before the tests)

- K12 (sanity of the lemma): the computed [Ĥ_F, Ĥ_J] restricted to columns of degree ≤ n−1 must be ≤ 1e3·u·‖Ĥ‖² in every run (it is exactly zero up to the rounding of my own evaluation); otherwise the lemma or the implementation is wrong.
- K13 (hypothesis A, product-aligned supports): "asymmetry stays ≤ 1e3·u·‖Ĥ‖ while loss := max|Q̂ᵀQ̂ − I| ≤ 1e-3" — killed by one run (random square or tensor-type set) violating it.
- K14 (hypothesis B, non-product supports): "the consistency defect grows with the loss (asymmetry ≥ 1e-3·loss·‖Ĥ‖ once loss ≥ 1e-8)" — killed by one rotated-square run with loss ≥ 1e-6 and asymmetry ≤ 1e3·u·‖Ĥ‖ (that would mean consistency survives loss there and (G) reduces to (E) at L̂).
- Forward error ‖Ĥ − H_exact‖ (H_exact from a Householder QR of the weighted Chebyshev–Vandermonde matrix in the same graded order) is recorded to separate the perturbative regime (forward error ≈ loss) from a genuine Greenbaum regime.

## 3a. Added kill criterion (written at 23:29 AFTER the K12–K14 runs below, BEFORE the least-squares test; disclosed)

The K13/K14 runs show asym_J ≈ fwd_J ≈ (0.7–10)·loss on both supports, but on the random square asym_F ≪ fwd_F, so the asymmetry may be amplification inside the implicit J-completion rather than inconsistency of the computed numbers. - K15 (hypothesis C, "weight-only Greenbaum model fits much better than the exact measure"): ρ ≤ 0.1 at every n ≥ 18 with loss ≥ 1e-9 on the rotated square; killed if ρ > 0.5 at some such n.
