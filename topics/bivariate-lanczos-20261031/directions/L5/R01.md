## 3. Results

All MEASURED, scope = the runs listed.

**3.1 Slopes (theory file `givens-deficit-slopes-measured.md`, PD-T?bivariate-lanczos-claude-502).**

| family | Givens-scheme | HL | BJ | CGS2 | Householder |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Padua cubature, M = 66..2016 | 1.40 [1.09, 1.70] | 0.73 | 1.17 | 0.48 | 0.38 |
| Padua U(0.1,1) | 1.55 [1.15, 1.94] | 0.79 | 1.01 | 0.53 | 0.38 |
| Padua unit weights (M ≤ 861) | 1.08 [0.90, 1.25] | 0.77 | 0.65 | 0.52 | 0.38 |
| random, arcsine density | 1.28 [1.06, 1.50] | 0.71 | 1.12 | 0.38 | 0.28 |
| random, uniform density (confounded) | 2.87 [2.49, 3.26] | 2.28 | 2.76 | 1.57 | 2.11 |

Upper range M ≥ 496 (4 sizes): Givens-scheme 2.15 / 2.50 (Padua cubature / U), HL 0.75 / 0.80, CGS2 0.57 / 0.55. Padua structured orders only (given, sortx): Givens 0.93 [0.89, 0.98] (cubature), 0.92 (U); random orders only: 1.71 and 1.96.

**Kill test as written, family by family (`logs/analysis_r01.txt`, last block).** Not killed: Padua cubature, U(0.1,1) and unit weights, and the arcsine random family (Givens > 0.7, HL ≤ 0.81, CGS2 ≤ 0.57 on full and upper range). **Killed literally:** uniform-density random nodes (CGS2 1.57, HL 2.28 ≥ 0.9). That family is confounded: cond of the weighted Chebyshev–Vandermonde matrix grows ∝ M^2.96 and Householder itself has slope 2.11, so the slopes measure the problem's sensitivity, not rounding; the plan does not name the family, so I report both and call the explanation *not killed in the conditioning-controlled families* and *killed in the confounded one*. The Givens slope is consistent with ≈ 1 on structured orders but not 'exactly 1' on random orders (1.7–2.0); the HL slope (0.71–0.81) lies between ½ and 1, so 'HL ∝ √M' is not supported at this precision, only HL < 0.9.

**3.2 Prefix mechanism (theory files `prefix-unisolvence-order-statistics-measured.md` PD-T?…-503 and `largest-redundant-pivot-degradation.md` PD-N?…-501).** Catastrophe := error(largest) ≥ 1e3 × error(scheme) and ≥ 1e-10. 'All prefixes unisolvent' := no prefix with certified σ_min < 1e-11 (observed: every singular prefix has σ_min ≤ 1e-15; none in 1e-15..1e-11). Over the 72 (weights, n, order) cases with n ≥ 20 (2 weight types × 4 sizes × 9 orders):

| | non-unisolvent order | all prefixes unisolvent |
|---|---|---|
| catastrophe | 13 | 16 |
| no catastrophe | 5 | 38 |

**The kill test fires:** 16 catastrophes occur on orders all of whose prefixes are unisolvent, including gepp and rpiv1–3 at n = 50 (min prefix σ_min 4.7e-4..7.5e-3, ratios 3e3..3e4, 'largest' 9e-10..3e-9 against 'scheme' ≈ 1.1e-13). So 'the catastrophe occurs only on orders with non-unisolvent prefixes' is **false** in the scope. Of the 16, 14 are above the pre-registered tolerance σ_min ≥ 1e-8 (the other two are n = 50 rand3, σ_min 3.0e-9). The mechanism is graded, not binary: the 'largest' error grows roughly ×15–50 per Δn = 10 on *every* order, and is 3–10 orders of magnitude larger on non-unisolvent orders at the same n (median ratio at n = 40: gepp/rpiv 460, random 1.2e3, sortx 1.6e7, given 1.4e9); the reported 7e-4 at n = 20 with random weights is reproduced in kind on the `given` order with U(0.1,1) weights (1.4e-3, scheme 1.4e-14) and is absent for cubature weights (1.4e-14 vs 9.9e-15), and absent for the random/constructed orders at n = 20 (ratios 1.5–5). Also the converse is not simple: non-unisolvent streams at small n with cubature weights are harmless. Random nodes: 'largest' is not worse than 'scheme' for uniform nodes (M = 2016: 1.45e-12 vs 5.6e-11) but worse for arcsine nodes (slope 2.10 vs 1.28).

**3.3 Side finding (the 'scheme' rule is not order-independent at n ≥ 50 on random orders).** For random orders at n = 50 'scheme' errors are 1.5e-13..1.6e-12 (cub) and 3.1e-13..3.4e-12 (U) against 1.1e-13..1.3e-13 on gepp/rpiv; across the 40 all-unisolvent n ≥ 30 cases Spearman(log10 scheme error, log10 min prefix σ_min) = −0.51 (p = 9e-4), −0.71 at n = 50 alone. Diagnosis of the REPRO n = 60 case is in R03.md.

Failed or killed routes: (F1) 'the catastrophe is a property of non-unisolvent prefixes' (killed); (F2) 'slope ratio 2:1 between Givens and HL' — not demonstrated at the required precision (Givens ≈ 1.1–1.6, HL ≈ 0.7–0.8); (F3) the uniform-random-node family as a controlled test — rejected as confounded; (F4) mpmath cross-check of the prefix tolerance — replaced by exhaustive SVD validation (0 classification mismatches on 18 order/size cases at n = 6, 10, 14) and the observed clean gap, disclosed deviation.
