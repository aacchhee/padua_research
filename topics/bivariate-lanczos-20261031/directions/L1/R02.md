- (iv) **The quantity that governs loss in both regimes is the per-node propagator of the computed recurrence.** Its response to iid rounding-size forcing, P(n), tracks the measured loss within 1–5× on random squares (where it also equals u/ρ_Z), tensor and Padua (flat), and on the rotated squares and the disk (where u/ρ_Z misses by 10³–10⁸); it underestimates on the triangle beyond n = 12 (`logs/r02_node_propagator.log`). The growth on non-product supports is amplification by the recurrence, not growth of a source: errors committed only in F-products or only in J-products are amplified at the same rate (`logs/r02_mixed_precision.log`), and in the ω-recurrence the sources stay at rounding level (F) or 2–3 orders below the loss (J commutator) while the propagation term carries the growth.
