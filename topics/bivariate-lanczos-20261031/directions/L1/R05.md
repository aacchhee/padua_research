## 5. Corrections and prior art (received during R05; reviewed files not edited)

- **PC-9 (PD-T?…-108, -104; Barrio–Peña–Sauer 2010, record `literature/records/barrio-pena-sauer-2010.md`, LIT?…-301–303; I also read pp. 409–410 (§3), 413 and 418–419 of AC's PDF in the scratchpad).** BPS give, for monic multivariate OPs from moments, an explicit recurrence using one variable x_j per new polynomial (eq. (5)) and an averaged all-variables form (eq. (6), Thm 8); Remark 9 states that choosing j for each component may give better numerical behaviour; §5 proposes point-dependent heuristics for j; and the multivariate Clenshaw algorithm is solved as a block triangular system (18) with Higham-type bounds through the inverse of a comparison matrix ((19)–(20)). Hence the following are **prior art in the evaluation setting**: the one-relation-per-polynomial selection, the existence of alternative selections and of an all-relations form, the fact that the selection matters numerically, and "recurrence = triangular substitution, error governed by an inverse of the triangular matrix". PD-T?…-108 is to be cited with BPS as the closest predecessor (and Xu's non-unique left inverse, PC-7). **Separation** (no matching statement in the pages of BPS read): (i) setting — the window recurrence generates discrete-measure Lanczos vectors (orthonormal, node values) from the operators X, Y with window truncation, not an evaluation of known monic OPs; (ii) the link to loss of orthogonality (node-row representation PD-T?…-104, exact ω-recurrence PD-T?…-106); (iii) the measured and certified dichotomy: exponential amplification of the exact propagator on non-product supports before Ritz convergence (PD-T?…-107, -109, -113) versus univariate factorisation on product-type measures (PD-T?…-105, -111). BPS's Remark 9 is the closest statement to "the selection matters"; it gives no growth rate, no geometry dependence and no orthogonality-loss link in the pages read.
- **PC-10 (PD-T?…-105 part 1; PD-T?…-111 part 1).** That monic (and orthonormal) OPs of a product measure are products of univariate OPs and can be generated by univariate three-term recurrences is stated in BPS Remark 5 (p. 410, by Fubini); PD-T?…-105 part 1 is therefore routine/known. What is not in BPS (pages read) is the factorisation of the window recurrence's error propagator (part 2) and the moment-matching extension to checkerboards/Padua (PD-T?…-111).
