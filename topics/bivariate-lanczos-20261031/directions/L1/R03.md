## 2. Attempt

- Theory: **PD-T?bivariate-lanczos-claude-108** (`tracks/bivariate-lanczos/theory/window-recurrence-row-selection-of-jacobi-pencil.md`, PROVED): at any point z, row k of the exact propagator matrix L*(z) is the pencil relation ℛ^{A_k}_{p(k)}(z) (three-term relation of operator A_k applied to the parent); the polynomial values satisfy all 2N_{n−1} relations; so the window recurrence is forward substitution for a **triangular selection** (one relation per column) of a consistent overdetermined system, and its error propagator is the inverse of that selection. Other assignments and the two-relation block recurrence are other left inverses with the same exact solution.
- Experiment A (`code/L1/l1_exact_propagator.py`): exact window coefficients from a long-double, fully reorthogonalised basis; idealised propagator response P* versus the computed-coefficient response P and the measured loss; plus a 12-angle rotation scan of the triangle.
