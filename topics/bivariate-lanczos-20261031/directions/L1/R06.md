## 3. Attempt: first-order gain identity

**PD-T?bivariate-lanczos-claude-114** (`tracks/bivariate-lanczos/theory/first-order-coefficient-channel-gains-product-type.md`), derived after the test. At the product-type point c* of a measure that matches a product measure up to degree 2n, the derivative of the Gram matrix of Ξ(c) with respect to a window entry (k, l) is −(A + Aᵀ). A is given by univariate data of the factors only: an impulse response of one factor recurrence, multiplied by the orthonormal polynomial of the source column, against the factor measure. Entries with the source on the chain of k (on-pattern) give exactly the univariate coefficient-map derivatives. Off-pattern entries carry cross-variable factors (⟨r_b, r_{j_l}G^{y,[j]}⟩, linearisation coefficients ⟨r_b, r_{j_l}r_r⟩) that univariate Lanczos does not contain. Parts 1–4 PROVED (elementary); part 5 MEASURED:
- Check against direct long-double propagation, 91 entries per set: relative discrepancy ≤ 1.1e-14 (criterion 1e-8, written before the check run; candidates.md R06 note).
- Gains (max|A + Aᵀ|, degrees ≤ n), clustered: on-pattern ≤ 5.0e3; off-pattern up to 9.9e11 (n = 16) and 1.3e14 (n = 20), with 709/7031 entries above 1e6 at n = 20. Equispaced: on ≤ 5.6, off ≤ 1.4e3.
- First-order prediction from the BJ off-pattern values: 1.8e-11, 7.8e-9 (clustered, n = 16, 20) against L = 2e-11, 8e-9. Equispaced: 4.2e-15, 1.5e-14 against 4e-15, 1e-14. For the u-noise draws: 2.3–4.8e-4, 2.7–5.8e-2 against the nonlinear medians 4e-4, 3e-2.

Consequence (MEASURED, scope: two m = 30 checkerboards, degrees ≤ 20, BJ block CGS): the clustered-checkerboard loss is the first-order coefficient channel through the structural zeros of c*. Its amplification comes from gains of 10¹²–10¹⁴ that exist only off-pattern. The BJ run's off-pattern errors are 10⁶–10⁷ times less harmful than generic u-noise on the same entries. This answers the R05 question with a qualification: the loss is the conditioning of the coefficient map, but only in its off-pattern directions, and the actual errors are far from generic.

## 8. Next question

Why are the BJ off-pattern errors 10⁶–10⁷ times more benign than generic u-noise? There are three candidates: (i) they are the rounding errors of the dot products, which are tiny at the high-gain entries because the absolute products |ξ_l||aξ_p| are small there; (ii) they are mainly the exact inner products of the slightly non-orthogonal computed vectors (a feedback loop from earlier loss); (iii) their signs cancel. The pre-registered R07 test follows.
