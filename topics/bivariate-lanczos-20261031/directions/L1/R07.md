## 3. Attempt / results

- **PD-T?bivariate-lanczos-claude-115** (`tracks/bivariate-lanczos/theory/closed-loop-dot-product-rounding-carries-coefficient-channel.md`, MEASURED). On the clustered product-type checkerboards:
  - the loss is dot-product rounding, fed back through the recurrence, amplified by the off-pattern gains of PD-T?…-114 and damped by the feedback of the window inner products;
  - the BJ off-pattern values at the dangerous entries are feedback-dominated (|φ| ≈ 100·u·ŝ) and sign-structured;
  - φ and ρ each alone give nearly equal and larger defects, so they cancel each other;
  - open-loop models over-estimate by 1× (quadratic) to about 10² (quartic);
