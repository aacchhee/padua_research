## 3. Results and kill-test outcomes

- **K1 (consistency of PD-T?…-110)**: 0 violations over 28 nodes × 210 (s, t) pairs on 4 sets. Passed.
- **K2 (informativeness, classification)**: informative only for an exact product measure (tensor 41²: 0.4 decades); 10.4 decades for the random square against the product of its marginals (median max_t ε_t = 0.95), 13.6 and 27.8 for rotations by 0.05 and 0.3. The bound is true but does not explain the stability of empirical near-product sets.
- **K3 (H_lin)**: killed — excess rate/θ = 1.02 at θ = 0.025 vs 4.39 at θ = 0.2 (factor 4.3 > 3): superlinear onset; the toy model's linear prediction fails at small coupling (PD-N?bivariate-lanczos-claude-103 (a)).
- **C1 (checkerboard product type)**: passes for Chebyshev–Lobatto (8.9e-16) and equispaced (5.3e-15); **fires** for the cubic-clustered family (1.0e-6 in double). Diagnosis (post hoc): in long double the deviation is 9.4e-10, a factor ≈ 1.1·10³ smaller (ε-ratio 2048), i.e. a precision effect of computing the coefficients for weights spanning 1.7e-33…0.47; with the algebraic lemma (PD-T?…-111 part 2) the coefficients are product-type exactly. The criterion as written did not allow for this conditioning (mis-specified threshold; disclosed).
- **C2 (no bivariate amplification on product-type measures, measured against max of univariate Lanczos losses)**: passes for Chebyshev–Lobatto (ratio 0.015) and equispaced (0.5); **fires** for the clustered family (2.6·10⁶): BJ loss 3e-14, 1e-11, 4e-9, 9e-7 at t = 12, 16, 20, 24 while univariate Lanczos on the factors stays ≤ 5e-16 to step 20. Diagnosis (post hoc, PD-T?…-112): the growth scales with u, persists with CGS2 and ORTH (not local Gram–Schmidt), is not Ritz-type (u/ρ_Z ≤ 4e-14), and the exact split shows Φ̃ (propagated vector-operation errors) at 5e-16 while the loss sits in the Gram defect of Ξ̄ — a **coefficient channel**, distinct from the vector channel that carries the loss on rotated squares and random squares. So PD-T?…-105 (propagator bounded) is not contradicted, but "bounded propagator ⇒ small loss" is false here.
- **S1 (scaled forcing improves the predictor)**: killed — triangle still 25×/350× under; square 6×, rotated π/4 7.5× (criterion 5×).
- **S2 (clustered checkerboard: factorised vs bivariate amplification)**: neither branch — both the factorised and the run-coefficient responses stay at rounding level (they coincide), far below the loss: the vector channel is not the mechanism there.
- **IDEA?…-115 (coefficient-aware worst-case forcing)**: killed — over-predicts by up to 10⁷ (clustered) and 40× (square).
- **Two-relation control on the clustered checkerboard**: catastrophic (loss 0.4 at n = 20) — the R03 remedy is not unconditional (PD-N?…-103 (c)).
