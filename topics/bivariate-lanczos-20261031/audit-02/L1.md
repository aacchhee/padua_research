## Focused mathematical reconstruction

**T108.** Multiplication by x or y preserves the fixed monomial order. If ψ_l is monic, Aψ_l has leading monomial A·lm(ψ_l); all other monomials precede it. Expanding in the graded orthogonal basis therefore has a unique last nonzero coefficient, at that monomial, equal to 1 in monic coordinates and ‖ψ_k‖/‖ψ_l‖>0 in orthonormal coordinates. The coordinate-evaluation term in the pencil row lies at the earlier parent. This proves the stated iff for every z, including zero values of some lower coefficients. Every admissible selection gives a lower-triangular system with nonzero diagonal. Its N_n−1 homogeneous rows are independent; C(z)p(z)=0 with p₀=1 supplies the opposite rank bound. Adding normalization makes the selected system invertible, proving the normalized left-inverse formula. None of this identifies the particular basis returned by an arbitrary orthonormal block completion.

**T116.** With identical windows the coordinate terms cancel in ΔL and row zero vanishes. The bound on the exact forced response gives κ≤B. Factoring L̂=L(I+L⁻¹ΔL) proves the two stated Neumann estimates; dividing the inverse-difference bound by κ gives relative error ≤κδ/(1−κδ). Replacing κ by B in a denominator additionally requires Bδ<1. This is exactly the gap isolated above. The algebra is routine and may remain within the CONDITIONAL record; no computed δκ measurement has been supplied or executed.

**T113.** For scalar c≠0 and σ=sign(c), the transformed monic polynomial is c^t ψ((z′−d)/c), its norm scales by |c|^t, and its node vector by σ^t. With D=diag(σ^deg k), S=diag(1,|c|,…), the transformed matrix is L′=SDL D. Thus L′⁻¹=DL⁻¹DS⁻¹, giving the corrected signed formula for columns s≥1. The positive scale used for the existing certificates is unaffected. The Q40 reduction and exact orthogonality evidence did not change; the finite status update follows from audit-01's completed independent check rather than a new numerical agreement claim.

**T114/T117.** The formula theorem's hypotheses and chain-rule proof are preserved. Perturbing a single h-coordinate is different from perturbing a tied Jacobi b-parameter, which affects an h-coordinate and a β-coordinate. The revised wording respects this. The measured census and first-order agreement now have their own file; they supply no uniform second-order or causation theorem.

**T401.** If S_n occurs before any A application, its dimension is at most the number of starts; if it occurs later, the existing affine intersection argument gives at least n+1 starts.
