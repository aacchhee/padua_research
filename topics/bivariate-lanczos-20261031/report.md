## 0. What the campaign asked and what it found

The object is the truncated bivariate Lanczos/Stieltjes window recurrence (Weisfeld 1959; Bartels–Jezioranski 1985; Huhtanen–Larsen 2002) for X = diag(x), Y = diag(y), one start vector, total-degree graded orthonormal basis. Huhtanen–Larsen SIMAX 2002 p. 1088 *observed* (not conjectured — Step 0 correction) that loss of orthogonality "appears closely connected" to Ritz convergence of the complex section N_k.

1. **Loss of orthogonality is not Ritz-governed in general** (L1). On non-product supports (rotated squares, disk, triangle, L-shape, annulus, Gaussian cloud, exp(8xy)-weighted square) it grows (log-loss convex in n) from degree ≈ 8–12 before any Ritz convergence, scales with u, and depends on the *selection rule* (which multiplication generates each new vector) by 10³–10⁸; no rule is best everywhere. The bare geometry dependence is prior art (Zaccaron 2014); the triangular "one relation per polynomial" framing is prior art (Barrio–Peña–Sauer 2010).
2. **Where Paige-type theory does transfer** (L1, L4, L2): exact local relations, a growing-block Paige identity, an exact 2D ω-recurrence, an L4 obstruction (not a band Krylov sequence of one commuting operator with fixed starts), and a semi-orthogonality theorem for the x-part of the sections (with an explicit normalisation factor χ) — while for the y-part an exact commutator-residual identity gives a reference-free lower bound on span drift; whether *every* reorthogonalization scheme fails is OPEN (PD-T?…-210), the tested schemes fail (MEASURED).
3. **Greenbaum-type backward interpretation** (L3), in the exact-representation reading: δ = 0 for overdetermined nodes; ≥ √ε necessary for square (interpolatory) sets; √ε attained for small n; and, in reading (E) only (exact representation, or a sufficiently smaller residual, of specified consistent perturbations given as data), an **obstruction for every Padua degree n ≥ 3** (for every ε ∈ (0, ε₁] some such perturbation has no representing measure within a per-degree radius δ₀(n) > 0 of the nodes), by explicit nonnegative certificates with double zeros and positive-definite Hessians at all Padua nodes. This does not show that actual finite-precision Padua runs lack a Greenbaum-type backward model: that reading (G) is OPEN.
4. **Givens updating** (L5, side note): Givens grows faster than HL with M (MEASURED), the 'largest' redundant-pivot rule is unsafe, and the suspected prefix-unisolvence mechanism is refuted.
