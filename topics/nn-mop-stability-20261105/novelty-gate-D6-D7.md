### IDEA?nn-mop-stability-codex-D6-graph — ratio graph

Aliases: y=phi(x); Radon–Nikodym graph; positive mixed-test realization.

- Question/scope: N distinct real nodes and strictly positive w1,w2; can the NN system equal a positive bivariate total-degree OP system?
- Mechanism/hypotheses: graph nodes (x_l,w2_l/w1_l), masses w1_l; test P_(n,m)(x) against x^k (k<n) and y*x^k (k<m).
- Closest full entries: IDEA-117/376/377 and PD-T057; exact mixed moments are IDEA-376 background.
- Separation: obtain distinct spectral y values while retaining all MOP equations. This is only an ambient dictionary until NN arrays are explicitly identified with graded sections. Full Pi_(n+m-1) orthogonality would be required for the literal total-degree reading of P_(n,m).
- Cheapest test / kill (before execution): compute P_(1,1) for x=0,1,2,3, w1=(1,1,1,1), w2=(1,2,4,8); test its x moment in the graph measure. A nonzero result kills only the literal total-degree polynomial identification.
- Discriminability: exact zero mixed moments plus a nonzero missing moment distinguish mixed Petrov constraints from full total-degree orthogonality. It does not reject positive graph formulations with different spaces.
- Literature limits: repository gate only; no new historical novelty claim.
- Verdict: variant with explicit positive ambient construction; no separated stable algorithm.
- Result/disposition: recorded after testing in attempts/D6.md; positive ambient encoding retained, literal standard-grade identification scoped separately.
- Canonical theory: theory/dictionary-graph.md; broad transfer target in dictionary-target.md.

### IDEA?nn-mop-stability-codex-D6-copies — labelled copies

Aliases: two coincident copies; two sheets; y=0/1; selector lift.

- Scope/mechanism: measure sum_l w1_l delta_(x_l,0)+w2_l delta_(x_l,1); diagonal P(x), selectors 1-y and y encode the two original moment conditions.
- Closest: IDEA-376, IDEA-377, PD-T057; escaped hypothesis is that both NN directions must themselves be the coordinate pair.
- Separation / kill: a faithful positive scalar realization exists, but standard Pi_2 is singular since y(y-1)=0. The exact annihilator kills only a no-deflation full Pi_2 realization on these two sheets.
- Cheapest test: verify selector identities and the explicit annihilator; inspect whether diagonal functions are invariant under y multiplication.
- Discriminability: non-invariance forces extra sheet modes. It does not rule out quotient spaces, vector OPs, more sheets or another support.
- Verdict: variant/background positive lift; standard-grade transfer deferred unless a quotient/regrading is supplied.
- Canonical theory: dictionary-lifts.md. Actual results: attempts/D6.md.

### IDEA?nn-mop-stability-codex-D6-lattice — lattice boundary realization

- Scope/mechanism: choose boundary/gauge and global lattice operators whose consistent MOP eigenstates reproduce the original NN equations.
