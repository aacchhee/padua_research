# PD-T?nn-mop-stability-codex-D6-target — exact positive bivariate NN transfer

## Statement

Find an explicit finite positive scalar bivariate model, with specified
polynomial grading or quotient, coordinate multiplication operators, boundary
and gauge, whose recurrence data recover the entire finite normal nearest-
neighbour MOP coefficient triangle, and an explicit implication that would
transport a stable NN algorithm to the intended Padua/bivariate setting.
The NN data are those for N distinct common real nodes and two strictly
positive measures, normal at every required index through degree N.

## Status by part

Overall status: OPEN. No standard-grade dictionary or stability transfer
has been established. Positive ambient moment encodings are supporting
facts, not solved cases of this target's full recurrence/transport conclusion.

## Definitions and assumptions

A dictionary must specify both domain and codomain; node/weight maps; the
state space and grading; an invertible or expressly qualified redundant map;
two coordinate actions and positivity; all finite boundary conditions;
and the exact coefficient correspondence. PD-T057's standard total-degree
hypotheses apply only when its full positivity/echelon conditions hold.
A Petrov moment encoding alone does not meet this acceptance criterion.

  elementary identity in dictionary-graph.md. Literal degree-two embedding
  fails at a displayed four-node input (dictionary-obstructions.md).
- Two sheets: selectors exactly encode both measures; y multiplication
  leaves the scalar diagonal subspace, and y²-y gives a Pi_2 null vector
  ([separate C record](dictionary-copy-obstruction.md)).
  Positive quotient/vector interpretations remain available.
- Hermite–Padé pair: selector encoding is equivalent background at this
  stage; no new graded NN or stable bivariate algorithm follows.
- Lattice with boundary: on any consistent MOP state f_l(nu)=P_nu(x_l),
  the NN equations say H_j f_l=x_l f_l for both j. Subtracting shows
  (H1-H2)f_l=0. Thus the two prescribed actions coincide on the span of
  these eigenstates, regardless of linear dependence among them.
  An extension outside that span could add an independent coordinate, but
  requires new specified boundary/state information and a positive form.
  None is constructed here; candidate deferred, not rejected.
