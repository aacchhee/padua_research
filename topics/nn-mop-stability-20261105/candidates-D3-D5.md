## IDEA?nn-mop-stability-codex-31 — A, direct orthobasis mixed solves

- Aliases: whole triangle direct QR/LU/SVD; modified moments; native direct
  mixed orthogonality; no layer propagation.
- Question: can a stable basis reduce the measured excess loss of frozen C4?
- Mechanism: choose a common degree-ordered discrete orthonormal basis
  phi_0,...,phi_(N-1). At degree s solve, for each nu=(n,m),
  `[<phi_i,phi_l>_w1;i<n;<phi_i,phi_l>_w2;i<m] c_l = -[...phi_s]`.
  Normalize each equation by its row norm; use LU, column-pivoted QR or
  untruncated SVD. Put R_nu=phi_s+sum_(l<s)c_l phi_l. Use its multiplication
  matrix and both successor equations to extract b, then least squares in
  the one/two predecessor vectors to extract a. Average the two computed
  a estimates. Stop if a mixed solve or predecessor basis is singular;
  a near-singular matrix is reported, never truncated silently. Terminal
  degree-N polynomials use the common node polynomial, not a zero divisor.

  has fixed leading coefficient, and satisfies both sets of moments.
  PD-T286 then supplies both NN identities and compatibility.

  §4.4 moment formulation (repository-mediated coverage); known modified

## IDEA?nn-mop-stability-codex-32 — B, node-space constraint QR

- Aliases: all-lattice constraint reorthogonalization; row-space nullspace
  intersection; nodeqr.
- Question: does forming the complete mixed constraint subspace first
  reduce direct mixed-system error?
- Mechanism: at each nu build the s-by-N node constraint matrix with rows
  `(w_j(x_l) phi_i(x_l))_l`, i<nu_j; scale rows to unit norm. Householder
  QR of its transpose gives an orthonormal basis U of all constraints.
  Intersect the nullspace with span(phi_0,...,phi_s) by solving
  `(U^T V_<s)c=-U^T v_s`, with column-pivoted QR and the same leading
  gauge. Extract both NN relations as in A. No path selection is used.
  Exact breakdown is mixed normality failure or predecessor dependence;
  no positive-definiteness replacement is assumed.
- Invariant: the polynomial evaluation vector lies in the orthogonal
  complement of all mixed constraint rows while retaining degree/monicity.
- Closest predecessor and separation: A and standard QR/nullspace methods;
  algebraically equivalent to A, numerically a different factorization.
  This is not newly claimed biorthogonal Lanczos or a core updater.
- Kill: same N16 two-family G4 rule; novelty killed by exact equivalence
  unless a separately demonstrated numerical/complexity theorem survives.
- Actual result: executed on the same twelve inputs. N16 gains2.39/3.49
  digits. HahnN8 gains2.96digits, KrawN8 gains1.85. No kill. The cheap
