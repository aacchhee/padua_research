## Formal field, embedding and cardinals

The new source adapts the frozen independent N6 replay core, not an N8
producer. It constructs the formal quotient `QQ[theta]/f12`, checks exact
irreducibility and independently verifies

`Res_u(u^4-4u²+2,(t-u)^3-3(t-u)-1)=f12(t)`.

It recovers a from the exact linear gcd of the two defining polynomials over
the formal quotient field, sets b=theta-a, and checks both defining relations
and theta=a+b. It independently builds the12 tensor basis vectors, computes
both conversion matrices, verifies both inverse identities and determinant
`det(native_to_tensor)=990465713`, and compares the exact supplied matrix.
No supplied conversion matrix is used to generate the field elements.

The real embedding uses supplied160-bit dyadics inside the exact principal
brackets `(46/25,37/20)` and `(187/100,47/25)`. Exact polynomial signs and
monotonicity, together with the U7/U8 factorizations in60, identify the largest
Chebyshev roots. Rational-function expression conversion in formal theta
uses exact quotient reduction; no numerical root selector or RootOf index is
trusted. Tensor intervals have basis `a^i b^j`, i=0,…,3,j=0,…,2. Exact
nonzeroness plus a weak one-sided interval may decide a strict scalar sign;
strict interval separation remains required for whole-box exclusions.

The45 parity-selected nodes are generated by exact Chebyshev recurrence.
The checker independently inverts the45-by45 total-degree-eight monomial
Vandermonde matrix, verifies its inverse, all2025 exact cardinal values and
partition of unity. It additionally checks the six coefficient identities
reproducing1,x,y,x²,xy,y². The producer instead uses a corrected kernel; this
checker reconstruction needs no part1 kernel theorem dependency. Signed
polynomials use540-component exact integer coefficient vectors.

Trusted dependencies: CPython integers/Fraction, SymPy exact field/matrix/
polynomial arithmetic, and direct python-flint exact fmpz_poly/fmpq endpoint
evaluation. Actual versions are Python3.12.14, SymPy1.14.0,

## Node, tree, root and boundary checks

The node-cone verifier independently checks all45 nodes, exact gradients,
feasible unit-square side segments, every directional-zero breakpoint and
the scalar gap at all endpoints/breakpoints. Whole-B positivity, Hessian
quadratic bounds, remainder inequality, square-face condition and strict
whole-leaf containment all have to pass. A missing/no-cube/zero-gap record
grants no exclusion. The proof is local15.

Ordinary leaves contain every compatible mask in position-derived order.
All three direct leaf types have strict mutually exclusive key sets:
`{path,patterns}`, `{path,node_cone}` or `{path,all_gradient}`. Complete prefix
coverage is checked independently. Interior Krawczyk, strict signs/Hessian,
whole-hull injectivity and root disjointness are as in18; singular-Hessian
equalities never discard candidates.

For edges, the checker constructs the squarefree union independently by
incremental exact lcm of individual small squarefree restrictions. An lcm of
squarefree polynomials is squarefree; induction verifies the same zero union
as the product, without one expanded degree360 derivative gcd. A second lcm
of multiplicity-one factors verifies the regular-crease cover. The norm uses
an independent quartic resultant followed by a cubic resultant; a rational
monic polynomial uses the equivalent immediate squarefree rational norm.

Rational norm factorization identities and pairwise coprimeness are verified
before adding exact Sturm counts across factors. Positive primitive scaling
preserves Sturm signs while controlling denominator growth. Individual
interval counts, exact rational singletons, disjointness and the total
all-real count are checked; no isolation is rerun. Gap samples and all
derivative-norm dispositions, inward derivatives, corners and global weak
maximum exceptions retain the complete generic predicates of18.

## New theory53 universal-gradient leaf

For a claimed coordinate nu, the checker recomputes cardinal signs on the
whole leaf. Its exact background coefficient vector is the independent sum
`P_F=sum(forced sign_i*L_i)`, with zero coefficients on ambiguous indices.
This equals the producer formula `2sum_positive L_i+sum_ambiguous L_i-1`
because constants are reproduced.

It evaluates the background derivative naturally and computes
`r=sum_ambiguous maxabs(partial_nu L_i(Q))` from natural derivative intervals.
If the natural `G+[-r,r]` contains0, it intersects G with the exact centered
mean-value enclosure using the corresponding background Hessian entries.
The radius and cardinal classification remain unchanged. Only strict
separation from0 accepts the leaf. This single inequality covers every
compatible signed polynomial, with no missing-mask subset or early root
return. By53 it excludes non-strict as well as strict interior maxima;
edges/corners remain separate. The global mode therefore needs no extra
value bound for this direct gradient type.

Counters distinguish ordinary pattern records, node leaves and all-gradient
leaves. The verifier checks `summary.all_gradient_leaves`, the complete
`leaf_counts` dictionary and ordinary `leaf_pattern_records`. Its own
`all_gradient_summary` additionally records coordinate/method counts and
compatible-pattern occurrences covered universally.
