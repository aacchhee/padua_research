## Formal field, embedding and cardinals

The new source adapts the frozen independent N6 replay core, not an N8
producer. It constructs the formal quotient `QQ[theta]/f12`, checks exact
irreducibility and independently verifies

`Res_u(u^4-4u²+2,(t-u)^3-3(t-u)-1)=f12(t)`.

It recovers a from the exact linear gcd of the two defining polynomials over
the formal quotient field, sets b=theta-a, and checks both defining relations
and theta=a+b. It independently builds the12 tensor basis vectors, computes
both conversion matrices, verifies both inverse identities and determinant
`det(native_to_tensor)=990465713`, and compares the exact supplied matrix.
No supplied conversion matrix is used to generate the field elements.

The real embedding uses supplied160-bit dyadics inside the exact principal
brackets `(46/25,37/20)` and `(187/100,47/25)`. Exact polynomial signs and
monotonicity, together with the U7/U8 factorizations in60, identify the largest
Chebyshev roots. Rational-function expression conversion in formal theta
uses exact quotient reduction; no numerical root selector or RootOf index is
trusted. Tensor intervals have basis `a^i b^j`, i=0,…,3,j=0,…,2. Exact
nonzeroness plus a weak one-sided interval may decide a strict scalar sign;
strict interval separation remains required for whole-box exclusions.

an independent quartic resultant followed by a cubic resultant; a rational
monic polynomial uses the equivalent immediate squarefree rational norm.

Rational norm factorization identities and pairwise coprimeness are verified
before adding exact Sturm counts across factors. Positive primitive scaling

all-real count are checked; no isolation is rerun. Gap samples and all

`P_F=sum(forced sign_i*L_i)`, with zero coefficients on ambiguous indices.

It evaluates the background derivative naturally and computes
`r=sum_ambiguous maxabs(partial_nu L_i(Q))` from natural derivative intervals.
If the natural `G+[-r,r]` contains0, it intersects G with the exact centered
mean-value enclosure using the corresponding background Hessian entries.

return. By53 it excludes non-strict as well as strict interior maxima;
