## Extracted published statements

### LIT-726 — Lagrange polynomials and cubature on the Chebyshev–Lobatto parity node sets

- Statement in faithful paraphrase: for nodes h_n = cos(nπ/m), index sets Q_k = {(n,q): n − q ≡ k mod 2}, the two parity sets N^0, N^1 (the Xu-type node sets) carry explicit Lagrange polynomials P_{n,q} (Lemma 1.1, "obtained from [8]" = Xu 1996, LIT-314), which are not written in rotated or product form. Lemma 1.2 and Thm 2.1 (p. 4449) give the correction term in terms of V_i = T_{m−i}(s)T_i(t) − (−1)^k T_i(s)T_{m−i}(t). Cor. 2.3 (p. 4450): the cubature (2/m²)Σ c_n c_q p(h_n,h_q) is exact to degree 2m−1 for the product Chebyshev weight. Thm 3.1 (p. 4451): unisolvence on triangular subsets for (skew-)symmetric polynomials.
- Hypotheses and notation: product Chebyshev weight of the first kind; N^k as above.
- Page / theorem / equation: Lemmas 1.1–1.2, Thms 2.1, 3.1, Cors 2.2, 2.3, 3.2, pp. 4447–4451 (as digested).
- Reading evidence and limitations: the words "Lebesgue", "norm", "bound", "maximum", "estimate" and "log" were not found; there are no sums of |P_{n,q}| and no univariate Lebesgue constant.
- Relation to campaign claims: algebraic interpolation and cubature on the Xu node sets; supplies no analytic estimate and nothing implying Λ^Xu_n = L_n² or the argmax. No inherited proof status.
