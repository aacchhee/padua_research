### LIT-388 (complete-reading addendum)

- Statement in faithful paraphrase:
  - §2.1, pp. 2–6. For nodes on [−1,1]:
    - there is exactly one maximiser per node interval (citing Luttmann–Rivlin 1965);
    - the global maximiser set A is finite and disjoint from the nodes;
    - classical cases are listed without proofs: equispaced (two symmetric maximisers in the outer intervals); Chebyshev 1st kind A = {±1}, credited to "Powell, 1965"; Chebyshev–Lobatto A = {0} for odd n and two points near 0 for even n, no source given; Chebyshev 2nd kind A = {±1} with Λ_n = n+1; extended Chebyshev.
    - Proposition 1 and Theorem 1 (pp. 4–5): if the outer node lies within a(n)/n² of an endpoint, with a(n) = log[2 − (2/π log(n+1) + 0.5212)⁻¹], then that endpoint is not a global maximiser.
  - §3, pp. 7–9: Padua and Morrow–Patterson definitions; numerical local-maxima counts (Tables 3–4).
  - §4, pp. 9–10: open problems, including sufficient conditions for an endpoint maximum in 1-D and the number of local maxima on the square.
