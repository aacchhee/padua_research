### LIT-727 — Lebesgue bound of order ln² for the degenerate Lissajous nodes LD_{n,p}

- Statement in faithful paraphrase: Thm 11: for the nodes LD_{n,p} (p ≥ 1, gcd(n, n+p) = 1) there are constants D, C, unspecified, with D ln² n ≤ Λ_{n,p} ≤ C ln²(n+p).
