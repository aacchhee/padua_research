### LIT?padua-literature-claude-86 — Lebesgue constants of Fourier partial sums over an anisotropically dilated simplex

- Statement in faithful paraphrase:
  - Eq. (1.3), p. 2914. For n = (n₁, n₂) with n₂ ≥ n₁ > 3, the Lebesgue constant of Fourier partial sums over the dilated simplex is (4/π³) log n₁ log n₂ + (2/π³) log² n₁ + 𝔉_n + O(log log n₁ · log n₂). 𝔉_n is an explicit oscillatory term: O(log n₁) when n₂/n₁ is rational, and O(log² n₁) otherwise.
  - The paper also proves the d-dimensional version (Theorem 2.1).
  - History, p. 2912: Daugavet's first nontrivial result; Kuznetsova's rhombic formula (for integer ratio of the sides) "for a long time continued to be the best possible asymptotic relation", with KL 2021 as recent progress; Skopina's simplex result.
- Hypotheses and notation: Fourier partial sums on Tᵈ over a simplex; norms (Lebesgue constants), not Lebesgue functions; no interpolation, no Padua points.
- Page / theorem / equation: (1.3) p. 2914; history p. 2912.
