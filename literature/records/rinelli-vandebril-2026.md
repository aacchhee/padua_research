### LIT-509 (LIT?padua-literature-claude-83) — correspondence and stated future work

- Statement in faithful paraphrase: Under a no-deflation assumption, polynomial block Krylov subspaces are shown to be isometrically isomorphic to spaces of matrix polynomials of bounded degree. The same holds for rational block Krylov spaces with matrix-valued rational functions, and, in the extended case, Laurent matrix polynomials. For normal A the induced inner product is a discrete spectral matrix measure. In the unitary case, the matrix Szegő recurrence and block CMV matrices give short-recurrence orthogonalization procedures (a block isometric Arnoldi) (pp. 1–2). Stated future work (p. 26): (i) removing the no-deflation assumption, which needs a theory of degenerate inner products for matrix polynomials; (ii) using the correspondence for alternative block Gaussian quadrature, especially for unitary matrices.
- Page / theorem / equation: abstract p. 1; contributions and outline p. 2; §7 p. 26.
