- Statement in faithful paraphrase: For every epsilon > 0 there is delta > 0 independent of the dimension n such that any self-adjoint contractions A, B in M_n(C) with ‖[A,B]‖ < delta admit commuting self-adjoint matrices A', B' in M_n(C) with ‖A - A'‖ and ‖B - B'‖ below epsilon (Herrera's formulation). ### LIT-223 (LIT?bivariate-lanczos-claude-219) — Quantitative history of Lin's theorem (as reported by Herrera and Kachkovskiy-Safarov)

- Verification: **Secondary digests only; UNVERIFIED against the cited papers.**
- Statement in faithful paraphrase: Pearcy and Shields (1979): delta = 2 epsilon^2/(n-1), dimension dependent, only one operator needs to be self-adjoint. Szarek (1990): delta = c epsilon^{13/2}/sqrt(n), constructive, dimension dependent. Davidson (1985): an example shows that the dimension dependence of Pearcy-Shields cannot be removed in general (for non-self-adjoint). Hastings (2008/2010): delta-dependence of the form epsilon(delta) = E(1/delta) delta^beta with beta > 0 and E growing slower than any polynomial (Kachkovskiy-Safarov quote a rate C_eps ‖[A,A*]‖^{1/5 - eps} for normal approximation; Herrera does not give beta). Kachkovskiy-Safarov (2015/2016): the optimal square-root dependence epsilon <= Const delta^{1/2}, not constructive.
