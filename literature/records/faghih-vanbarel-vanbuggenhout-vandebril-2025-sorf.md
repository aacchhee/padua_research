## Extracted published statements

### LIT-505 (LIT?padua-literature-claude-78) — method summary and absence of stated future work

- Statement in faithful paraphrase: Recurrence coefficients of rational functions with prescribed poles, orthonormal for a continuous Sobolev inner product, are obtained by discretizing with a rational Gauss quadrature and solving a Hessenberg-pencil IEP. The IEP comes from the link between Sobolev ORFs and rational Krylov bases for a Jordan-like matrix. Two solvers are given: an updating procedure based mostly on unitary similarity transformations of the pencil (adding nodes one by one), and an alternative route that first builds a Sobolev OP Hessenberg matrix and then converts it to a pencil. Both are compared numerically with the Krylov method of the companion least-squares paper. The conclusion states **no future work**.
- Page / theorem / equation: abstract p. 1; sections 3 (pp. 6–13), 3.5 (p. 13), 4.2 (p. 15); conclusion §5 pp. 16–17.
