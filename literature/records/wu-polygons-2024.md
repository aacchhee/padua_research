### LIT?padua-literature-claude-24 — MZ for scattered data on triangles/polygons via Bernstein–Bézier interpolatory rules

- Statement in faithful paraphrase: on a triangle, an interpolatory rule at Bernstein–Bézier domain points of degree d is exact on P_d (Theorem 3.1, p. 4), has positive weights for d = 1, 3, 5, and then satisfies two-sided L^p MZ inequalities on P_N for N ≤ d (Theorem 3.3, p. 5); for N > d only perturbed (non-MZ) estimates hold (Theorem 3.4, Remark 3.1, p. 8). On a polygon triangulated from scattered points (vertices included), the vertex rule with weights one third of adjacent triangle areas is exact on P_1 (Theorem 3.5, p. 10), and MZ-type inequalities with a constant η hold for P_N under a mesh/shape condition (Theorem 3.6, pp. 10–11).
