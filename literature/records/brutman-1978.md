### LIT-466 (LIT?padua-literature-claude-07) — local maxima of 1-D Lebesgue functions at Chebyshev-type nodes; attribution of the endpoint maximum

- Statement in faithful paraphrase:
  - p. 695: Luttmann–Rivlin conjectured that for Chebyshev zeros T the Lebesgue constant equals the value of the Lebesgue function at x = 1. Ehlich–Zeller proved it, "see also Powell" (Comput. J. 9 (1967) 404–407).
  - Theorem 1 (p. 697): for T, the local maxima on the node intervals are strictly decreasing from the endpoints toward the middle.
  - Lemma 1 (p. 698): each local piece is concave between the interval midpoint and its maximiser.
  - Theorem 2 (p. 699): asymptotics of the smallest local maximum.
  - §3: any two local maxima differ by at most 1/2, and by less than 0.201 for extended Chebyshev nodes.
  - §4 (pp. 702–703): for Chebyshev extrema U (cos(kπ/n)) the local maxima strictly INCREASE toward the middle ((44)); the largest maximum is taken from Ehlich–Zeller and McCabe–Phillips ((45)). For interior second-kind nodes (endpoints deleted), Λ_n = n+1 ((47)).
- Hypotheses and notation: one variable; the node families T, extended T, U, U without endpoints, and equispaced E.
