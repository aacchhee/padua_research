### LIT?padua-literature-claude-08 — asymptotic expansions of 1-D Lebesgue constants and deviation between local maxima

- Statement in faithful paraphrase:
  - 1980, abstract and §2, about Lebesgue constants of the form (2/π) log n + c + r_n:
    - covers Chebyshev zeros T and expanded nodes, with bounds on r_n (Thm 2 for the expanded nodes); similar results hold for the extrema U and for equispaced trigonometric interpolation;
    - the deviation between local maxima is below (2/π) log 2 ≈ 0.441 for T (Thm 3), improving Brutman's 0.4788, and asymptotically at most about 0.016 for the expanded nodes;
    - restates (from Brutman 1978) that each local maximum on an interval lies at or to the right of its midpoint, and that the maxima decrease toward the middle.
  - 1988: an asymptotic expansion exists for the Lebesgue constants at extended Chebyshev nodes, and it differs from the zeros case. Its maximum on the relevant interval is attained at a single point φ*(n).
- Page / theorem / equation: 1980 pp. 512–513 (abstract, Thm 2, Thm 3, Lemma 2); 1988 pp. 461–463.
