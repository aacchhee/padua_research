### LIT-485 (LIT?padua-literature-claude-05) — conjectured corner location of the Padua Lebesgue maximum

- Statement in faithful paraphrase: Section "The Lebesgue constant" gives two results.
  - Theorem (credited to the authors' ref. [1]): the Padua Lebesgue constant is O(log² n).
  - Boxed Conjecture: the maximum of the Lebesgue function is about (4/π²) log² n, and it is attained at one of the vertices of the square.
- Hypotheses and notation: Padua points Pad_n on [−1,1]²; the Lebesgue function is Σ_ξ |L_ξ(x)|.
- Page / theorem / equation: single page, column "The Lebesgue constant".
- Reading evidence and limitations: the text layer was read in full. The poster is not peer reviewed, and the source gives no degree range or evidence.
- Relation to campaign claims (no inherited proof status): this is the earliest located statement of the corner-argmax conjecture. PD-T209/T214 (degrees 2 and 9–16), the finite certifications for n = 3–8 and PD-T229 (all n ≥ N_*) prove it in those ranges and sharpen it to exactly the two corners that are not nodes.
