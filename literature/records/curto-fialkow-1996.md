### LIT?bivariate-lanczos-claude-225 — Flat extension theorem for the truncated moment problem

- Verification: **The Memoir itself NOT read. Bibliographic data from the University of Iowa repository entry; theorem statement from Fialkow (J. Operator Theory 42, 1999) and Curto-Fialkow (hyperbolic moment problem, arXiv math/0507069) which both attribute it to Theorem 5.13 of the Memoir. `LITERATURE` attribution only.**
- Statement in faithful paraphrase: Citation: R. E. Curto and L. A. Fialkow, Solution of the truncated complex moment problem for flat data, Mem. Amer. Math. Soc. 119 (1996), no. 568, pp. 1-50 (as listed), doi:10.1090/memo/0568; section outline: moment matrices, positive moment matrices and representing measures, existence of representing measures, extension of flat positive moment matrices, applications, several variables. Flat extension theorem as quoted by Fialkow (1999, Theorem 1.1, citing [CF, Theorem 5.13]): for a truncated moment sequence gamma^(2n) with moment matrix M(n), there is a rank M(n)-atomic representing measure for gamma if and only if M(n) is positive semidefinite and admits a flat extension M(n+1), i.e. an extension to a (necessarily positive) moment matrix M(n+1) with rank M(n+1) = rank M(n). Related statement cited by Fialkow (Theorem 1.4, from Curto-Fialkow, Oper. Theory Adv. Appl. 104 (1998), Theorem 3.1): if M(n) is positive and recursively generated with Z^k = p(Z, Z-bar) for some low-degree p and 1 <= k <= [n/2] + 1, then M(n) has a unique flat extension M(n+1). The Memoir is about complex moments over the plane (gamma_ij = integral of z-bar^i z^j); the real two-variable form is the one used in the hyperbolic-moment paper, eq. (1.8).
- Hypotheses and notation: Truncated moment data of degree 2n in two variables; positive semidefinite moment matrix M(n).
- Page / theorem / equation: Memoir Theorem 5.13 (as cited by two later papers); Fialkow 1999 Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 1.4.
