### LIT-731 — one-dimensional Lebesgue constant of the Chebyshev zeros and its expansion

- Statement in faithful paraphrase: §2.2, p. 112: for the n Chebyshev zeros the Lebesgue constant is Λ_n(T) = λ_n(T, ±1) = (1/n) Σ_{k=1}^n cot((2k−1)π/(4n)), the maximum at ±1 being credited to Ehlich–Zeller (LIT-470). Eq. (2.2), p. 112: Λ_n(T) = (2/π)(log n + γ + log(8/π)) + O(1/n²), with n nodes; the expansion is said to be deducible from the cotangent form.
- Hypotheses and notation: n-node convention.
- Page / theorem / equation: §2.2, p. 112, eq. (2.2).
