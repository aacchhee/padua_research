Cube cubature constructions and their lower bounds are not imposed on interpolation sets.

## Evidence policy

Random-axis exact rank samples remain MEASURED for generic claims unless a written symbolic or probabilistic reduction proves more. A finite field witness proves only its recorded specialization unless the lifting argument is explicitly supplied and independently checked. Floating Lebesgue estimates are MEASURED sample maxima, never rigorous supremum or asymptotic bounds. Hand proofs and computer proofs are distinguished; the latter need finite reduction, exact code/evidence and independent rerun. All source facts have named reading coverage.
