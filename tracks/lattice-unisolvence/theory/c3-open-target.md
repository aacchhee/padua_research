## Statement

Find and prove a family, for infinitely many unbounded degrees n, of finite-index periodic/character-defined subsets I_n of three-axis product grids, with exactly binomial(n+3,3) points, generically unisolvent for Pi_n, and with either an explicit axis-factorized determinant or comparably useful structural criterion, or a Chebyshev-type specialization with a proved favorable Lebesgue growth bound. State every field, axis and asymptotic hypothesis. Minimal or near-minimal cubature is not required and is outside this construction scope.

## Status by part

Overall status: OPEN. Finite generic rank witnesses, a direct isolated n2 factorization and elementary certificate lemmas do not turn this unbounded-degree target into PARTIAL. No family meeting its structural/performance requirements has been accepted here.

## Actual development and limitations

Five frozen rounds screened the bounded C0 catalogue, proved explicit low-degree facts and tested meaningful finite numerical comparisons. The k6,a111 consecutive-axis count family survives generic ranks in represented degrees, but each n3..6 determinant has a mixed-axis rectangle obstruction and its finite Chebyshev observations are poor. This refutes only axis-only products in those degrees, not an analogous useful mixed structural criterion or every future specialization.

The isolated k6,a312,n2 fibre is axis-factorable and Chebyshev-unisolvent. Its naive fixed-character continuation already has deficient finite ranks: n3 residues0,1 rank19/20; n4 residues0,1,2 rank33/35; n6 residues0,1,2 rank79/84 on consecutive lengths. There is no n5 cardinality-admissible consecutive row for that character in C0. These existing observations remove the immediate induction opening; they are not an all-degree nonexistence theorem.

Nine always-deficient n2/3 representatives lack a certificate from covers, projected counts or exhaustive subbox dimensions. Their generic singularity is unresolved here. This residual classification problem may be resumed with a concrete new annihilator mechanism, but does not supply a surviving construction opening.

## Next unresolved step / sources

A new cardinality-valid family with a separated structural mechanism, or a rigorous mixed-axis criterion for an existing infinite cardinality family. Higher moduli, coset unions, boundary corrections and nonconsecutive grids remain untested. Compare Dencker–Erb's spectral space and known extraction families before any novelty or acceptance claim; full source coverage remains in the literature baseline.
