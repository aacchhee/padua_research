## Meaningful results (accepted at stated scope)

**Conventions:** even-checkerboard Padua nodes (x denominator m, y denominator m+1); signed interpolatory weights for dx dy (mass 4); Gram matrix on total degree n=m−1 in the Legendre-orthonormal basis.

- **[PD-T106](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-aliasing.md): exact all-degree aliases.** With the triangular fold A=f_m(a), B=f_(m+1)(b), Q_m(T_aT_b) = h_A h_B if A+B ≤ m, and h_(m−A) h_(m+1−B) otherwise. The degree of exactness is exactly m, because T_(m+1)(y) aliases to T_m(x). The Gram matrix comes from a rational Chebyshev pencil.
- **[PD-T109](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-defect-structure.md): finite defect structure.**
  - The defect is a sum of at most 160 masked tensor Hankel/one-sided Toeplitz terms (90 nonempty sectors).
  - Each mask is a triangular truncation with Fourier norm ≤ 1+log(8n+1) in Chebyshev coefficients.
  - This passes the D1 structural kill criterion, but it is not a sharp spectral bound.
- **[PD-T107](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-certified-spectra.md): computer-assisted exact brackets** of width 1e-6:

  | m | λ_min | λ_max |
  |---|---|---|
  | 10 | (0.356191, 0.356192) | (1.634856, 1.634857) |
  | 20 | (0.272233, 0.272234) | (1.723313, 1.723314) |
  | 30 | (0.236705, 0.236706) | (1.757929, 1.757930) |
  | 40 | (0.214817, 0.214818) | (1.778088, 1.778089) |

  The brackets were checked four times:
  - The developer certified all eight by exact signed-dominance congruence.
  - A different Codex agent replayed that checker.
  - Claude reran it again.
  - Claude also reproduced the brackets with its own code. At m=10/20/30 it used a different method, Sylvester leading minors from exact FLINT fraction-free LU. That run stopped before reaching a verdict at m=40. [Addendum A1](../topics/padua-mz-2d-20261101/audit-01.md#addendum-a1--r3-at-m--40-2026-11-01-same-auditor) certifies m=40 independently instead: a Cholesky-proposed integer congruence followed by an exact positive strict row-dominance check, plus exact Rayleigh quotients. A1's caveat: this shares the developer's general congruence-and-dominance principle, although the transform, the acceptance test and the code differ.
- **[PD-N044](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-eta-counterexample.md): η > 3/4 at m=30.** An explicit 225-term integer polynomial at m=30 has Q(p²)/∫p² < 1/4, so η(30,29) > 3/4. This refutes a uniform bound η ≤ 3/4.
- **[PD-T111](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-lower-corner-weights.md): exact corner weights.**
  - Formula: w_c = (A_r − 2h_m)/(2m(m+1)), with r=⌊m/2⌋, N=2r+1, A_r = 8/(N(N+1)) − 16S_r/(N²−1) and S_r = Σ_{i=1}^{r} 1/(2i−1).
  - The weights are 0 at m=2, negative for every m ≥ 3, and asymptotically −(4 log m + O(1))/m⁴.
  - Measured only, for m=3..40: these two corners are the only negative weights.
- **[PD-T115](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-fixed-ratio-diamond-reconstruction.md): diamond reconstruction.** A diamond (diagonal-angle) Fejér filter reconstructs every p ∈ Π_n exactly from the checkerboard samples, for every 0 ≤ n < m.
- **[PD-T112](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-hyperinterpolation-absolute.md) and [PD-T113](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-hyperinterpolation-reproduction.md): signed hyperinterpolation.**
  - PD-T112, norm: for the direct signed coefficient operator H_(m,n), the exact norm ‖H_(m,n)‖_(C→L²(dx dy)) is the supremum of Σ_z |w_z||p(z)| over p ∈ Π_n with ‖p‖_(L²(dx dy)) = 1. This is at most √(τ_m β_abs(m,n)), where τ_m = Σ|w_z| is the total variation of the weights and β_abs is the largest eigenvalue of the absolute-weight Legendre Gram matrix. Definitions are in PD-T112.
  - PD-T113, reproduction: the operator reproduces Π_r whenever both 0 ≤ r ≤ n and n+r ≤ m hold. At the endpoint n=m−1 this gives r=1 for m ≥ 2, and r=0 for m=1, n=0.
- **[PD-T117](../tracks/mz-constants/theory/mz2d-tensor-control.md): tensor-CC control.** The tensor-CC rule on the same total-degree space has extreme eigenvalues equal to the extremes of the two coordinate CC rules. At the endpoint, λ_min ≍ (1+log m)⁻² and η → 1.
